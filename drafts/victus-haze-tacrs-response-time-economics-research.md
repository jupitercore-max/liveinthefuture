## Original Analysis Angles

### 1. Cost of Orbital Denial (Key Calculation)
Pre-TacRS: Replacing a destroyed satellite takes 18-24 months (~13,000+ hours)
ASAT weapon cost: ~$10-50M (Chinese SC-19 test, Russian Nudol)
Cost per hour of denial = ASAT cost / replacement hours

Pre-TacRS: $50M / 13,000h = $3,846/hour of denial — CHEAP for adversary
Post-Victus Haze: $50M / 16.7h = $2.99M/hour of denial — EXPENSIVE for adversary

That's a ~780x increase in cost-per-hour-of-denial for the attacker.
This is the deterrence shift: ASAT attacks become economically irrational against reconstitutable constellations.

### 2. Vertical Integration Speed Premium
Victus Nox (multi-contractor): 27 hours
- Millennium had to ship spacecraft 165 miles, integrate at launch site
Victus Haze (single prime): 16h42m
- Rocket Lab built spacecraft AND rocket, same integration
Speed improvement: 38% faster
Key insight: eliminating the contractor handoff saved ~10 hours

### 3. The Payload Capacity Gap
Electron: ~300 kg to LEO
Critical defense satellites:
- GPS III: 3,880 kg
- SBIRS GEO: 4,500 kg
- WGS: 5,987 kg
Responsive launch works only for small sats right now.
Neutron (Rocket Lab's next rocket): 8,000 kg to LEO — but won't fly until 2027+
The TacRS program is building the PROCESS before it has the PAYLOAD capacity.

### 4. China Comparison
Kuaizhou = "Speedy Vessel", derived from DF-21 ballistic missile
- 200-300 kg to LEO, road-mobile, solid fuel
- T-240 min for vehicle erection
- China launched 2 KZ-1A rockets in 6 hours, but PRE-PLANNED, not responsive to unknown orbit
- Key difference: US demonstrated launch to PREVIOUSLY UNKNOWN orbit on <24h notice
