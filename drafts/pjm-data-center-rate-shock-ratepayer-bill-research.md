# Research: PJM Data Center Rate Shock — 65 Million Ratepayers Subsidizing AI

## Story Summary
PJM Interconnection, the largest grid operator in the US (65 million people, 13 states + DC), is in crisis. Capacity auction prices surged from $28.92/MW-day to $329.17/MW-day in three years — an 11x increase. Data centers caused 63% of it. The 2027-2028 auction was the first in PJM's 27-year history to fail to meet reliability requirements (6,625 MW shortfall). Now PJM is running an unprecedented emergency 15GW auction backed by Trump and 13 bipartisan governors. Meanwhile, the same grid operator told Constellation Energy that Three Mile Island's 835 MW nuclear restart can't connect until 2031. States are responding with rate reforms, tax rollbacks, and moratoriums — 238+ data center bills in 50 states.

## Primary Sources (3+)

### Source 1: PJM Interconnection BRA (Base Residual Auction) Reports
- Capacity clearing prices:
  - 2024-2025: $28.92/MW-day (trough)
  - 2025-2026: $269.92/MW-day (+833%)
  - 2026-2027: $329.17/MW-day (hit FERC price cap)
  - 2027-2028: $333.44/MW-day (hit cap again, FIRST EVER shortfall: 6,625 MW)
- 134,479 MW procured vs 141,101 MW reliability requirement
- Installed reserve margin: 14.8% vs 20% target
- Summer peak load projected to grow 3.6%/year to 222GW by 2036

### Source 2: NRDC / Synapse Energy Economics Analysis
- Data centers caused 63% of capacity price increase in 2025-2026 auction
- $9.3 billion in data center-driven capacity costs (single year)
- Cumulative cost projections through 2033: $100B (Synapse, conservative) to $163B (NRDC, if price cap expires)
- Annual capacity bill: $27-30B/year during 2028-2033 window
- Historical norm: $8-10B/year before data center surge
- Source: https://www.nrdc.org/bio/claire-lang-ree/real-reason-your-energy-bills-are-increasing-pjm

- New data center demand added annually: 5-7 GW

  - Phase 2: Central backstop procurement if Phase 1 falls short
- $15 billion+ in new baseload generation
- Vote expected May 2026, FERC submission June 2026
- Online by June 2031

### Source 6: State Legislative Response
- 238+ data center bills in all 50 states (2025), 40+ enacted in 21 states
- Oregon POWER Act (HB 3546): First dedicated data center rate class, 10-year minimum contracts, separate cost allocation
- Virginia SB 253: Shift distribution + capacity costs from residential to data centers >25MW; SCC estimated 3.4% residential rate reduction (~$5.52/month) and 15.8% data center rate increase; Dominion Energy supports
- Bipartisan governor coalition (13 governors) principles: data centers must pay for capacity they drive
- PJM Independent Market Monitor: existing price cap prevented $13B in additional costs
- Source: Introl Blog analysis (https://introl.com/blog/pjm-rate-shock-100-billion-data-center-electricity-2026)
- Source: https://radiofreehubcity.com/2026/04/11/governors-across-pjm-region-demand-data-centers-pay-fair-share-for-rising-energy-costs/

### Source 8: 60GW Potential Shortfall
- PJM could face up to 60GW shortfall within a decade
- Equivalent to output of 60 large nuclear reactors
- Driven by data center growth concentrated in Virginia and Pennsylvania

## Strongest Counterargument
Data centers bring massive economic benefits — jobs, tax revenue, and the AI services that 65 million people in PJM territory themselves use daily. Microsoft's deal with TMI would create hundreds of jobs and $1.6B in investment. The emergency auction itself could catalyze $15B+ in new generation that strengthens the grid for everyone. If data centers are forced to fully internalize costs, they may relocate to states with weaker ratepayer protections or offshore entirely, leaving PJM states with neither the tech jobs nor the grid improvements.

## Limitations
- The $70/month household impact figure is a projection dependent on price cap policy decisions not yet made
- NRDC and Synapse estimates differ by $63B — the range is wide
- Data center share of demand growth (94%) is PJM's own projection; actual buildout depends on market conditions
- We don't have granular data on which specific companies' data centers drive the most demand
- The emergency auction is a proposal not yet approved by FERC
