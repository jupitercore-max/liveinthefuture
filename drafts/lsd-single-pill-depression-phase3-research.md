## Primary Sources

### DT120 Emerge Trial Data
- **Drug:** DT120 ODT 100µg (lysergide/LSD), orally disintegrating tablet
- **N=149**, randomized, double-blind, placebo-controlled
- **Population:** Ages 18-74, DSM-5 MDD diagnosis, MADRS ≥26 at baseline (moderate-to-severe)
- **Sites:** 20 sites
- **Single oral dose** — patient discharged same day

### Efficacy Results
- **Primary endpoint:** MADRS change at Week 6: -8.1 points placebo-adjusted (p<0.0001)
- **Week 1:** -14.2 points placebo-adjusted (p<0.0001) — extremely rapid onset
- **Week 12:** -7.3 points placebo-adjusted (p<0.0001) — durable
- Remission rates, response rates TBD from full data

### Safety
- 99% adverse events mild-to-moderate
- No serious adverse events (SAEs)
- No suicidality signal (C-SSRS)
- Discharge time: average 5.8 hours, median 5.1 hours, 100% by hour 8

## Comparison Benchmarks

### SSRIs (standard of care)
- MADRS drug-placebo difference: ~2-3 points per meta-analysis
- Hengartner & Plöderl 2020 (BMC Psychiatry): 2.1 points
- Jakobsen et al. (BMC Psychiatry 2017): 1.94 HDRS points across 131 RCTs
- Dosing: daily, indefinitely
- Onset: 4-6 weeks

### Esketamine (Spravato, J&J)
- TRANSFORM-2 trial: -4.0 MADRS vs placebo (N=223)
- Our psilocybin article cited -3.6 to -4.2
- Dosing: twice weekly → biweekly, ongoing maintenance
- Requires 2+ hours REMS monitoring per session
- Population: TRD (treatment-resistant depression)
- Annual cost: $6,000-10,000

### COMP360 Psilocybin (Compass Pathways)
- COMP005: -3.6 MADRS (p<0.001)
- COMP006: -3.8 MADRS (p<0.001)
- Population: TRD
- 1-2 doses total, 8 hours supervised per session
- Existing LITF article covers this extensively

### DT120 (LSD) — this article
- -8.1 MADRS at Week 6
- Single dose, 5.8 hours monitoring
- Population: MDD (NOT TRD — important caveat)

## Original Analysis: The 8-Point Threshold

### Key Novel Insight
The clinical significance threshold for MADRS improvement:
- Meta-analyses identify ~7-8 points on MADRS as "minimal improvement" detectable by clinicians (CGI-I rating)
- Leucht et al. (2013, Lancet Psychiatry) established benchmarks
- DT120 at 8.1 points is the FIRST antidepressant of any class to cross this clinician-detection threshold in a Phase 3 placebo-controlled trial
- SSRIs at 2-3 points: clinicians literally cannot distinguish treated from untreated patients by clinical observation alone
- This is a genuinely novel calculation — nobody has framed it this way

### Efficacy Gap Table (original)
| Treatment | MADRS Δ vs placebo | Doses needed | Onset | Monitoring | Population |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| SSRIs | ~2-3 pts | Daily, indefinite | 4-6 weeks | None | MDD |
| Esketamine | ~4 pts | Repeated + oral AD | 24 hours | 2+ hrs/session | TRD |
| COMP360 psilocybin | 3.6-3.8 pts | 1-2 doses | 1-3 weeks | ~8 hrs | TRD |
| DT120 (LSD) | 8.1 pts | Single tablet | 1 week | ~6 hrs | MDD |

**Critical caveat:** TRD vs MDD populations are NOT directly comparable. TRD patients have failed 2+ treatments and are harder to treat. DT120's larger effect size partly reflects an easier-to-treat population.

## Limitations to Acknowledge
1. N=149 is small for a Phase 3 trial (typical: 300-500+)
2. MDD vs TRD population difference makes cross-trial comparison imprecise
3. Single study, no independent replication
4. 20 sites — geographic diversity unclear
5. LSD is Schedule I — DEA manufacturing/distribution hurdles beyond FDA approval
6. Long-term durability data limited (12 weeks)
7. 6-hour monitoring requirement has healthcare system cost implications
8. Placebo response rates in depression trials have been rising for decades
9. Second Phase 3 trial still enrolling — could fail

## Strongest Counterargument
DT120 tested in MDD (any severity above MADRS 26), not TRD. SSRIs' 2-3 point effect is measured against broad MDD populations too, but patients who reach a psychedelic trial are self-selected (willing to try LSD, willing to spend 6 hours in a clinic). Placebo response in psychedelic trials is complicated by expectation effects and unblinding (patients know if they got the psychedelic). The 8.1-point advantage may partly reflect an enthusiastic placebo arm performing poorly rather than the drug performing exceptionally. Furthermore, N=149 is small enough that a single replication failure would erase the finding.

## Actionable Insights
- Clinicians: watch for DT120 NDA filing (~2027-2028), consider REMS implications
- Patients with MDD: trial data does NOT mean you should seek LSD — illegal, uncontrolled doses carry real risks
- Investors: DFTX moved 52%, peak sales $620M-$2B, but one trial + Schedule I = high regulatory risk
- Policymakers: Trump executive order + 3 FDA Priority Vouchers signal regulatory momentum; DEA rescheduling remains the actual bottleneck
- Healthcare systems: 6-hour monitoring per patient per dose — plan capacity
