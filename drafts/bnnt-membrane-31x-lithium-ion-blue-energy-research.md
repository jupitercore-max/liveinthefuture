## Key Findings

1. **31× anomalous Li+ transport:** Lithium ions moved through BNNT membranes at 31 times the rate predicted by standard diffusion theory
2. **Selectivity:** Li+ transported significantly faster than competing ions (Na+, K+, etc.) — selective highway for lithium
3. **Power density:** Per-pore power density up to **15,300 W/m²**
4. **Energy conversion efficiency:** Approaching theoretical limit of **~50%** at pH 5.5
5. **Membrane construction:** Millions of aligned BNNTs with charged surfaces, aligned under 40 Gauss magnetic field
6. **Proof of concept:** Membranes powered a watch, calculator, and LEDs from salt-solution gradients alone (stack of eight 1 cm² membranes connected in series)
7. **Mechanism analogy:** Similar to how electric eel electrocytes generate electricity via ion channels

## Original Calculations

### Calculation 1: Blue Energy Membrane CAPEX Reduction
- **Per-pore power density (BNNT):** 15,300 W/m²
- **Current best commercial membrane power density:** ~5 W/m²
- **Conservative translation to membrane-area scale:** 1% of per-pore → **153 W/m²** → 30× improvement
- **5 MW blue energy plant with current membranes:** needs 5,000,000 W ÷ 5 W/m² = 1,000,000 m² of membrane → at $50–100/m² = **$50–100 million in membrane CAPEX alone**
- **Same plant at 30× power density:** 5,000,000 ÷ 153 = ~33,000 m² → at $50–100/m² = **$1.65–3.3 million** in membrane
- **Membrane CAPEX reduction: ~97%**
- **Potential LCOE impact:** Current $0.17/kWh → membrane is ~40% of plant CAPEX → 97% membrane reduction = ~39% total CAPEX reduction → LCOE could drop to $0.08–0.10/kWh range (still above fossil but competitive with offshore wind)

### Calculation 2: Lithium Recovery Throughput
- **31× Li+ transport multiplier** over standard membrane diffusion
- Current DLE membrane modules process ~10–50 m³/hr of brine per module
- At 31× throughput → 310–1,550 m³/hr per module
- Smackover brine Li concentration: ~200 ppm → per module: 62–310 kg Li/hr
- Current DLE target cost: $4,600–7,850/ton LCE
- Capital amortization spread over 31× throughput → could reach $2,000–3,000/ton LCE
- **Context:** LCE spot at $8K–10K/ton makes even $5,000/ton extraction profitable

### CRITICAL HONESTY NOTES
- Per-pore ≠ per-membrane-area. The 15,300 W/m² is measured at the individual nanotube pore opening. Packing density, inter-tube spacing, membrane support structures, and boundary effects will all reduce effective area power density. The 1% translation factor is a guess.
- Lab powered a watch, not a city. Stack of eight 1 cm² membranes is 8 cm² total. A 5 MW plant needs millions of cm².
- BNNT production cost: >$100/gram in small quantities (BNNT LLC, NAiEEL Technology). No large-scale production exists.
- No durability data published. How long do these membranes last under continuous operation?
- Selectivity ratios not quantified in press coverage — need actual paper for Li+/Na+, Li+/K+ separation factors.
