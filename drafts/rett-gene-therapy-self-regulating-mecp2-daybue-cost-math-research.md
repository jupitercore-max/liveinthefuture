# Research: Rett Gene Therapy's Self-Regulating MECP2 vs. Lifelong Daybue — The Cost Math

## Story Angle
Neurogene's NGN-401 gene therapy for Rett syndrome just posted Phase 1/2 data showing 47 developmental milestones gained across 10 patients — with no plateau through 30 months. The story isn't just the clinical data. It's the **economics of one-shot vs. lifelong treatment** and the **engineering breakthrough** that makes it possible: a self-regulating gene circuit (EXACT) that solves the uniquely dangerous MECP2 dosage sensitivity problem.

- **Source:** https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20260629170706/en/

### 3. EXACT Technology — Science Translational Medicine (pre-clinical)
- **Source:** PubMed PMID via Neurogene SEC filings, STM publication
- EXACT = Expression Attenuation via Construct Tuning
- MicroRNA-based feed-forward loop
- Self-regulates MECP2 expression on a cell-by-cell basis
- Prevents overexpression toxicity — critical because **MECP2 Duplication Syndrome** (too much MECP2) is as debilitating as Rett
- Conventional AAV-MECP2 constructs caused toxicity in wild-type mice and nonhuman primates; NGN-401 with EXACT was well-tolerated
- Only clinical candidate delivering **full-length human MECP2** (vs. Taysha's miniMECP2)

## Original Calculation: The Break-Even Math

### Daybue Annual Cost by Patient Weight

|-------------|----------|----------|-------------|--------------|-------------|

| ≥50 kg | 12,000 mg | 120 mL | 3.75 | 97.3 | $1,048,237 |

### Median Rett Patient Lifetime Cost on Daybue
- Diagnosis typically at age 2.5 years
- Treatment starts at age 2+ (FDA label)
- Survival to 45+ years = 42+ years of treatment potential
- Median annual cost (35-50 kg band over much of life): ~$700K/year
- **Lifetime Daybue cost: ~$700K × 42 years = $29.4 million**
- Even discounting at 3%: NPV ~$16.5M

### One-Shot Gene Therapy Break-Even
- Estimated NGN-401 price: $2.0-3.5M (based on comparables, CNS delivery premium)
- At $700K/year Daybue: break-even in **2.9-5.0 years**
- At $525K/year (smaller patient): break-even in **3.8-6.7 years**
- **Net savings per patient over 42 years: $26M-$27M** (at $700K/year)

### Novel Finding: Accelerating Returns
NGN-401 milestone gains aren't plateauing — they're **accelerating**:
- 6→12 months: 95% increase in milestones
- 6→≥12 months: 147% increase
- This contradicts the typical gene therapy trajectory where efficacy plateaus
- Suggests ongoing neurological remodeling, not just initial rescue

### The MECP2 Thermostat Problem
The fundamental engineering challenge nobody outside gene therapy circles discusses:
- Each Rett patient's cells have TWO X chromosomes, one normal, one mutant
- Random X-inactivation means ~50% of cells already make normal MECP2
- If gene therapy adds MECP2 to those cells: **MECP2 Duplication Syndrome** (equally devastating)
- Previous approaches (including Taysha's TSHA-102 with miniMECP2) couldn't solve this
- EXACT creates a molecular thermostat: higher MECP2 triggers microRNA-mediated suppression of the transgene
- This is genuine bioengineering innovation, not just a drug candidate
