# Research: Two Nuclear Reactors Went Critical in 14 Days. The Fuel Supply Can't Keep Up.

## Core Story
The US DOE Reactor Pilot Program just achieved two nuclear criticalities in 14 days — the fastest pace of new reactor startups in American history since the 1950s-60s golden age. But the fuel supply chain (HALEU) is the choke point that could strangle the renaissance.

## Original Analysis: The HALEU Supply Gap

### Current supply:
- Total US HALEU production to date: ~920 kg (Centrus)
- Each microreactor needs: ~120 kg (Antares spec)
- Current stockpile supports: ~7-8 reactors total

### Near-term pipeline:
- Centrus demonstration cascade: 16 centrifuges, not commercial scale
- Urenco Capenhurst (2031): 27 MT/year → ~225 reactors/year of Mark-0 class
- No other commercial HALEU enrichers operational

### The 400 GW math:
- Current US nuclear: ~97 GW
- 2050 target: 400 GW → need 303 GW new
- If all microreactors at 5 MWe: 303,000 MW / 5 MW = 60,600 reactors
- At 120 kg HALEU each: 60,600 × 120 kg = 7,272 MT of HALEU needed
- Urenco at 27 MT/year: 7,272 / 27 = 269 years to produce the fuel
- Even at 10x capacity: 26.9 years (barely feasible by 2050)

### Reality check:
- Not all 303 GW would be microreactors — mix includes SMRs (NuScale 77 MWe), large LWR restarts
- But even SMRs need HALEU (most Gen IV designs do)
- LWR restarts use conventional LEU (<5% enrichment) — domestic supply exists
- The bottleneck is specifically for the innovative designs that just proved they work
- The Catch-22: the physics has been proven (2 criticalities in 14 days), but the fuel supply to commercialize at scale won't exist until 2031 at the earliest

### Cost comparison:
- Vogtle Units 3&4: $36.8B / 2,234 MW = $16,472/kW
- Ward 250 at 5 MWe scale: costs undisclosed, but Valar raised privately (startup capital model vs. regulated utility)
- Antares: $140M raised, targeting 100 kWe-1 MWe product → even at $140M/MW, that's $140,000/kW (but early R&D)
- Industry target for nth-of-a-kind microreactors: $5,000-10,000/kW
- The fuel is the wild card: HALEU at commercial scale doesn't have a stable price yet
