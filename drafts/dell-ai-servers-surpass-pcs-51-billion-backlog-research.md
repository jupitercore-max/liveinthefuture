## Novel Calculations

### Fulfillment Gap
- Orders per quarter: $24.4B
- Revenue (shipped) per quarter: $16.1B
- Gap: $8.3B ($24.4B - $16.1B) = 51.6% more demand than supply
- This means Dell can only fill ~66% of demand in a quarter

### Backlog Velocity
- Q to Q backlog growth: $43B → $51.3B = +$8.3B (+19.3%)
- Annualized backlog accumulation rate: ~$33B/year at current pace
- At $60B/year shipping target, backlog would take ~10 months to clear if orders stopped today

### The Crossing Point
- Dell's identity since 1984: PC company
- Q1 FY2027: AI servers ($16.1B) > PCs ($14.6B) — first time EVER
- This is not a blip: at 757% YoY growth vs 17% YoY, the gap widens every quarter
- By Q4 FY2027: AI servers could be 2-3x PC revenue

### Revenue Composition Shift (annualized)
- FY2026 total (actual): ~$92B
- FY2027 guidance midpoint: $167B
- AI server portion: $60B / $167B = 35.9% of total revenue
- PCs portion: ~$58B / $167B = 34.7% (estimated)
- AI servers > PCs at the annual level too
