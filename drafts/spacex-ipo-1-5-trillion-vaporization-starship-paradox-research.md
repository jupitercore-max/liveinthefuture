# Research: SpaceX IPO — $1.5 Trillion Vaporized While Starship Sticks Its Best Landing

## Core Thesis
SpaceX's stock shed $1.5 trillion in 6 weeks — the largest IPO value destruction in history — at the exact moment its Starship achieved unprecedented technical milestones. The market isn't punishing the rockets. It's repricing the AI bet to zero.

## Original Calculations

### Calculation 1: Largest IPO Value Destruction in History
- SpaceX: $1.5T destroyed in 6 weeks
- Previous records:
  - Rivian (Nov 2021): peaked at ~$170B, lost ~$155B (85% decline over 1 year)
  - Facebook (May 2012): lost ~$50B (54% decline in 4 months)
  - Snap (Mar 2017): lost ~$20B
  - WeWork (Oct 2021): lost ~$39B
- SpaceX's $1.5T loss > **next 10 largest IPO value destructions COMBINED**
- $1.5T ≈ 1 Tesla, > GDP of Mexico ($1.3T), > GDP of South Korea ($1.7T)

### Calculation 2: The Implied AI Repricing
- At peak ($225.64, ~$3T): Morgan Stanley values space+Starlink at $136 → AI business implied at $225.64 - $136 = $89.64/share → $1.18T for AI
- At current ($113.50, ~$1.5T): Space+Starlink should still be $136 → AI implied at $113.50 - $136 = **negative $22.50/share**
- **The market is saying SpaceX's AI business (including xAI) is worth LESS than zero** — it's actually a drag on value
- This for a company that raised $25B in bonds specifically for AI infrastructure

### Calculation 3: The Technical Progress vs. Market Price Paradox
- Flight 1 (Apr 2023): Exploded 4 minutes after launch. Private valuation: ~$150B
- Flight 7 (Jan 2025): First booster catch attempt. Private valuation: ~$350B
- Flight 12 (May 2026): First V3 Starship. Private valuation (pre-IPO): ~$1.77T
- Flight 13 (Jul 2026): First intact upper stage landing, first Starlink V3 deploy. Public market cap: $1.5T (DOWN from $3T four weeks earlier)
- **Price per successful flight milestone:**
  - Pre-IPO: each milestone added $50-200B in private valuation
  - Post-IPO: the best flight in Starship history coincided with the stock hitting record lows
  - The market has decoupled technical achievement from equity value

### Calculation 4: Starship Replacement Economics
- Falcon 9 cost/launch: ~$67M, ~50/year = $3.35B revenue
- SpaceX winding down Falcon 9 after 2028
- If Starship costs $10M/launch (Musk's target), needs 335 launches/year just to match Falcon 9 revenue
- At 26 launches/year (current manifest extrapolation), revenue = $260M — a 92% revenue decline
- But Starship can carry 10x payload → revenue per kg drops, volume must rise 10x
- Falcon 9's 50-launch cadence took 15 years to build. Starship needs to match it in 2-3 years
