# Research: Intuitive Surgical's $987,000-Per-Robot Recurring Revenue Machine

## Story Angle
The da Vinci 5 just posted its best quarter in Intuitive Surgical's history — $2.89B revenue, 85% recurring, $1,830 per procedure — and the stock fell 14%. The market fixated on GLP-1 bariatric erosion and slowing procedure growth. The math tells a different story: each of Intuitive's 11,710 installed da Vinci systems now generates approximately $987,000 per year in revenue, a "razor ratio" that puts Gillette, HP printers, and enterprise SaaS to shame. Cardiac procedures are growing 39%, nipple-sparing mastectomy 43%, and the SP stapler went from 40% to 60% eligible-case adoption in a single quarter. This isn't a company losing to GLP-1s — it's a company migrating its procedure mix faster than the drugs can eat into it.

## Original Calculations

### 1. Revenue Per Installed System Per Year ("The $987K Robot")
- Annual revenue run rate: $2.89B × 4 = $11.56B
- Installed da Vinci systems: 11,710
- **Revenue per system per year: $11.56B / 11,710 = ~$987,000**
- Note: This includes ALL revenue (INA + systems + services) divided by installed base, reflecting the full economic value of each deployed system.

### 2. The "Razor Ratio" — Recurring vs. Capital Revenue
- Annual recurring revenue: $2.47B × 4 = $9.88B
- Annual system (capital) revenue: $685M × 4 = $2.74B  
- **Razor ratio: 3.6:1** — for every $1 in system sales, Intuitive collects $3.60 in recurring revenue
- Compare: Gillette ~4:1 (blades vs handle), HP ~3:1 (ink vs printer), Nespresso ~2:1 (pods vs machine)

### 3. INA Revenue Per System Per Year
- Q2 procedures: 889,000
- Installed systems: 11,710
- Procedures per system per quarter: 889,000 / 11,710 ≈ 75.9
- Procedures per system per year: ~304
- INA revenue per procedure: $1,830
- **INA revenue per system per year: 304 × $1,830 ≈ $556,000**

### 4. Estimated System Lifetime Value
- Typical system life: 7-10 years (based on trade-in timing — 144 trade-ins from older systems)
- Conservative 8-year life:
  - Recurring revenue per system over life: $556K INA + ~$161K services = ~$717K/yr × 8 = $5.74M
  - System sale: ~$1.5-2.5M (ASP varies by config)
  - **Lifetime value: $5.74M recurring + ~$2M purchase = ~$7.7M per system**
  - **Multiple of purchase price: 3.8-5.1×**

### 5. Procedure Migration Math (Bariatric → Cardiac/Oncology)
- Bariatric: <3% of US da Vinci procedures, declining
- Cardiac: growing 39% YoY
- Nipple-sparing mastectomy: growing 43% YoY
- Even if bariatric goes to zero, it only removes <3% of volume
- Cardiac + NSM growth alone adds >10% of the growth rate
- **GLP-1 impact on Intuitive: at most -3% of procedures, offset 10:1 by cardiac+NSM growth**

### 6. Trade-In Acceleration as Revenue Multiplier
- Q2 2025: 83 trade-ins → Q2 2026: 144 trade-ins (+73%)
- Each trade-in replaces an older system with dV5 → higher ASP + higher INA/procedure ($1,830 vs prior gen lower)
- 144/468 = **30.8% of all Q2 placements were upgrades**
- iPhone-like upgrade cycle: existing customers buying new, more profitable robots

### 7. ASC Penetration TAM
- 27 ASC placements in Q2 2026 (20 were XiR, the ambulatory-specific variant)
- ~6,100 Medicare-certified ASCs in the US performing surgical procedures
- Current da Vinci ASC penetration: estimated <1%
- Even 10% penetration = 610 additional systems
- At $987K/system/year = **~$602M additional annual revenue at 10% ASC penetration**

## Strongest Counterarguments (must address honestly per STORY_GUIDE)
1. **Decelerating growth:** Slowest procedure growth year since pre-COVID. Market paying >60x forward earnings for what might be maturing growth.
2. **Extended-use instruments:** Coming H1 2027, will LOWER INA/procedure for benign procedures. Intuitive is voluntarily cutting its razor blade revenue per use.
3. **Chinese competitors:** Wego Medical's MicroHand, Tinavi Medical robots cost 1/3 of da Vinci. China is Intuitive's fastest-growing market.
4. **The Surgie threat:** Humanoid robots at $16K vs $1.6M. UCSD published in Nature. The 100:1 cost ratio is real.
5. **ACA subsidy expiration:** HCA Healthcare warned about more uninsured patients avoiding elective procedures.
