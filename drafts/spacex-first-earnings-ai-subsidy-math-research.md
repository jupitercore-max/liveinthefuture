# Research Notes: SpaceX First Earnings — The AI Subsidy Math Nobody's Running

## Story Angle
SpaceX just reported its first-ever public quarterly earnings (Q2 2026). The headline: revenue nearly doubled, beating estimates. But the real story is in three numbers that nobody has put together: SpaceX is spending 236% of its revenue on CapEx, its AI business flipped from a $2.5B operating loss to $1.1B EBITDA positive in a single quarter, and approximately 75% of that AI surprise can be traced to a single customer contract with Anthropic.

## Original Analysis
1. **CapEx-to-Revenue Ratio**: SpaceX at 236% vs any other public tech company (Meta ~31%, Alphabet ~18%, Amazon ~16%). This is historically unprecedented.
2. **Anthropic Contract Contribution**: $1.25B/month contract ramping in May/June explains ~75% of AI revenue.
3. **Starlink ARPU Decay Curve**: From $99 (2023) to $66 (Q1 2026), 22% YoY decline while subscribers doubled. Revenue ceiling calculation.
4. **Cash Burn vs Profitability**: $3.5B EBITDA but $18.4B CapEx = net $14.9B cash consumed from operations alone.

## Calculations

### 1. CapEx-to-Revenue Ratio (Q2 2026)
SpaceX: $18.4B / $7.8B = 236%
For comparison (approximate Q2 2026 ranges from recent earnings):
- Meta: ~$13B / ~$42B = ~31%
- Alphabet: ~$17B / ~$94B = ~18%
- Amazon: ~$25B / ~$155B = ~16%
- Microsoft: ~$22B / ~$66B = ~33%

SpaceX's ratio is 7-15x higher than any hyperscaler.

### 2. Anthropic Contract Math
$1.25B/month contract, ramping in May-June:
- April: ~$0 (not yet started or minimal)
- May: ~$625M (ramp month, est. 50%)
- June: ~$1.25B (full ramp)
- Estimated Q2 Anthropic contribution: ~$1.875B

Total AI revenue est. (from 250% YoY surge on ~$727M Q2 2025 base): ~$2.5B
Anthropic as % of AI revenue: ~75%

### 3. AI Business EBITDA Swing
Q1 2026: ($2.469B) operating loss on $818M revenue
Q2 2026: $1.1B EBITDA on ~$2.5B revenue
Swing: ~$3.6B in one quarter (largest single-quarter EBITDA turnaround for a tech business segment)

EBITDA margin: $1.1B / ~$2.5B = ~44%
This is cloud IaaS territory (AWS operating margin ~35-37%, Azure est. ~42-45%)

### 4. Starlink ARPU Floor Model
2023: $99/month
Q1 2026: $66/month (33% decline over ~2.5 years)
YoY decline: 22%
At 22% annual decline:
- Q1 2027: ~$51/month
- Q1 2028: ~$40/month
- Q1 2029: ~$31/month

Subscribers (doubling annually):
- Q2 2026: 12M
- Q2 2027: ~24M (at current growth)
- Q2 2028: ~48M

Revenue ceiling (ARPU × subs × 3 months/quarter):
- Q2 2026: $66 × 12M × 3 = $2.376B consumer + enterprise = ~$4.3B total connectivity
- Q2 2027: $51 × 24M × 3 = $3.672B consumer + enterprise (grows slower) ≈ $6-7B
- Q2 2028: $40 × 48M × 3 = $5.760B consumer + enterprise ≈ $9-10B

Plateau scenario: subscriber growth slows to 30% by 2028, ARPU stabilizes at ~$45
Revenue: $45 × 31M × 3 = $4.185B + enterprise = ~$7B/quarter = ~$28B/year
This would still represent healthy growth from the current ~$17B annual run rate

### 5. Cash Sustainability
Q2 EBITDA: $3.5B
Q2 CapEx: $18.4B
Net cash consumed: ~$14.9B/quarter
CFO said similar CapEx for "next couple of quarters"
At $14.9B/quarter burn: $59.6B/year
SpaceX raised $75B in IPO. Cash runway at this burn rate: ~1.25 years from IPO proceeds alone (assumes no debt)
But SpaceX also has Starlink's strong operating cash flow (~$10.4B annualized from $2.6B quarterly EBITDA)
