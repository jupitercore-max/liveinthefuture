# Research: PJM Capacity Price 1,038% — Factory Displacement Math

## Core Story
PJM Interconnection capacity prices jumped from $28.92/MW-day (2024) to $329.17/MW-day — a 1,038% increase — driven primarily by data center demand. This is pricing Rust Belt manufacturers off the grid.

## Key Data Points

### The Belden Brick Case Study
- **Company:** Belden Brick Company, Sugarcreek, Ohio — 141 years old (founded 1885)
- **Products in:** Texas Alamo, Notre Dame University
- **Capacity charge:** $1,600/month → $12,000/month (650% increase on that line item alone)
- **Overall electricity costs:** up ~90% YoY
- **Source:** Reuters July 7, 2026; Akron Beacon Journal op-ed by president Bradley H. Belden (Feb 2026)

### PJM Grid Facts
- Largest US grid operator, 13 states + DC (Mid-Atlantic to Midwest)
- Covers largest amount of data center demand in the world
- 5 of 8 emerging US data center hub states are in the Rust Belt (Synergy Research Group)
- 30,000–60,000 MW of data center load wants to come on PJM system by 2030 (Joseph Bowring, independent market monitor)
- AEP Ohio alone: ~13,000 MW in its queue
- PJM warned of electricity shortfall as early as 2027

### Capacity Price History
- 2024: $28.92/MW-day
- 2025-26 delivery year: 833% increase (Charles River Associates study)
- 2026-27 delivery year: additional 22% after regulatory intervention (price cap from PA Gov. Shapiro coalition)
- Current: $329.17/MW-day (1,038% cumulative from 2024 baseline)

### Industrial Price Impacts (DOE data via Reuters)
- Pennsylvania industrial electricity: +31% YoY (Dec 2025)
- Ohio industrial electricity: +26% YoY (Dec 2025)
- Nationwide industrial average: +7% YoY
- Residential PA: +14%, Residential OH: +9%
- Capacity charges = ~10% of residential bills but up to 30% for manufacturers

### EIA STEO July 2026 — Historic Crossover
- 2025: record 4,195 billion kWh total US demand
- 2026: forecast 4,269 billion kWh
- 2027: forecast 4,399 billion kWh
- **First time ever:** Commercial sector electricity demand will surpass residential in 2026
  - Residential: 1,508 billion kWh (down from 1,515 record)
  - Commercial: 1,550 billion kWh (up from 1,493 record)
- Data centers classified as "commercial" — this crossover IS the data center story

### Ohio PUCO Ruling (July 9, 2026 — just published!)
- Approved AEP Ohio settlement: data centers must pay for minimum 85% of requested power even if unused
- Must prove financial viability
- Must pay exit fee if project canceled
- Chair Jennifer French: protect ratepayers while providing "dependable environment"
