# Research: Pentagon Drone Budget 244× Increase

## Story Thesis
The Pentagon's FY2027 budget requests $55 billion for drone and autonomous warfare — a 244× increase from $225 million the year before. But Ukraine already receives 200,000 drones per month. The Pentagon's entire multi-year procurement target of 200,000 units equals one month of Ukraine's current consumption. The math exposes how far behind the U.S. industrial base has fallen in the drone era.

## Novel Contribution
1. **Exchange ratio math:** Calculate the cost asymmetry. Navy burned ~220 interceptors in Red Sea ($2-4M each = $440M-$880M) against Houthi threats costing a fraction. Ratio: 10:1 to 200:1 cost disadvantage per engagement.
2. **Production gap analysis:** Pentagon DDP targets 200,000 units by end of 2027 (30K + 60K + 150K across Gauntlet phases). Ukraine receives 200,000/month (2.4M/year). Annual production: 4.5M. Pentagon multi-year plan = 4.4% of Ukraine's single-year output.
3. **Unit cost comparison table:** Pentagon buying at $5,000 declining to $2,300. Ukrainian FPV drones cost $400-$500. At Ukraine prices, $55B buys 110-137M drones. At Pentagon prices, 11-24M.
4. **The Shrike irony:** Pentagon's own competition winner is Ukrainian-designed. The U.S. is buying battle-tested technology from the country it's been arming.
