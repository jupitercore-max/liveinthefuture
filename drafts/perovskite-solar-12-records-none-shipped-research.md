## Core Thesis
Perovskite solar cells are smashing efficiency records at an unprecedented pace — more records fell in the first five months of 2026 than in all of 2023. But the gap between lab record and commercial panel has never been wider. Only one company (Oxford PV) ships commercial perovskite modules. The story is about the tension between acceleration in the lab and stasis in the factory.

### Historical Acceleration
- 2009: Perovskite first demonstrated at 3.8% efficiency
- 2014: 17.9% (5 years, +14.1 pp)
- 2019: 25.2% (10 years, +21.4 pp)
- 2024: 33.7% (tandem) (15 years)
- 2026: 34.85% (tandem) (17 years)
- Silicon: 6% (1954) → 26.1% (2022) — 68 years to reach ~26%
- Perovskite reached 26%+ in ~14 years

### The Silicon Ceiling
- Shockley-Queisser theoretical limit for single-junction silicon: 29.4%
- Best silicon cell ever: 26.8% (LONGi, 2024)
- Perovskite-silicon tandem theoretical limit: ~46%
- LONGi's 34.85% is already 20% above silicon's theoretical max

### The Stability Gap (crucial)
- Best perovskite stability: 24,000 hours (~2.7 years) at 26% efficiency (Korea/Toledo/Seoul)
- Standard silicon panel warranty: 25-30 years
- The gap: perovskite stability is 1 order of magnitude short
- Operational stability testing protocol (IEC 61215): 1,000 hours (damp heat), 200 cycles (thermal)
- Most perovskite records tested under "standard illumination conditions" — not field conditions

### The Commercialization Bottleneck
- Oxford PV: ONLY company shipping commercial perovskite modules (since late 2024)
- Oxford PV factory: Brandenburg, Germany — capacity ~100 MW/year
- First Solar: licensed Oxford PV patents for US manufacturing (Feb 2026)
- Fraunhofer ISE: Pero-Si-SCALE lab for industry partner testing at full wafer size
- Global silicon PV production: ~500 GW/year (2025)
- Global perovskite production: ~0.1 GW/year (estimated)
- Ratio: perovskite is 0.02% of silicon production volume

### China Dominance
- Of the top ~10 efficiency records in 2026, approximately 7-8 are from Chinese institutions
- LONGi (China), Nankai University (China), Huazhong University (China), Chinese Academy of Sciences (multiple labs)
- Non-Chinese: Korea University/University of Toledo/Seoul National University, Fraunhofer ISE (Germany), Oxford PV (UK)

### Cost Projections
- Perovskite manufacturing cost estimate: $0.15-0.25/W (vs silicon $0.20-0.30/W)
- All-perovskite tandem market: $237M (2025) → $2.28B (2032) at 38.2% CAGR
- Perovskite overall market: $0.7B (2023) → $2.7B (2028) at 33.3% CAGR

## Strongest Counterargument
The stability problem may be structural, not just a matter of more R&D time. Perovskite's crystal structure degrades under moisture, heat, and UV — exactly the conditions solar panels face outdoors for decades. Some researchers argue the ion migration issue is fundamental to halide perovskites. If so, lab records are irrelevant because the material may never survive 25 years outdoors at commercial scale regardless of efficiency.
