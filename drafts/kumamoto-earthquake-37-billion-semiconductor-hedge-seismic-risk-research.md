# Research: Kumamoto Earthquake Semiconductor Supply Chain Risk

## Core Thesis
Japan invested $37 billion building a semiconductor hedge against Taiwan geopolitical risk in Kumamoto, Kyushu. A 7.1-magnitude earthquake on July 28, 2026 revealed the irony: they traded geopolitical concentration risk for seismic concentration risk, placing the world's most critical chip diversification bet in one of the most earthquake-prone prefectures on the planet.

## Original Analysis: The Diversification Paradox

### Calculation 1: Daily Production Value at Risk
- JASM Phase 1: 55,000 wafers/month at 12/16nm
- Revenue per wafer (12/16nm): ~$3,500-5,000 (industry estimates based on TSMC pricing)
- Monthly production value: 55,000 × $4,250 (midpoint) = $233.75M/month
- Daily production value: ~$7.8M/day
- Including Sony (4 CIS fabs), Renesas, Texas Instruments, Rohm, Mitsubishi Electric:
  - Kyushu total semiconductor output: ¥1 trillion/year = ~$6.7B/year
  - Daily value: ~$18.4M/day
  - With TSMC expansion (60% increase): ~$29.4M/day
- **Every day of full Kyushu shutdown = ~$29M in lost semiconductor production**

### Calculation 2: Concentration Risk Score
- Pre-TSMC (2020): Kyushu = ~40% of Japan's chip output, Japan = ~15% of global
  → Kyushu = ~6% of global semiconductor production
- Post-TSMC expansion (2026): Kyushu = ~50% of Japan's output, growing
  → Kyushu = ~7.5% of global production and rising
- By 2028 (Phase 2 operational): Could reach ~9-10% of Japan's output concentrated in one prefecture
- Kumamoto alone: was 30% of Kyushu before TSMC, now potentially 40%+ with JASM

### Calculation 3: Seismic Risk Comparison — Taiwan vs Kumamoto
- Taiwan: ~2,000 earthquakes/year (most small), M7+ roughly once per 15-25 years
- Kumamoto: M7+ earthquakes in 2016 AND 2026 — twice in 10 years
- TSMC's Taiwan fabs: clustered in Hsinchu/Tainan/Kaohsiung (lower seismic frequency than east coast)
- TSMC's Japan fab: directly in Kumamoto (proven M7+ seismic zone)
- Irony: the "diversification" site has a HIGHER recent earthquake frequency than the primary site

### Calculation 4: Recovery Timeline Cost
- 2016 Kumamoto earthquake impact on auto sector: Aisin plant severely damaged → Toyota nationwide shutdown lasting weeks → estimated ¥300B ($2B+) in lost production
- 2026 recovery timeline: 1-3 weeks for most facilities
  - TSMC: Gradual resumption, calibrations (days to weeks)
  - Renesas: Aug 5 earliest (8+ days)
  - Sony: mid-August full recovery (~2.5 weeks)
  - Toyota: Aug 5 minimum (8+ days, multiple plants)
- Estimated total lost production value: $200-500M across all sectors
- But the semiconductor downtime ripple effects multiply: $1 of lost chip production can cause $10-100 in downstream manufacturing delays
