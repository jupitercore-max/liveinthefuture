# Research Notes: Medicare GLP-1 Bridge Program — Article #539

## Topic
Medicare's GLP-1 Bridge Program: launched July 1, 2026. $50/month copay for beneficiaries, $245/month negotiated MFN price. 18-month demonstration (through Dec 2027). The break-even price for Medicare cost neutrality is $150/month — 39% below what was negotiated. Generics don't arrive until 2032.

## Original Calculation: The Fiscal Gap
**What nobody has run:** Cross-reference the Bridge's 18-month window against the JAMA MFN pricing model's 10-year break-even analysis, then overlay the STEP 1 extension's discontinuation data to model the "rebound cost" when the program sunsets.

### Key Numbers
- CMS net cost per enrollee: ($245 - $50) × 12 = $2,340/year
- At 3M enrollees (JAMA base case 10% uptake): $7.02B/year in drug costs
- Over 18 months: ~$10.5B
- JAMA MFN study: At $245/mo, NO scenario achieves net savings over 10 years
- At $150/mo + 40% adherence: net SAVINGS at every uptake level
- The gap: $245 - $150 = $95/month per patient = $1,140/year
- For 3M patients: $3.4B/year in overpricing above break-even
- Semaglutide LOE: 2032 (year 7) — Bridge ends 2027, 5 years before generics

### The Bridge Sunset Problem
- STEP 1 extension: 2/3 of weight loss regained within 1 year of discontinuation
- Cardiometabolic improvements revert to baseline
- SELECT trial: CV benefits emerge at ~12 months
- Bridge is 18 months — benefits barely accrue before program ends
- When 3M people stop simultaneously in Dec 2027: mass weight regain, reversal of whatever CV protection accrued

### The Adherence Paradox (from JAMA MFN study)
- Higher adherence (70%) INCREASES net spending vs lower adherence (40%)
- Because weight loss plateaus but drug costs persist
- At $245/mo, 10% uptake: 40% adherence = $5.0B net; 70% adherence = $15.8B net
- The drugs work TOO well — people stay on them, and CMS keeps paying

## Primary Sources

1. **Hwang et al., JAMA Health Forum (April 2025)**
   - DOI: 10.1001/jamahealthforum.2025.0905
   - "Fiscal Impact of Expanded Medicare Coverage for GLP-1 Receptor Agonists to Treat Obesity"
   - 30M eligible, base case (10% uptake, 40% adherence, 10% discount): $65.9B drug costs, $18.2B offsets = $47.7B net over 10 years
   - Full 3-way sensitivity table (uptake × adherence × discount)

2. **Hwang et al., JAMA Network Open (March 2026)**
   - "Most Favored Nation Pricing and Affordability of GLP-1RAs for Obesity Treatment in Medicare"
   - At $245/mo MFN, 30% uptake, 40% adherence: $73.9B drug cost, $56.3B offsets = $17.6B net over 10 years
   - At $150/mo with 40% adherence: net savings at ALL uptake levels (-$4.5B at 10%, -$11.0B at 30%, -$30.5B at 90%)
   - Break-even price = ~$150/month
   - Semaglutide LOE 2032 with 32-59% additional discounts modeled years 7-10

3. **STEP 1 Extension (Wilding et al., Diabetes Obesity Metabolism 2022)**
   - PMID: 35441470, DOI: 10.1111/dom.14725
   - 327 participants in extension
   - Mean weight loss on semaglutide: 17.3% at week 68
   - Weight regain after discontinuation: 11.6 percentage points by week 120
   - Net weight loss 1 year post-discontinuation: 5.6% (down from 17.3%)
   - "Participants regained two-thirds of their prior weight loss"
   - Cardiometabolic improvements reverted towards baseline

4. **CMS Bridge Program Details**
   - Launched July 1, 2026
   - $50/month flat copay (does NOT count toward Part D out-of-pocket max)
   - Demonstration through December 31, 2027
   - Covers: Wegovy (injection + pill), Zepbound KwikPen, Foundayo pill
   - Does NOT cover: Ozempic (not FDA-approved for weight loss)
   - Prior authorization required + lifestyle program certification
   - BMI tiers: 35+ auto-qualify; 30+ with HF/uncontrolled HTN/severe CKD; 27+ with pre-diabetes/prior MI/stroke/PAD
   - Eligible: 12.5-20M; expected uptake: "single-digit millions" (CMS director Chris Klomp)

5. **CBO estimate:** $35B increase in federal spending over 9 years

6. **Morgan Stanley estimate:** 10% uptake = ~$5.5B/year; 20% uptake = ~$11B/year

7. **BMO analyst Evan Seigerman:** ~$3B/year in added pharma sales assuming modest uptake

8. **Balance program (shelved April 2026):** Insurer-run permanent Part D benefit. CVS, UnitedHealthcare declined to participate. Bridge extended as stopgap.
