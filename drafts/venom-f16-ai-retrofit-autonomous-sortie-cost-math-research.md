# Research: VENOM F-16 AI Retrofit — The Autonomous Combat Sortie Cost Math

## Core Thesis
DARPA and the USAF just flew an F-16 under AI control for the first time using an aftermarket retrofit kit. But the real story isn't the technology — it's the math. The US has 900+ F-16s. Allied air forces operate 4,500+ more. A bolt-on autonomy kit that turns existing jets into AI-capable combat platforms has a fundamentally different cost curve than building purpose-built autonomous drones from scratch.

## Key Data Points

### VENOM Program Details (from DARPA/USAF)
- **Full name**: Viper Experimentation and Next-gen Operations Model – Autonomy Flying Testbed (VENOM-AFT)
- **Partners**: DARPA Tactical Technology Office + USAF 96th Test Wing + 53rd Wing
- **Location**: Eglin Air Force Base, Florida
- **Milestone**: First AI-controlled flight of modified F-16, July 2026
- **Key quote** (BG James Valpiani, DARPA): "The air force and DARPA team has automated flight controls and sensors on a standard F-16 without changing the jet's core software"
- **Toggle capability**: Pilot can switch between human and AI control "with the flip of a switch" (FlightGlobal)
- **Physical mods**: Auto-throttle added, additional computing systems, sensors, flight instrumentation, specialized software
- **Fleet**: At least 4 F-16s modified (3 arrived 2024, 4th in 2025), total of 6 F-16Cs planned
- **Testing**: 40th Flight Test Squadron (developmental) + 85th Test and Evaluation Squadron (operational)
- **Simulation**: "countless aircraft combat scenarios" since 2024 — 1v1, 2v1, WVR, BVR. "A specific scenario can be run 1,000 times" (Maj. Trent McMullen)
- **Human-on-the-loop**: Pilot always in cockpit. "There will never be a time where the VENOM aircraft will solely 'fly by itself' without a human component" (Lt. Col. Joe Gagnon)

### Next Phase: AIR Program
- **Name**: Artificial Intelligence Reinforcements (AIR)
- **Goal**: Test multiple AI agents in live-flight scenarios
- **Purpose**: Enable human pilots to "seamlessly command and orchestrate teams of autonomous, uncrewed aircraft"
- **New PM**: Lt. Col. Patrick "Dice" Highland, Ph.D., replacing Valpiani

### F-16 Fleet Numbers (for cost calculation)
- **USAF active F-16s**: ~900+ (mix of C/D variants)
- **Air National Guard + Reserve**: ~200+
- **Total worldwide F-16s**: ~4,500+ in 25+ countries (largest fleet outside US: Turkey, Israel, Egypt, South Korea, Pakistan, Taiwan, etc.)
- **F-16 unit cost**: $14.6M (1998 dollars) ≈ $28-30M adjusted for inflation
- **F-16 flight hour cost**: ~$27,000/hour (USAF data)
- **Production**: 4,600+ built since 1974, still in production (new F-16V Block 70/72)

### Comparative Cost Points
- **CCA (Collaborative Combat Aircraft)**: ~$25M per unit (target), purpose-built autonomous drone wingman
  - YFQ-42A (General Atomics) and YFQ-44A (Anduril) — unveiled March 2026
  - Designed to be "attritable" (acceptable to lose in combat)
- **F-35A**: ~$80M per unit (current), ~$42,000/flight hour
- **F-22A**: ~$150M per unit (no longer produced), ~$70,000/flight hour
- **QF-16 (full drone conversion)**: ~$6.7M per conversion (for target drones, not combat)

### Original Calculation: VENOM Retrofit Economics
- **Estimated VENOM kit cost**: Unknown publicly, but comparable defense retrofit programs suggest $3-8M per aircraft
  - AN/APG-83 SABR AESA radar upgrade: ~$3M
  - Sniper/LITENING targeting pod: ~$4-5M
  - Electronic warfare suite upgrades: $2-6M
  - Reasonable estimate for autonomy package: $5-10M per aircraft
- **At $5M per kit across 900 USAF F-16s**: $4.5 billion for an autonomous-capable fleet
  - vs. $25B for 1,000 new CCAs
  - vs. $80B for 1,000 new F-35s
- **Per sortie cost**: If autonomous capability reduces crew training costs ($5M per pilot over career) and increases sortie rate (no human fatigue limits), the math shifts dramatically

### The Simulation-to-Experience Ratio
- **Human F-16 pilot career**: ~2,000-4,000 flight hours total. ~200-300 sorties/year. Maybe 50-100 BFM engagements in training.
- **VENOM AI**: "1,000 times" per scenario × "countless scenarios" since 2024. Conservative estimate: 10,000+ simulated combat engagements before first flight.
- **Ratio**: The AI has "fought" more engagements before its first real flight than a human pilot will in an entire 20-year career.

### The Proliferation Math
- **25+ allied air forces** operate F-16s
- If the VENOM kit is exportable under FMS (Foreign Military Sales), allied autonomous capability could multiply within 2-3 years
- This is fundamentally different from CCA, which requires new production lines
- Turkey alone has 245 F-16s, Israel has 175, South Korea has 169
