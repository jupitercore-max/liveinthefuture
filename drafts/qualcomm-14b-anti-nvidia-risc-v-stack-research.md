# Research Notes: Qualcomm's $14B Anti-NVIDIA RISC-V Stack

## Topic
Qualcomm assembling a ~$14B anti-NVIDIA/anti-ARM full-stack data center platform via three acquisitions in ~6 months.

## Key Acquisitions
1. **Ventana Micro Systems** (RISC-V server CPUs) — completed Jan 2026, terms undisclosed. Veyron V2: up to 32 RISC-V cores at 3.85 GHz, 128 MB L3. Founded 2018 by Balaji Baktha.
2. **Tenstorrent** ($8–10B, in talks) — RISC-V AI accelerators, Tensix core architecture. Led by Jim Keller (CEO since 2023). Series D: $693M at $2.6B post-money (Dec 2024). Investors: Samsung Securities, AFW Partners, Bezos Expeditions, Fidelity, Hyundai, LG. Customer contracts ~$150M. The Information reported June 16, 2026.
3. **Modular** (~$4B, advanced talks) — AI compiler/runtime (MAX inference engine). Founded 2022 by Chris Lattner (LLVM/Swift creator) and Tim Davis (ex-Google). $380M total raised, $250M Sep 2025 at $1.6B valuation. Bloomberg reported June 22, 2026.
4. **Also: Alphawave Semi** ($2.4B, completed late 2025) — high-speed chip interconnect.

## Original Calculations

### CUDA Tax
- NVIDIA non-GAAP gross margin: 75.0%
- AMD TTM gross margin: 49.5%
- Margin delta: ~25.5 percentage points
- NVIDIA annualized DC revenue: $75.2B × 4 = ~$300B
- "CUDA premium" in gross profit: $300B × 0.255 = ~$76.5B/year
- Note: uses company-wide margins as proxy; segment-specific DC margins not separately disclosed by either company in comparable format
- Caveat: not all of the premium is CUDA — hardware advantages, manufacturing scale, ecosystem effects all contribute

### Jim Keller Career Pattern
- DEC: Alpha 21264
- AMD: K8 (Athlon 64/Opteron) — first x86-64, forced Intel to adopt AMD64
- Apple: A4/A5 (iPhone 4, iPad 2) — launched Apple's silicon independence
- Tesla: FSD HW3 — replaced NVIDIA Drive PX
- Intel: brief stint
- Tenstorrent: RISC-V AI accelerators
- Pattern: each engagement broke a silicon dependency/monopoly

### ARM Royalty Context
- ARM-Qualcomm lawsuit: ARM sued over Nuvia license terms (2022), Qualcomm won jury trial Dec 2024
- RISC-V eliminates per-unit royalties and licensing dependency
- Qualcomm already using RISC-V in SoCs since Snapdragon 865 (2019)
