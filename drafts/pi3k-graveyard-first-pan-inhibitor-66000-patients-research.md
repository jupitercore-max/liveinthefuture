# Research: The PI3K Graveyard — 20 Years, 10+ Failed Drugs, 66,000 Patients Finally Get a Targeted Therapy

## Core Thesis
On July 14, 2026, the FDA approved REVTORPYK (gedatolisib) — the first drug EVER to comprehensively inhibit all class I PI3K isoforms (α, β, δ, γ) AND both mTOR complexes (mTORC1, mTORC2). This is remarkable because:

1. The PI3K pathway has been a drug target for ~40 years
2. At least 10 PI3K-targeting drugs were approved, withdrawn, or failed in late-stage trials
3. The FDA described the PI3K drug class's safety record as producing "unprecedented findings in oncology"
4. ~66,000 women living with HR+/HER2- metastatic breast cancer who are PIK3CA wild-type had NO targeted PI3K therapy until now
5. The approval came from Celcuity — a small Minneapolis biotech — not a pharma giant

## The PI3K Graveyard (Documented Failures & Withdrawals)

### Pan-PI3K inhibitors that failed in solid tumors:
- **Buparlisib (Novartis, NVP-BKM120)** — Pan-PI3K inhibitor. BELLE-2 and BELLE-3 Phase 3 trials showed some PFS benefit but severe liver toxicity (elevated ALT/AST), hyperglycemia, rash. Development discontinued. [Source: PMC, "Development and safety of PI3K inhibitors in cancer"]
- **Pictilisib (Genentech/Roche, GDC-0941)** — Pan-PI3K. Development halted after disappointing results in HR+/HER2- breast cancer. [Source: PMC, "Phosphoinositide 3-Kinase (PI3K) Inhibitors and Breast Cancer"]
- **Taselisib (Genentech/Roche, GDC-0032)** — Beta-sparing PI3K inhibitor. Failed due to toxicity. Novartis's Piqray was described as "something of a triumph" specifically because taselisib failed. [Source: Pharmaphorum]

### Dual PI3K/mTOR inhibitors that failed:
- **BEZ235/Dactolisib (Novartis)** — Dual PI3K/mTOR. Phase 1 in renal cell carcinoma terminated early due to frequent dose-limiting toxicities without objective responses. Phase 2 in pancreatic neuroendocrine tumors: 29 of 31 patients discontinued due to adverse events. [Source: PMC, "The PI3K pathway in human disease"]
- **Apitolisib/GDC-0980 (Genentech)** — Dual pan-PI3K/mTOR. Proved inferior to everolimus in Phase 2 renal cell carcinoma trial. More serious adverse events and discontinuations. [Source: PMC]

### PI3K inhibitors approved then withdrawn (hematologic):
- **Idelalisib/Zydelig (Gilead)** — PI3Kδ. Approved 2014 for CLL, SLL, FL. Indications for FL and SLL withdrawn. Infection rates nearly doubled, "unprecedented" worsened overall survival despite improved PFS. [Source: AJMC, BioSpace]
- **Duvelisib/Copiktra (Secura Bio)** — PI3Kγ/δ. FL indication voluntarily withdrawn. ODAC voted against retaining CLL/SLL indications. Fatal infections in 31% of patients. [Source: AJMC, Healio]
- **Umbralisib/Ukoniq (TG Therapeutics)** — PI3Kδ. Completely withdrawn from market (April 2022) after "imbalance" in overall survival. [Source: BioSpace, FDA]
- **Copanlisib/Aliqopa (Bayer)** — Pan-PI3K. Withdrew supplemental NDA for combination therapy. All FDA-approved PI3Kδ inhibitor indications for lymphoma have been withdrawn as of January 1, 2025. [Source: PMC, "Targeting PI3Kδ in cancer: a setback or the end"]

### Isoform-selective PI3Kα inhibitors (limited):
- **Alpelisib/Piqray (Novartis, 2019)** — PI3Kα-selective. Only works for PIK3CA-mutant patients (~40%). Revenue declining: $382M in 2025 (down 15% YoY, second straight year of decline). Novartis paying $2B ($3B with milestones) for replacement drug SNV4818. [Source: MedCity News, Pharmaphorum]
- **Inavolisib/Itovebi (Roche, 2024)** — PI3Kα. Also limited to PIK3CA-mutant patients. Projected peak sales $2.3B. [Source: Synapse/PatSnap]

### FDA's extraordinary statement:
Richard Pazdur, FDA Oncology Center of Excellence director: "The consistent findings of decrements in overall survival in six randomized trials, in the setting of an advantage or potential advantage in PFS, is **unprecedented in oncology**." [Source: BioSpace]

## REVTORPYK (Gedatolisib) — The Breakthrough

### Drug profile:
- Originally PKI-587, discovered at Pfizer
- Acquired and developed by Celcuity Inc. (Minneapolis)
- First-in-class: pan-PI3K (α, β, δ, γ) AND mTORC1/2 inhibitor
- FDA approved July 14, 2026

### VIKTORIA-1 Phase 3 trial results (PIK3CA wild-type cohort):
| Metric | Triplet (REVTORPYK + palbociclib + fulvestrant) | Doublet (REVTORPYK + fulvestrant) | Control (fulvestrant alone) |
|--------|------|---------|---------|
| Median PFS | 9.3 months | 7.4 months | 2.0 months |
| PFS improvement | +7.3 months | +5.4 months | — |
| Hazard ratio | 0.24 (p<0.0001) | 0.33 (p<0.0001) | — |
| Risk reduction | 76% | 67% | — |
| ORR | 32% | 28% | 1% |
| Median DOR | 17.5 months | 12.0 months | N/A (1 response) |

### Safety profile:
- Stomatitis: 72% (triplet, 22% Grade 3); 58% (doublet, 12% Grade 3)
- Rash: 30% (triplet); 40% (doublet)
- Hyperglycemia: 46% (triplet); 57% (doublet)
- Required steroid-containing mouthwash prophylaxis

### Commercial timeline:
- Launch expected late Q3 2026
- sNDA for PIK3CA-mutant patients (based on VIKTORIA-1 mutant cohort) planned Q3 2026
- VIKTORIA-2 ongoing: first-line treatment (two independent studies)

## Original Calculation: The "60% Gap" — Quantifying the Untreated Population

### Patient population math:
- ~310,000 new breast cancer cases/year in US (ACS 2024 projection)
- ~70% HR+/HER2- = ~217,000 new HR+/HER2- cases/year
- ~150,000+ women living with HR+/HER2- metastatic breast cancer in US (Lilly/SEER)
- Among metastatic breast cancer, 73% is HR+/HER2- (SEER 2010 data)
- ~60% of HR+/HER2- patients are PIK3CA wild-type (Celcuity, Anderson et al. 2020)
- Therefore: ~90,000 women living with HR+/HER2- mBC who are PIK3CA wild-type

### The gap before REVTORPYK:
- Alpelisib/Piqray: Only for PIK3CA-mutant patients (~40%)
- Inavolisib/Itovebi: Only for PIK3CA-mutant patients
- For the 60% wild-type majority: their best post-CDK4/6i option was fulvestrant alone
- Fulvestrant alone median PFS: just 2.0 months (VIKTORIA-1 control arm)
- That's patients watching their cancer progress within 8 weeks of starting treatment

### The PFS improvement calculation:
- Eligible 2nd-line+ patients per year (rough estimate): ~25,000-30,000 (subset of wild-type mBC patients who progress past CDK4/6 inhibitors)
- PFS gain per patient (triplet): 7.3 months
- At 25,000 patients/year: 25,000 × 7.3 = 182,500 patient-months = **~15,200 patient-years of progression-free survival gained per annual cohort**
- Before REVTORPYK, these patients had median PFS of 2.0 months — disease progressing within 60 days

### The response rate inversion:
- Control (fulvestrant): 1% ORR — literally 1 out of ~100 patients responded
- REVTORPYK triplet: 32% ORR — 32x higher response rate
- This is one of the most dramatic response-rate differentials in recent oncology approvals

## The Competitive Landscape Irony

Novartis just paid $2B ($3B with milestones) for SNV4818 to replace alpelisib — which serves only the ~40% PIK3CA-mutant population. Meanwhile, Celcuity (market cap ~$4B pre-approval) just captured the larger 60% wild-type population that nobody else could treat. The larger market opened to the smaller company.

Roche's inavolisib projects $2.3B peak sales — but also only serves PIK3CA mutant patients.

## Strongest Counterargument

Overall survival data from VIKTORIA-1 is not yet mature. The PI3K class is haunted by a specific ghost: six prior randomized trials showed improved PFS but WORSENED overall survival. The FDA's own advisory committee called this "unprecedented in oncology." REVTORPYK's PFS numbers are dramatic, but the question that looms over every PI3K drug is whether PFS translates to OS or whether toxicity erases the benefit. The 72% stomatitis rate and 22% Grade 3 stomatitis are significant quality-of-life burdens. Until mature OS data are available (expected ~2027-2028), the PI3K curse cannot be declared broken.

## Limitations

1. OS data from VIKTORIA-1 not yet mature — this is the critical unknown given the PI3K class history
2. VIKTORIA-1 was an open-label trial (patients and doctors knew who got the drug), which can inflate PFS via assessment bias
3. Patient population estimate (~25,000/year eligible) is derived by applying prevalence percentages from different sources (SEER, ACS, Anderson et al.) rather than a single integrated dataset
4. We don't yet have pricing data — cost-per-QALY calculation is impossible until launch pricing is announced
5. Side effect burden is substantial (72% stomatitis), and real-world tolerability may differ from clinical trial settings
