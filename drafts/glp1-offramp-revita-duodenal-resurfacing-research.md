## Thesis
Fractyl Health's REMAIN-1 trial data (released July 15, 2026) shows a single outpatient endoscopic procedure (Revita DMR) can maintain 84% of GLP-1-induced weight loss at one year vs. 46% with sham — but the number comes from just 10 patients. The original contribution: running the break-even economics that nobody has published. If the math works, Revita threatens the $82 billion chronic-use GLP-1 industry model. If the sample size is too small to trust, it's the most dangerous number in obesity medicine.

## Kill Test — Novel Contribution
**Original calculation: The GLP-1 Discontinuation Cost Cascade**

The numbers nobody has assembled together:
- 12% of US adults currently on GLP-1s (KFF survey) ≈ 30 million people
- 70% discontinue within the first year (Fractyl/published literature)
- Weight regain after stopping: ~2/3 of lost weight within 1 year
- Annual cost of continuous GLP-1 therapy: $2,940/yr (Medicare, starting Jul 1 2026) to $13,000-17,000/yr (commercial)
- Combined Lilly + Novo GLP-1 revenue 2026: ~$84.5B ($45B tirzepatide + $39.5B semaglutide)

**The break-even math:**
A single Revita procedure (est. $5,000-15,000 based on comparable outpatient endoscopy) vs. cumulative chronic GLP-1 costs:
- At Medicare rate ($2,940/yr): break-even in 1.7-5.1 years
- At commercial rate (~$13,000/yr): break-even in 4.6-13.8 months
- At list price ($15,600/yr Wegovy): break-even in 3.8-11.5 months

**The addressable disruption:**
- 21 million annual GLP-1 discontinuers × even 5% Revita adoption = 1.05 million procedures/year
- At $10,000/procedure = $10.5B market from nothing
- Captured directly from Lilly/Novo recurring revenue

**CRITICAL CAVEAT (must be foregrounded):**
- The 84% number: N=10 patients (optimized subgroup, complete ablation >14cm + high weight loss ≥17.5%)
- Full mITT population (N=45): 40% reduction in weight regain vs. sham — still significant but less dramatic
- 73% responder rate (mITT) vs. 91% (complete ablation) meeting FDA >50% performance goal
- No procedure-related SAEs, but long-term durability beyond 1 year unknown
- FDA De Novo submission expected Q4 2026

## Primary Sources

### 1. Fractyl Health Press Release (July 15, 2026)
- REMAIN-1 Midpoint Cohort one-year results
- Revita DMR System: outpatient endoscopic procedure, ablates duodenal mucosa (first ~25cm of small intestine beyond stomach)
- Mechanism: chronic high-fat/high-sugar diets alter duodenal tissue → metabolic dysfunction. Revita ablates damaged tissue → healthier mucosa regenerates
- Complete duodenal ablation (>14cm): 81% weight loss maintained at 1 year (4.8% regain vs. 13.0% sham)
- Optimized population (>14cm + ≥17.5% GLP-1 weight loss): 84% maintained (4.1% regain vs. 13.5% sham)

## Strongest Counterargument
Fractyl's 84% headline number is built on 10 patients in an optimized subgroup selected after the trial finished. The mITT population — which is what the FDA will actually evaluate — showed 40% reduction in weight regain, meaningful but far less dramatic. Pharma has a long history of companies cherry-picking their best subgroup to inflate results. The pivotal trial data (Q4 2026) will determine whether this is real or whether Fractyl is selling a number it can't reproduce at scale.

## Limitations Acknowledgment
- Single trial, small sample (N=45 mITT, N=10 in optimized subgroup)
- Post-hoc subgroup selection (complete ablation + high weight loss) inflates apparent effect
- No data beyond 1 year
- Procedure cost unknown pre-approval — all break-even math uses estimates
- Comparator was sham procedure, not alternative interventions (e.g., lifestyle modification, bariatric surgery)
- Trial excluded people with diabetes — the largest GLP-1 user population
