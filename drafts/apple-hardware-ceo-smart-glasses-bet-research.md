## Novel Analysis

### 1. Apple's "Late Entry" Timeline (years after category creator)
| Product | Category Created | Apple Entry | Gap (years) | Outcome |
|---------|-----------------|-------------|-------------|---------|
| iPod (2001) | Diamond Rio (1998) | 2001 | 3 | Dominated, 70%+ share |
| iPhone (2007) | PDA phones (2000) | 2007 | 7 | Created smartphone era |
| iPad (2010) | Tablet PCs (2002) | 2010 | 8 | Defined tablet market |
| Apple Watch (2015) | Pebble (2012) | 2015 | 3 | 50%+ smartwatch share |
| AirPods (2016) | Truly wireless (2014) | 2016 | 2 | Dominated category |
| Vision Pro (2024) | VR headsets (2016) | 2024 | 8 | Under 500K sold — failure |
| Smart Glasses (2027) | Google Glass (2013) | 2027 | 14* | TBD |
| Smart Glasses (2027) | Meta Ray-Ban (2023) | 2027 | 4 | TBD — matches pattern |

*Google Glass was premature. The real category began with Meta Ray-Ban in 2023. Gap of 4 years matches Apple Watch and AirPods pattern.

Average successful gap: 4.6 years. Smart glasses at 4 years (from Meta Ray-Ban) = right in the sweet spot.

### 2. Revenue Math: VR vs Smart Glasses
**Vision Pro path:**
- ~500K units × $3,499 = ~$1.75B lifetime revenue (2 years)
- Growth stalled, shelved successor

**Smart glasses path (conservative):**
- Year 1 (2027): 5M units × $399 = $2.0B
- Year 2 (2028): 12M units × $399 = $4.8B
- Year 3 (2029): 20M units × $399 = $8.0B
- 3-year total: $14.8B revenue (vs $1.75B from VP)

**Smart glasses path (aggressive, matching Meta's trajectory):**
- Year 1: 10M units × $399 = $4.0B
- Year 2: 25M units × $399 = $10.0B
- Year 3: 40M units × $399 = $16.0B
- Plus: Services/AI upsell per device

**Meta comparison:**
- 7M units in 2025 at ~$300 ASP = ~$2.1B (split with EssilorLuxottica)
- Targeting 20-30M annual capacity by 2027

## Strongest Counterargument
Meta has a 4-year head start, 9M units sold, manufacturing partnerships with EssilorLuxottica (world's largest eyewear company), and is already iterating on Gen 2 + display variants. Apple's late entries succeed in NEW categories (smartphones, tablets) where incumbents are weak. But Meta is a strong incumbent with deep AI capabilities, fashion partnerships, and a rapidly scaling supply chain. This is more like Apple Watch vs Fitbit than iPhone vs Nokia.
