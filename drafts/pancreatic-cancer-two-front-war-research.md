# Research: Pancreatic Cancer Two-Front War

## Core Thesis
Two independent breakthroughs — an AI model that detects pancreatic cancer up to 3 years before clinical diagnosis, and a vaccine that prevents pre-cancerous lesions from progressing — are converging on the same disease from opposite directions. Together they create a detect-then-prevent pipeline that could fundamentally alter the math of pancreatic cancer, the deadliest of all major cancers.

## Primary Sources

### Source 1: Mayo Clinic REDMOD AI Model
- **Paper:** Published in *Gut* (BMJ), April 2026
- **URL:** https://newsnetwork.mayoclinic.org/discussion/mayo-clinic-ai-detects-pancreatic-cancer-up-to-3-years-before-diagnosis-in-landmark-validation-study/
- **Senior author:** Dr. Ajit Goenka, Mayo Clinic radiologist and nuclear medicine specialist
- **Key findings:**
  - REDMOD (Radiomics-based Early Detection Model) analyzes routine abdominal CT scans
  - Identified 73% of prediagnostic cancers at median 16 months before diagnosis
  - Nearly double the detection rate of specialists reviewing same scans without AI
  - At 2+ years before diagnosis: nearly 3x more early cancers detected
  - Validated on nearly 2,000 CT scans from multiple institutions
  - Specificity: 81% multi-institutional, 87.5% on independent NIH dataset
  - 90-92% concordance across serial scans in same patient
  - Designed for high-risk patients (e.g., new-onset diabetes)
  - Prospective study (AI-PACED) now underway

### Source 2: Johns Hopkins mKRAS Vaccine
- **Paper:** Published in *Cancer Discovery*, July 2026
- **URL:** https://www.the-scientist.com/a-pancreatic-cancer-vaccine-shows-promise-in-a-phase-1-clinical-trial-74762
- **Lead authors:** Elizabeth Jaffee and Neeha Zaidi, Johns Hopkins
- **Key findings:**
  - Peptide-based vaccine targeting 6 most common mutant KRAS (mKRAS) variants
  - Phase 1 trial: 20 patients with genetic predisposition + confirmed pancreatic lesions
  - 18.2-fold increase in T cell responses against mKRAS antigens
  - 16.5-month median follow-up: ZERO patients progressed to cancer
  - 3 patients had cysts completely resolve, 3 more regressed
  - Unvaccinated comparison cohort showed more progression
  - Both CD4+ and CD8+ T cell responses, including memory phenotypes
  - Only mild side effects (fatigue, chills, flu-like symptoms)
  - "First proof of concept for vaccines for interception of pancreatic cancer in human patients"
  - COI: Jaffee and Zaidi are founders/consultants of Adventris Pharmaceuticals

### Source 3: Epidemiological Data (ACS Cancer Statistics 2026)
- **Published:** CA: A Cancer Journal for Clinicians, January 2026
- **URL:** https://acsjournals.onlinelibrary.wiley.com/
- 67,530 new pancreatic cancer diagnoses in 2026
- 52,740 deaths from pancreatic cancer in 2026
- Mortality-to-incidence ratio: 0.867 (78% of patients die)
- 3rd leading cause of cancer death in US
- Projected to become 2nd by 2030
- 85%+ diagnosed after spread
- 5-year survival: 13% overall; 44% if caught before spread; 3.2% Stage IV
- Only 15-20% of tumors are resectable at diagnosis
- NIH funding: $440M ($8,945 per death vs. $69,800 per breast cancer death)

### Source 4: Stage-by-Stage Survival (Japan data, peer-reviewed)
- Stage I: 42.9% five-year survival
- Stage II: 16.8%
- Stage III: 7.4%
- Stage IV: 1.5%
- Only 1.1% diagnosed at Stage 0; 24.5% at Stage I

### Source 5: Johns Hopkins Prognosis Data
- Stage IV: 3.2% five-year survival, average 1 year post-diagnosis
- If caught early: up to 10% become disease-free
- Resected patients with adjuvant chemo: 50% five-year survival
- Average post-resection survival: 2.5 years

## Original Calculations

### Calculation 1: Stage-Shift Lives Saved
- 67,530 diagnosed annually
- Current: 85% diagnosed Stage III/IV → ~57,400 late-stage
- REDMOD at 73% detection rate on high-risk scans
- If REDMOD were applied to the ~10% of diagnoses that come from new-onset diabetes screening CTs (~6,750 patients):
  - 73% flagged = ~4,928 caught earlier
  - Even if only 50% of those shift from Stage IV to Stage I/II (resectable):
    - ~2,464 patients gain access to surgery
    - 44% five-year survival vs. 3.2% → net survival gain: ~40.8 percentage points
    - That's ~1,005 additional lives saved per year from just one high-risk subgroup

### Calculation 2: KRAS Coverage
- KRAS mutations present in ~90% of PDAC tumors
- Vaccine targets 6 most common mKRAS variants
- Assuming these 6 variants cover ~80% of KRAS-mutated PDAC
- Theoretically addressable population: 90% × 80% = ~72% of all PDAC
- Of 67,530 annual US cases: ~48,600 could theoretically benefit

### Calculation 3: NIH Funding Disparity
- Pancreatic cancer: $440M NIH funding / 52,740 deaths = $8,945 per death
- Breast cancer: $1.58B / 22,606 deaths = $69,800 per death
- Prostate cancer: $663M / 5,219 deaths = $126,992 per death
- Pancreatic cancer gets 7.8× less per death than breast cancer and 14.2× less than prostate
- Despite having the highest mortality-to-incidence ratio (0.867) of any major cancer
