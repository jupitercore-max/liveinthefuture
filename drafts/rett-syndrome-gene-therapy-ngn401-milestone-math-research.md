# Research: Neurogene NGN-401 Rett Syndrome Gene Therapy — The Milestone Math

## Core Story
Neurogene (NASDAQ: NGNE) reported 30-month follow-up data from its Phase 1/2 trial of NGN-401 gene therapy for Rett syndrome on June 29, 2026. 10 participants gained 47 developmental milestones total (avg 4.7 per participant), with 100% response rate, no plateau, no milestone loss. The registrational trial (Embolden) has completed dosing with zero treatment-related SAEs or DLTs as of June 16, 2026.

## Primary Sources

### 1. Neurogene Press Release (June 29, 2026) — BusinessWire
URL: https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20260629170706/en/Neurogene-Reports-Positive-Long-term-Clinical-Data-from-Phase-12-Trial-of-NGN-401-Gene-Therapy-for-Rett-Syndrome

Key data points:
- N=10 participants, follow-up 12-30 months post-treatment
- 47 total developmental milestones gained (avg 4.7 per participant)
- 100% participants improved on CGI-I and gained ≥1 milestone
- ALL participants gained ≥1 milestone in the PAST 12 MONTHS (no plateau)
- Milestones gained in progressive, developmentally ordered stepwise sequence → "restart of developmental progression"
- Median time to first improvement: 2 months post-treatment
- Milestone gains ACCELERATING: 95% increase from 6→12 months, 147% increase from 6→≥12 months
- 7/10 participants gained ≥2 milestones across ≥2 core Rett domains (hand function, gross motor, communication)
- Both pediatric AND adolescent/adult participants responded
- Safety: N=35 at 1E15 vg dose, generally well-tolerated
- All treatment-related AEs mild (Grade 1) or moderate (Grade 2)
- Zero treatment-related SAEs since Oct 2025
- Embolden registrational trial: dosing complete, zero SAEs/DLTs, topline data 2H 2027

### 2. Natural History of Rett Syndrome — Journal of Neurodevelopmental Disorders (2026)
URL: https://link.springer.com/article/10.1186/s11689-026-09680-6
DOI: 10.1186/s11689-026-09680-6 (PMID 41820836)

N=1,228 females with Classic RTT. Key findings:
- Skill acquisition peaks before age 2, rarely occurs after age 6
- After age 6, the incidence of gaining or regaining skills is essentially ZERO
- Regain of lost skills was infrequent (<30% for most domains)
- Regain occurred mainly before age 6 and within 2 years of loss
- Fine motor and expressive language loss had highest frequency
- Gross motor loss continued beyond age 6 (unique among domains)

THIS IS THE CRITICAL COMPARISON: In the natural history, gaining milestones after age 6 essentially doesn't happen. NGN-401 showed adolescent/adult participants GAINING new milestones — something the 1,228-patient natural history says shouldn't occur.

### 3. EXACT Technology — Self-regulating MECP2 gene therapy (Science Translational Medicine)
PMID: 40173263

The MECP2 dosage problem:
- Too little MECP2 → Rett syndrome
- Too much MECP2 → MECP2 duplication syndrome (equally devastating)
- This makes MECP2 gene therapy uniquely dangerous among gene therapies
- NGN-401 uses EXACT (Expression Attenuation via Construct Tuning): microRNA-based feed-forward loop that auto-regulates transgene expression
- Every cell that expresses the therapeutic MECP2 also expresses a microRNA that caps MECP2 levels — preventing toxic overexpression
- In mice: NGN-401 extended survival from ~10 weeks to ~37 weeks in Mecp2-null males
- Wild-type females tolerated it without toxicity (critical: proves overexpression protection works)

### 4. Competitor: TSHA-102 (Taysha Gene Therapies)
- Also AAV9-based, uses miRARE regulatory element
- Uses MINI-MECP2 (truncated version), not full-length
- Intrathecal delivery (spinal fluid), not intracerebroventricular (ICV)
- Extended knockout mouse survival by 56%
- In Phase 1/2 clinical trials (REVEAL Adult + pediatric)
- Less clinical data publicly available compared to NGN-401

### 5. Rett Syndrome Epidemiology
- Prevalence: ~1 in 10,000-15,000 females
- ~350,000 patients worldwide
- ~25,000 in US + Europe
- Caused by mutations in MECP2 gene on X chromosome
- Almost exclusively affects females (males with MECP2 mutations typically die in infancy)
- No FDA-approved disease-modifying treatment exists

## Original Calculations

### 1. Milestone Acceleration Rate
From Neurogene data:
- At 6 months: X milestones
- At 12 months: 1.95X milestones (95% increase)
- At ≥12 months: 2.47X milestones (147% increase from 6-month)

If we assume linear growth from treatment, you'd expect a ~100% increase from 6→12 months (doubling the time). The 95% increase is consistent with linear.

But the 147% increase from 6→≥12 months (some participants now at 30 months) suggests ACCELERATION — milestones accumulate faster over time, not at a constant rate. This is the "restart of development" claim in quantitative form.

### 2. Natural History Comparison — The After-Six Wall
From the 1,228-patient natural history study:
- After age 6, the one-year incidence of gaining any of 51 developmental skills drops to near zero
- NGN-401 trial included adolescent/adult participants who gained milestones
- This is the strongest evidence that the gene therapy is doing something the disease NEVER does naturally — enabling post-regression skill acquisition in patients well past the age where natural gains cease

### 3. Safety Math
- N=35 patients dosed at therapeutic level (1E15 vg)
- 0 treatment-related SAEs
- 0 dose-limiting toxicities
- Upper bound of 95% CI for SAE rate: 1 - 0.05^(1/35) = ~8.2%
- This means: if the true SAE rate were >8.2%, there's a >95% chance we'd have seen at least one SAE by now

### 4. Market/Access Calculation
- ~25,000 patients in US + Europe
- Gene therapy pricing precedent: Zolgensma (SMA) = $2.1M, Hemgenix (hemophilia B) = $3.5M
- At $2M per treatment × 25,000 patients = $50B total addressable market
- But realistic penetration: maybe 30-50% over 10 years = $15-25B
