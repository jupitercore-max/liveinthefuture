# Research: Aurora's Driverless Freight Economics

## Topic
Aurora Innovation's autonomous trucking scaling — from 10 driverless trucks to 200+ by year-end 2026 — and the unit economics math that explains why customers keep signing up despite Aurora's staggering $272 loss for every $1 of revenue.

## Category
🚗 Transport — Autonomous Trucking

## Journalist
Kai Nakamura — Transport beat

## Original Calculation: The Loss-to-Revenue Decomposition
Aurora lost $816M (TTM net loss) on $3M revenue = $272 loss per $1 earned.
At 200 trucks and projected $80M revenue, the ratio drops to ~$10:$1.
At 1,250 trucks (back-of-envelope break-even), the ratio hits ~$1:$1.

Key insight: The $272:$1 ratio is almost entirely R&D fixed cost that amortizes over fleet size. Each incremental truck added costs far less than the marginal revenue it generates.

## Utilization Advantage Calculation
Human OTR truck:
- FMCSA HOS: 11-hour daily driving limit, 14-hour duty window
- Practical: ~500 revenue miles/day, ~120,000 miles/year
- Driver pay: $0.60/mile × 120K miles = ~$72,000-$90,000/year
- Total operating cost: $1.66/mile (Geotab 2026 data) = ~$199,200/year
- Deadhead rate: 16.7% of all miles (ATRI 2026), costing ~$37,742/year wasted

Aurora driverless truck:
- Operating: ~20 hours/day (4 hours for fueling, maintenance, terminal transfer)
- Potential: ~1,000 revenue miles/day, ~250,000-300,000 miles/year
- No driver wages, no HOS compliance costs
- Utilization multiplier: 2.0-2.5× more revenue-miles per asset
- Revenue per truck at 200 fleet: $80M / 200 = $400,000/year

## Counterargument
$5.4B in accumulated losses. The R&D cost per driverless mile is ~$3,264 ($816M ÷ 250K miles). Aurora's gross margin is -467% — it costs $6 to deliver $1 of service. Even at projected 200 trucks and $80M revenue, operating losses will dwarf revenue. The bet is that fixed R&D costs flatten while fleet revenue scales linearly — but that bet requires flawless execution on hardware industrialization, regulatory expansion, and customer acquisition simultaneously. Every prior autonomous vehicle company (Argo AI, Embark, TuSimple) that attempted this failed.

## Limitations
- Aurora does not disclose per-truck operating costs or marginal cost per mile
- The $80M revenue projection for 2026 is management guidance, not a confirmed result
- Hirschbach deal is an MOU, not a binding contract
- Safety record is over 250K miles; statistically significant assessment requires millions
- Break-even calculation assumes fixed costs flatten at ~$500M/year, which is an assumption
