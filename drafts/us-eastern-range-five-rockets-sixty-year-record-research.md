# Research: US Eastern Range Breaks 60-Year Launch Record — Five Unique Rockets in One Month

## Story Thesis
In April 2026, the US Eastern Range (Cape Canaveral) supported five unique orbital launch vehicles in a single calendar month for the first time since the Apollo era, breaking a 60-year record. The US launched 18 rockets total that month. The original calculation: comparing cost-per-kg-to-orbit across all five vehicle types reveals the staggering economic diversification of America's launch industry, and estimating total mass delivered to orbit in April 2026 vs. the entire record-setting months of 1965/1966.

### Historical Comparison
- Feb 1965: 4 unique vehicles (Delta C, Titan 3A, Saturn 1, Atlas Agena) — ~3-5 launches total
- July 1966: 4 unique vehicles (Delta E1, Saturn 1B, Atlas-Agena, Titan 2) — ~4-5 launches
- April 2026: 5 unique vehicles, 18 total US launches

### Mass to Orbit (estimated)
- April 2026 from Eastern Range alone: ~10 Falcon 9 × 17t + 2 Atlas V × 16.5t + 1 FH × 6.4t + 1 New Glenn × 5t + 1 SLS × 27t = ~241 metric tons (rough)
- Entire year 1965 from US: ~56 orbital launches, estimated total mass ~150-200 metric tons
- April 2026 alone likely exceeded the mass-to-orbit of most individual years in the 1960s
