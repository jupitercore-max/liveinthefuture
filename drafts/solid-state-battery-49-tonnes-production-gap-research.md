# Research Notes: Solid-State Battery 49-Tonne Production Gap

## Thesis
Despite prototype solid-state batteries achieving extraordinary range (833 miles in a Mercedes EQS), the global supply chain for sulfide solid electrolyte — the critical material enabling the highest-performance SSBs — produced only 49 tonnes in H1 2026. That's enough for roughly 1,300 EVs. Global EV production will hit ~18-20 million this year. The gap between what SSBs can do and what the supply chain can deliver is 4 orders of magnitude.

## Original Contribution: Three Calculations

### 1. The Sulfide Electrolyte Bottleneck (per-vehicle math)
- H1 2026 global sulfide electrolyte production: 49 tonnes (SMM)
- Sulfide SSB cells use ~15-20% electrolyte by weight
- At 400 Wh/kg cell energy density, a 75 kWh pack = ~187.5 kg cells
- Electrolyte per pack at 20% share: ~37.5 kg
- 49,000 kg / 37.5 kg = **~1,307 EVs in six months**
- Full-year 2026 estimate (130-170 tonnes): **~3,500-4,500 EVs**
- Global EV production 2026: ~18-20 million
- SSB electrolyte covers: **0.02% of global EV production**

### 2. Cost-Per-Mile-of-Range Break-Even
- Current Li-ion EQS: 118 kWh, 390 mi EPA → 3.31 mi/kWh
- Pack cost at $99/kWh (BNEF BEV avg 2025): $11,682
- Cost per mile of range: **$29.95/mi**
- SSB energy density advantage: ~1.6× (400 vs 250 Wh/kg NMC)
- For same range, SSB pack needs only 62.5% the capacity
- Break-even SSB cell price: $99 × 1.6 = **$158/kWh**
- Current SSB cell cost: ¥3-5/Wh = **$410-690/kWh** (2.6-4.4× above break-even)
- Gotion's 2027 semi-solid target: ¥1/Wh = ~$140/kWh → below break-even

### 3. China's Production Lead Quantified
- Chinese SSB capacity commissioned/online H1 2026: ~10.7 GWh (Qingtao 3.5, Xinjie 2, Jinyu 1.2, Enli 2, Cangzhou ~2)
- Under construction China: 50+ GWh (Guoxiang 20, Qingtao 20, Jinheng 10, etc.)
- US/EU/Japan/Korea: QuantumScape EagleLine (pre-production), SolidPower (pilot installation), ProLogium Dunkirk (2028 target), SK On Daejeon (pilot), Toyota Aichi (pilot)
- Commissioned production capacity outside China: effectively 0 GWh at scale
- China leads by ~2 years on volume production timeline per SMM

## Strongest Counterargument
Oxide electrolyte production is 28× larger than sulfide (1,380 tonnes vs 49 tonnes in H1 2026). The sulfide bottleneck is real but sulfide isn't the only path. Semi-solid batteries using oxide or polymer electrolytes with some liquid are already in commercial vehicles (SAIC/Qingtao MG4X deliveries started). The industry doesn't need to solve the sulfide problem to put solid-state batteries in cars.

## Limitations
- Cost-per-kWh figures for SSB cells are industry estimates, not audited production costs
- The 833-mile Mercedes prototype likely used aerodynamic and efficiency optimizations beyond just the battery swap
- SMM data heavily weighted toward Chinese market; some Western production may be underreported
- "Semi-solid" is a spectrum — some products marketed as semi-solid contain 5% liquid, others 40%
- Yuan-to-dollar conversions use mid-July 2026 rate (~¥7.25/$1)
