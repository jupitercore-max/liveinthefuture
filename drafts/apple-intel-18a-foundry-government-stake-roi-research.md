# Research: Apple-Intel 18A-P Foundry Partnership + Government Stake ROI

## Core Story
- **June 18, 2026:** Trump announces on Truth Social that Apple has agreed to work with Intel to design and manufacture chips in the United States
- **June 17, 2026:** Intel announced 18A-P entered "risk production" at VLSI Symposium in Honolulu
- Intel stock up 9.2% premarket on Apple news (Barrons), extending ~400%+ gain over 12 months
- Preliminary deal reported by WSJ in May 2026 after 1+ year of discussions

## Government Stake Details
- **August 22, 2025:** Trump administration converted $8.87B in CHIPS Act grants to equity
- 433.3M shares at $20.47/share = 9.9% stake (Motley Fool, CoinDesk)
- Government also holds warrants for additional 5% at $20/share (deep in money)
- **Condition:** Intel must own 51%+ of foundry for 5 years, or government can exercise additional 5%
- Intel CFO Zinsner: "I don't think there's a high likelihood that we would take our stake below 50 percent"
- **Original CHIPS Act grants** were supposed to be subsidies, not equity — legally dubious conversion (Motley Fool)

## Government ROI Calculation (Original Analysis)
- Shares: 433.3M
- Buy price: $20.47/share ($8.87B total)
- Intel closing price June 17: ~$121/share (pre-Apple news)
- June 18 premarket (post-Apple news): up 9.2% → ~$132
- Value at $121: 433.3M × $121 = $52.4B → ROI: 491% in 10 months
- Value at $132: 433.3M × $132 = $57.2B → ROI: 545% in 10 months
- Warrant value (5%, ~216.6M shares at $20 strike, current ~$121): intrinsic value = $21.9B
- **Total position including warrants at $121:** ~$74.3B
- Trump claimed "more than $60 billion" — conservative vs the full warrant-inclusive number

- **Intel stake: $8.87B → $52-60B in 10 months = 491-577% return**

- This is the single most profitable government equity investment in U.S. history by return percentage

## Intel 18A/18A-P Technical Details
- **18A yields:** ~55% as of mid-2025, improving 7-8% monthly (TrendForce via ainvest)
- From 10% early 2025 → 55% mid-2025 → likely higher now (June 2026)
- **18A-P improvements over 18A:** 9% faster at same power OR 18% less power at same speed
- **18A-P features:** "Power Boost" dual-contact transistor (industry first), subtractive Ru interconnects with airgap (35% capacitance reduction, 50% lower via resistance)
- **18A performance:** TechInsights score 2.53 vs TSMC N2 2.27 vs Samsung SF2 2.19
- **18A density:** 238 MTr/mm² vs TSMC N2 313 MTr/mm² (HD), but BSPDN means effective density is closer
- **18A BSPDN:** First commercial node with backside power delivery — higher cost but better signal routing
- **Diamond Rapids:** Next-gen Xeon CPUs on 18A-P (announced Computex 2026)

## TSMC Comparison
- TSMC N2 yields: ~65% as of mid-2025
- TSMC N2 density: 313 MTr/mm² (HD)
- TSMC 2024 capex: >$35B (more than 2x Intel's)
- TSMC market cap: ~$62.2T (TWD) ≈ $1.9T USD
- Apple iPhone 17 constrained by TSMC capacity (Tim Cook Q1 2026 earnings call)
- TSMC 1.4nm (A14) planned for 2028 — 15% better performance or 30% power savings vs N2

## Apple Deal Specifics
- Apple M7 processor likely first test on Intel 18A-P (Counterpoint Research)
- For Mac and iPad devices (NOT iPhone — iPhones stay TSMC for now)
- iPhone 18 Pro → TSMC 2nm (Sept 2026)
- 2028 iPhones → TSMC 1.4nm
- Intel would make ARM-based chips from Apple designs
- "Yield matters more than nodes. Matching TSMC's manufacturing consistency will be the make-or-break factor." — Counterpoint Research

## Strongest Counterargument
- Yield gap is real: Intel 55% vs TSMC 65% as of mid-2025
- Apple is HEDGING, not switching — TSMC remains primary
- Google's 3M TPU order doesn't start until 2028 (2 years to prove yields)
- Intel Foundry still losing $2.4B/quarter
- Government converted grants to equity "after the fact" — dilutive and legally dubious
- Intel stock at 400%+ gain may already price in the optimism
