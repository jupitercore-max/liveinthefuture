# Research Notes: Agility Robotics IPO — First Publicly Traded Humanoid Robot Company

## Core Event
- Agility Robotics announced June 24, 2026 a $2.5B SPAC merger with Churchill Capital Corp XI (CCXI)
- First pure-play humanoid robotics company to be publicly listed in the US
- Ticker: AGLT (exchange TBD)
- Expected close: Q4 2026

## Deal Structure
- $620M+ gross proceeds
  - $420M cash from Churchill XI
  - ~$200M PIPE (private investment in public equity) led by Foxconn at $10/share
- Pre-money equity value: $2.5B
- CCXI shares jumped ~29% briefly on news, settled at +15%

## Company Profile
- Founded 2015, spin-off from Oregon State University (Dynamic Robotics Lab)
- Co-founders: Johnathan Hurst (chief robot officer), Damion Shelton, Mikhail Jones
- CEO: Peggy Johnson (ex-Microsoft Corp VP, ex-MagicLeap CEO)
- HQ: Salem, Oregon
- Flagship: Digit — bipedal humanoid robot for warehouse/logistics
- Prior funding: $641M+ private, including $400M Series C-3 (June 2025, led by Humanoid Global + Nvidia)
- Investors: Nvidia, Amazon, SoftBank Vision Fund 2, Foxconn, DCVC, Schaeffler, Playground Global

## Operational Track Record
- 65,000+ hours in real workplaces (most for any commercial humanoid)
- 9 active customer sites
- Customers: Schaeffler, GXO, Toyota Motor Manufacturing Canada, Mercado Libre, Amazon
- RoboFab facility: 70,000 sqft in Salem, OR — capacity 10,000 robots/year
- World's first full-scale humanoid robot factory (opened 2024)
- Digit capabilities: walks, lifts 55 lbs, reaches shelves, navigates around spaces

## Order Pipeline
- $300M+ multi-year contracted orders for Digit v5 (next-generation)
- 30+ potential customers evaluating large-scale deployments
- Digit v5: finer dexterity, higher safety standards, "cooperatively safe" design
- Company estimates $1T addressable market

## Economics Analysis (ORIGINAL)

### 1. Valuation per deployed hour
- $2.5B valuation / 65,000 hours = $38,462 per hour of proven autonomous work
- For comparison, Tesla's 2012 IPO valued the company at ~$1.7B with ~2,650 cars produced — roughly $641K per car delivered
- Early-stage premium is the norm; the question is whether the deployed hours scale

### 2. Order book revenue multiple
- $2.5B / $300M multi-year orders = ~8.3x forward revenue (if recognized evenly over 3 years = ~25x annual revenue)
- Robotics SPAC comps historically 5-15x forward revenue
- But $300M backlog is just secured orders; 30+ pipeline customers not yet contracted

### 3. Unit economics model
Using CleanTechnica methodology + BLS data:
- Assume Digit unit cost: ~$100K-$250K (industry estimates; Agility has not disclosed)
- Let's use $150K as midpoint estimate
- Warehouse worker: $28/hr fully burdened (BLS median $19/hr + 47% benefits loading)
- Robot operating hours: 16 hrs/day × 250 days = 4,000 hrs/yr (conservative, dual-shift)
- Robot throughput: 60 tote moves/hr (Siemens benchmark for HMND 01, conservative for Digit)
- Human throughput: 200 units/hr (industry average)
- Robot effective throughput at 90% uptime: 54 moves/hr

Annual costs:
- Human: $28/hr × 2,000 hrs/yr = $56,000/worker
- Robot (amortized over 5 years): $150,000/5 = $30,000/yr capital + ~$10,000 maintenance/energy/supervision = $40,000/yr
- Robot cost per operating hour: $40,000 / 4,000 hrs = $10/hr

But: robot at 54 moves/hr vs human at 200 units/hr means 1 robot ≈ 0.27 human-equivalents
- Adjusted robot cost per human-equivalent hour: $10/hr / 0.27 = $37/hr
- WORSE than human labor at $28/hr

The break-even requires either:
a) Robot throughput reaching ~113 moves/hr at 90% uptime (vs current 60)
b) Robot capital cost dropping below ~$80K
c) Running robots 3 shifts (6,000 hrs/yr): $150K/5yr + $15K = $45K/yr, $7.50/hr, adjusted = $27.78/hr ≈ PARITY

### 4. The real story
Current humanoid economics DON'T work at today's throughput levels. The $2.5B valuation is betting on the trajectory:
- Gen 1 Digit: 60 moves/hr → Gen 5 target likely 150+ moves/hr
- Hardware costs following learning curve: ~15-20% cost decline per doubling of production
- At 10,000 units/yr: cost could reach $60-80K range in 3-5 years
