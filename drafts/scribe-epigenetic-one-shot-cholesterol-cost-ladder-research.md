# Research: Scribe Therapeutics — One-Shot Epigenetic Cholesterol Editing Cost Ladder

## Story Angle
Scribe Therapeutics IPO'd July 24, 2026 (SCTX, Nasdaq) at $15/share, raising $129M. First gene editing biotech IPO in 2+ years. Lead candidate STX-1150 uses epigenetic silencing (ELXR technology) to repress PCSK9 without cutting DNA. This comes 8 days after FDA approved Merck's Lipfendra (enlicitide), the first oral PCSK9 inhibitor ($315/month = $3,780/year).

## KILL TEST — Original Analysis: Cost-Per-Decade Ladder
Nobody has built a complete cost-per-decade comparison across all 5 generations of PCSK9-targeting cholesterol therapy:

| Approach | Drug/Company | Dosing | Annual Cost (list) | 10-Year Cost | LDL Reduction | Status |
|----------|-------------|--------|-------------------|-------------|---------------|--------|
| Generic statin | Atorvastatin | Daily pill | ~$48/yr | $480 | ~50% | Gold standard since 1987 |
| PCSK9 antibody injection | Repatha (Amgen) | Every 2 weeks | ~$5,850/yr | $58,500 | ~60% | Approved 2015 |
| siRNA injection | Leqvio (Novartis) | Every 6 months | ~$6,500/yr | $65,000 | ~50% | Approved 2021 |
| Oral PCSK9 inhibitor | Lipfendra (Merck) | Daily pill | ~$3,780/yr | $37,800 | 56-59% | Approved Jul 16 2026 |
| Epigenetic silencing | STX-1150 (Scribe) | Single infusion | TBD (est $50K-200K one-time) | $50K-200K | >50% (primate) | Phase 1, data H1 2027 |
| DNA base editing | Verve/Lilly | Single infusion | TBD | TBD | TBD | Phase 1 |
| DNA cutting (CRISPR) | CRISPR Therapeutics | Single infusion | TBD | TBD | ~50% (primate) | Phase 1 |

KEY INSIGHT: If Scribe's epigenetic edit lasts 10+ years from one infusion (primate data shows 2+ years and counting), the break-even vs. Lipfendra is $37,800. Vs. Repatha it's $58,500. Any one-time treatment priced below those thresholds wins on pure cost over a decade.

But 93 million Americans have elevated LDL. At $3,780/year, treating all of them with Lipfendra = $351 billion/year. A one-shot at $100K for 93M people = $9.3 trillion. The math only works if you target the ~8.5M statin-intolerant or statin-inadequate patients.

## Key Facts
- Scribe IPO: $15/share, raised $129M. Market cap ~$235M fully diluted. Ticker SCTX. Priced Jul 23, trading Jul 24.
- Stock jumped ~70% on first day, settled to ~$18.75 by Monday Jul 28.
- Founded 2017 by Benjamin Oakes from Jennifer Doudna's lab (Nobel laureate). Based in Alameda, CA.
- ELXR technology: epigenetic silencing via CasX enzyme. Does NOT cut DNA. Potentially reversible. Durable (2+ years in primates).
- XE technology: gene editing (DNA cutting) for Lp(a) and triglycerides. Phase 1 trials 2027-2028.
- Lilly owns ~10.9% post-IPO. Sanofi bought $7.5M concurrent private placement. Sanofi deal worth potentially $1B+ for NK cell therapies.
- $36M collaboration revenue in 12 months ended March 2026.
- Lipfendra (Merck): approved Jul 16 2026. First oral PCSK9. $315/month (~$3,780/yr). Daily pill. LDL reduction 56-59%.
- Repatha (Amgen): ~$5,850/year. Injection every 2 weeks.
- Leqvio (Novartis): ~$6,500/year. siRNA injection every 6 months.
- Analyst consensus: Lipfendra peak sales $5B/year.
