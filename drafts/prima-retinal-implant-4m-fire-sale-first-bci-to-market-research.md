# Research Notes: PRIMA Retinal Implant — $4M Fire Sale to First BCI to Market

## Core Story
Science Corporation, founded by Neuralink co-founder Max Hodak, bought the PRIMA retinal implant technology from bankrupt French company Pixium Vision for ~€4 million in 2024. With PRIMA, Science Corp is now on track to become the first BCI company to bring a product to market (CE mark expected mid-2026 in Europe), valued at $1.5 billion after a $230M Series C.

## Key Data Points

### Clinical Trial (NEJM, Oct 20, 2025)
- Study: PRIMAvera, open-label, multicenter, single-group, baseline-controlled
- Paper: "Subretinal Photovoltaic Implant to Restore Vision in Geographic Atrophy Due to AMD"
- PMID: 41124203, DOI: 10.1056/NEJMoa2501396
- Led by Prof. Frank Holz, University Hospital of Bonn
- 38 patients enrolled at 17 hospital sites in 5 European countries
- 32 completed 12-month follow-up (3 died, 1 withdrew, 2 unavailable)
- **Primary endpoint met:** 81% (26/32) showed ≥0.2 logMAR improvement (P<0.001)
- Using multiple imputation: 80% (95% CI, 66-94; P<0.001) would have improved
- 78% improved by ≥0.3 logMAR (≥15 letters)
- Mean improvement: 0.51 logMAR (25.5 letters)
- Maximum improvement: 1.18 logMAR (59 letters ≈ 12 lines on eye chart)
- 84% reported reading letters, numbers, words at home
- With zoom function: up to 20/42 Snellen equivalent (vs. ~20/400 native)
- Natural peripheral vision unaffected
- 26 serious adverse events in 19 participants; 81% within 2 months post-surgery, 95% resolved within 2 months
- NEJM editorial called it "The First Treatment to Restore Vision"

### The Device
- 2×2mm chip, smaller than grain of rice
- 378 photovoltaic pixels (100μm each)
- Subretinal placement (under the retina)
- Wireless — no batteries, no wires, no electronics outside the eye except glasses
- Uses AR glasses with camera + near-infrared light projection
- Solar-powered: each pixel converts light to electrical current
- Stimulates inner retinal neurons → visual cortex
- Patients train ~months to interpret prosthetic visual input
- Invented by Prof. Daniel Palanker, Stanford ophthalmology/electrical engineering

### Science Corporation
- Founded 2021 by Max Hodak (co-founder & former president of Neuralink, departed 2021)
- Bought Pixium Vision PRIMA assets for ~€4M (~$4.7M) in 2024 court sale
- Series C: $230M (Feb 2026), Lightspeed, Khosla, Y Combinator, IQT (CIA investor arm), Quiet Capital
- Post-money valuation: $1.5B
- Total raised: ~$490M
- HQ: Alameda, CA
- Also developing: Biohybrid neural interface, Vessel perfusion platform
- 47 total successful surgeries worldwide (as of Jul 2026)
- CE mark expected mid-2026 (Europe launch)
- FDA: pursuing humanitarian device exemption
- Would be first BCI company with a product in market

### Disease Burden
- Geographic atrophy (advanced dry AMD): leading cause of irreversible blindness in older adults
- 5+ million people affected worldwide
- ~1 million in US with severe cases
- CDC: vision loss/blindness costs US $134.2B total annually
- Per-person annual cost: $16,838 (CDC 2017 estimates)
- GA-specific: mean $11,533/year to payer post-diagnosis
- Indirect costs from GA: ~$24.4B/year in lost wages (US)
- GA annual costs per patient (total, incl indirect): $7,721 (UK) to $38,665 (USA)

### Cochlear Implant Precedent
- FDA approved 1984 (first multi-channel)
- Total procedure cost: $40,000-$50,000 (device ~$20K-$25K + surgery + rehab)
- Medicare coverage expanded 2022 (from 40% word recognition to 60%)
- <10% of eligible adults with moderate-to-profound hearing loss receive implants (ACIA)
- Growing ~10% annually
- Medicare reimbursement often below cost (initially killed market — manufacturer discontinued)
- Took ~40 years from FDA approval to adequate coverage
- 1989 NEJM paper documented how Medicare underpayment nearly killed adoption

### Comparison Technologies
- Extraocular magnifiers: mean improvement 0.24 logMAR (vs. PRIMA's 0.51)
- Implantable telescopes: similar ~0.24 logMAR improvement, plus magnify whole visual field
- Anti-VEGF injections (Eylea, Lucentis): for wet AMD only, not geographic atrophy
- Syfovre/Izervay: complement inhibitors for GA, slow progression but don't restore vision
- Second Sight Argus II: retinal prosthesis, discontinued 2020, crude light perception only
- Neuralink Blindsight: FDA breakthrough designation Sept 2024, no human trials yet, cortical approach

## Original Analysis

### 1. The €4M → $1.5B Arbitrage
Pixium Vision went bankrupt despite having the most advanced retinal prosthesis in the world, because it ran out of money at the worst possible moment — just as its clinical trial was producing the best vision restoration results ever recorded. Science Corp bought the entire technology for €4M. Today, PRIMA is the cornerstone of a $1.5B company.

The markup: ~375x return on acquisition cost. For context:
- Google bought Android for $50M → became the OS for 3.9B devices
- Facebook bought Instagram for $1B → worth $100B+
- But PRIMA may be the only case where a bankrupt medtech company's lead asset was purchased at liquidation value and reached first-to-market BCI status within 2 years

### 2. Cost-per-Letter-Restored Calculation
If PRIMA procedure costs approximately what a cochlear implant does ($40K-$50K):
- Mean improvement: 25.5 letters on ETDRS chart
- Cost per letter restored: $1,569 - $1,961
- Comparison: extraocular magnifiers improve ~12 letters for ~$2,000-$5,000 = $167-$417/letter
- But PRIMA restores central vision (reading ability), magnifiers just enlarge
- PRIMA's effect is qualitatively different — it creates NEW photoreceptor function

### 3. Break-Even vs. Blindness Costs
Annual cost of vision loss per person: $16,838 (CDC)
Assuming PRIMA procedure cost of $50,000:
- Break-even: 2.97 years ($50,000 / $16,838)
- At GA-specific $38,665/year (US estimate): break-even in 1.29 years
- 10-year NPV at 3% discount rate: $143,725 in avoided costs vs. $50K procedure = net $93,725 benefit
- This is conservative — doesn't include QOL improvements, fall prevention, reduced nursing home admission

### 4. Resolution Roadmap: Pixels to Normal Vision
- Current: 378 pixels → 20/400 native (20/42 with zoom)
- Next-gen target: 5x resolution → ~1,890 pixels → potential 20/80 native
- Legal driving (20/40): ~3,780 pixels needed
- Normal vision (20/20): ~150,000 pixels needed — decades away
- For comparison: iPhone camera has 48 million pixels, human retina has ~120 million photoreceptors
- Relevant milestone: 20/80 enables independent daily living tasks; 20/40 enables driving; face recognition needs ~5,000 pixels minimum

### 5. BCI Market Positioning
- Science Corp will be first BCI to market (ahead of Neuralink, Synchron, Paradromics, CorTec)
- Ironic: Hodak left Neuralink 2021, founded Science, now beating Neuralink to market
- Key distinction: PRIMA is retinal (simpler), not cortical (Neuralink Blindsight)
- Retinal approach has lower risk but only works when optic nerve is intact
- Cortical approach needed for optic nerve damage/congenital blindness
