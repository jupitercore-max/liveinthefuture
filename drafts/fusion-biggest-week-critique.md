2. **Differentiation from prior coverage** — LITF published "Fusion's Experience Rate Is 34× Worse Than Solar's" (#382) by the same journalist. The cost skepticism angle overlaps. This draft needs to lean harder into the CFS-vs-Helion publication divergence as the novel contribution.

3. **The Helion section lacks teeth** — $15.5B valuation without peer review is the most provocative data point in the piece. Currently it's presented fairly but could be sharper. Kirtley's "we don't want to theorize" quote deserves more interrogation.

4. **Bottom Line runs long** — Should be one paragraph, currently reads as two compressed into one. Trim to the funding gap + SPARC timeline.

5. **Related articles** — The CFS PJM article link needs verification that the slug exists.
