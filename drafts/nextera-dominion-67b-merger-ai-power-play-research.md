# Research: NextEra Energy $67B Dominion Energy Acquisition

## Deal Summary
- **Announced:** May 18, 2026
- **Structure:** All-stock transaction, $66.8B (~$67B)
- **Terms:** 0.8138 NextEra shares per Dominion share
- **Ownership:** NextEra shareholders 74.5%, Dominion shareholders 25.5%
- **Combined market cap:** $249 billion
- **Enterprise value:** $420 billion
- **Expected close:** 12-18 months, pending regulatory approval
- **Largest utility deal since Exxon-Mobil merger in 1998**

## Combined Company Profile
- Over 80% regulated structure
- Nearly 10 million customers
- 110 GW generation assets
- 130 GW of large-load growth opportunities
- Rate base: ~$138 billion, growing ~11% annually through 2032
- 9%+ annual EPS growth projected through 2032
- 6% annual dividend growth through 2028
- World's largest regulated electric utility

## Leadership
- CEO: John Ketchum (NextEra, continues)
- Robert Blue (Dominion CEO) oversees regulated utilities business, joins board
- HQ: Juno Beach, FL (NextEra)

## Data Center Angle
- Dominion serves Northern Virginia - "Data Center Alley"
- N. Virginia: 4,900 MW operating data center capacity, 1,000 MW under construction
- 70% of global internet traffic passes through N. Virginia
- Dominion contracted 51 GW of data-center capacity
- 25 GW with energization dates through 2031
- Another 45 GW without connection dates yet
- NextEra plans to build 30+ data center hubs across US

## Bill Credits / Consumer Impact
- $2.25 billion in bill credits over 2 years post-close for VA, NC, SC customers
- Dominion serves ~3.6 million customers
- One-time $360 million cash payment to Dominion shareholders

## NextEra Florida Track Record
- FPL (Florida Power & Light) is NextEra's subsidiary
- Won approval for $7 billion rate plan, four years
- Raises customer bills by up to $148/month by 2029
- Largest electric rate hike in US history
- Florida customers recently absorbed massive rate increase

## Regulatory Concerns
- Virginia Gov. Abigail Spanberger pledged to make data centers pay more
- David Pomerantz (Energy and Policy Institute): "will lead to higher bills"
- Commonwealth of Virginia: "cautionary response" urged
- FPL history raises red flags for consumer advocates

## Reuters Analysis
- Deal is a "template" for future utility consolidation
- AI boom "resetting the minimum efficient scale of power supply"
- Power supplies = single biggest constraint on data center growth
- Data center power needs expected to nearly triple by end of decade
- Hyperscalers (Amazon, Google, Meta) consume more electricity than entire cities

## Context
- AES Corporation being acquired by consortium led by GIP/EQT for $15/share
- Constellation acquired Calpine for natural gas assets
- PJM capacity auction: 1,038% price increase in 3 years (covered in LITF #earlier)

## Original Calculation - The Virginia Household Math
- $2.25B bill credits / 3.6M customers / 24 months = $26.04/month per customer
- FPL rate increases for Florida: up to $148/month by 2029
- Gap: $122/month difference between credit and potential rate increase trajectory
- If NextEra applies similar rate-base growth strategy in Virginia...
