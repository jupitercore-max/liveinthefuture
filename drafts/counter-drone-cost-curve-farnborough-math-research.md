### 2. Reuters — Lockheed PAC-3 ACE (Jul 20, 2026)
- New PAC-3 Adapted Capability Effector (ACE) missile
- Cost: less than half of PAC-3 MSE (~$4M), so <$2M per missile
- Initial production within 36 months
- U.S. and European industry partners
- Source: Reuters, Jul 20 2026

### 3. Lockheed MORFIUS X-Rotor (Jul 20, 2026)
- High-power microwave technology, engages multiple drones at once
- Designed for recovery and reuse after missions
- "High kill rate while keeping cost per kill low" — Randy Crites, VP Lockheed Martin
- Source: Stocktwits/Lockheed Martin press

### 6. DragonFire Laser (UK MoD)
- Cost: £10 (~$13) per shot
- Capital: £316M ($413M) contract to MBDA
- Accuracy: can hit a £1 coin from 1km
- 50kW class laser (QinetiQ + Leonardo beam director + MBDA C2)
- Tested against drones at 650km/h
- Fitted to Type 45 destroyer by 2027
- 4 ships to be equipped by 2027
- Source: UK Ministry of Defence, Nov 2025

### 7. RTX Coyote Interceptor
- Block 2: ~$100,000-$120,000 per shot (kinetic kill)
- Block 3NK: non-kinetic, reusable, potentially lower cost per engagement
- $5B long-term contract
- $2.1B FS-LIDS sale to UAE (10 systems, 240 Coyote Block 2)
- Source: Army Recognition, Bloomberg

### 8. CounterPunch — Exchange Ratio Analysis
- Patriot PAC-3: ~$4M/shot
- THAAD: $10-12M/shot

- "Precise mass" concept: cheap drones exhaust interceptor queues, clearing path for missiles
- At least 12 US/allied radar systems hit since war began
- Source: CounterPunch/Defense Express/Asia Times
