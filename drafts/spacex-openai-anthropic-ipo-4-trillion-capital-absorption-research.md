## Core Thesis
Three companies worth a combined $3.7 trillion filed for U.S. IPOs within 11 days (June 1–12, 2026). SpaceX alone is raising $75 billion — more than the entire U.S. IPO market in 2022, 2023, and 2024 combined ($58.1B). The capital absorption math has no historical precedent.

## Novel Calculation: Capital Absorption Ratio
SpaceX $75B raise vs:
- Saudi Aramco (prev record): 2.93× larger
- Full-year 2025 ($44B): 1.70×
- 2022+2023+2024 combined ($58.1B): 1.29×
- 2026 YTD before these three ($34.2B): 2.19×

If OpenAI and Anthropic each raise $40-80B (implied by valuations):
- Combined $155-235B in potential capital demand
- The 2021 all-time record ($142.4B across 397 IPOs over 12 months) could be exceeded by 3 companies alone
- Capital absorption ratio: 1.09–1.65× the best IPO year in U.S. history

## Strongest Counterargument
Large marquee IPOs expand market liquidity rather than draining it. Saudi Aramco attracted sovereign/institutional capital that wouldn't have entered equity markets otherwise. The 2021 SPAC boom coincided with record venture funding and all-time equity highs. These filings may be GENERATING the momentum that lifts all boats. 2026 YTD market running +163.7% is evidence.
