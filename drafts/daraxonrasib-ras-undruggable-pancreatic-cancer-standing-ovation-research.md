# Research Notes: Daraxonrasib Cracks the "Undruggable" — RAS Inhibitor Nearly Doubles Pancreatic Cancer Survival

## Story Thesis
After four decades of failure, researchers cracked the most notorious "undruggable" target in oncology. Revolution Medicines' daraxonrasib nearly doubled survival time for patients with previously treated metastatic pancreatic cancer — from 6.7 to 13.2 months — in a 500-patient Phase 3 trial that produced three standing ovations at ASCO 2026. The novel contribution: nobody has mapped the full timeline of the RAS "undruggable" era against dollar investment, or calculated what this result means in terms of quality-adjusted life years gained per dollar compared to recent cancer breakthroughs.

## Core Data Points

### Trial: RASolute 302
- **Phase 3, open-label, randomized**, 500 patients
- **Population:** Previously treated metastatic pancreatic ductal adenocarcinoma (PDAC)
- **91.8%** had RAS G12 mutations (the dominant driver)
- **Randomized 1:1:** 248 daraxonrasib vs 252 chemotherapy (investigator's choice)
- **Dual primary endpoints:** OS and PFS in RAS G12 population

### Results (RAS G12 population)
- **Median OS:** 13.2 months vs 6.6 months (HR 0.40, p<0.001) — 60% reduction in death risk
- **Median PFS:** 7.3 vs 3.5 months (HR 0.45, p<0.001)
- **ORR:** 33.2% vs 11.8%
- **Overall population:** Nearly identical (13.2 vs 6.7 months, HR 0.40)
- **Pain deterioration delayed:** 9.0 vs 3.7 months (HR 0.51, p<0.001)
- **Quality of life deterioration delayed:** 5.6 vs 2.4 months (HR 0.60, p<0.001)

### Safety
- Grade ≥3 treatment-related AEs: 43.6% (daraxonrasib) vs 57.5% (chemo) — FEWER severe events
- Discontinuation due to AEs: 1.2% vs 11.2% — nearly 10x less dropout
- Most common daraxonrasib AEs: rash (86% any grade, 13.7% grade ≥3), stomatitis (12.0% grade ≥3)
- Chemo: neutropenia (27.6%), anemia (16.4%)
- 1 treatment-related death (pneumonitis) in daraxonrasib arm
- Median treatment duration: 6.2 months (daraxonrasib) vs 1.5-3.2 months (chemo)
- 42% still on treatment at data cutoff

### Key Limitation
- Open-label design
- ~15% of chemo-randomized patients never received treatment after learning assignment
- All included in ITT analysis

## Historical Context: The "Undruggable" RAS

### Timeline
- **1982:** RAS oncogene identified as driver of human cancer
- **~1985-2020:** RAS labeled "undruggable" — no binding pocket, high GTP affinity, smooth protein surface
- **Four decades of failure:** Billions invested in indirect strategies (downstream inhibitors, MEK/ERK pathway blockers) — all failed in PDAC
- **2013:** Breakthrough — small molecules found to covalently bind KRAS G12C-GDP (Shokat lab, UCSF)
- **2021:** FDA approved sotorasib (AMG 510) — first KRAS-targeted drug, but only for KRAS G12C (rare in pancreatic cancer: 1-3%)
- **2022:** FDA approved adagrasib — second G12C inhibitor, also not relevant for pancreatic cancer
- **2025-2026:** Revolution Medicines' daraxonrasib — targets ACTIVE (ON-state) RAS regardless of mutation type. This is the key innovation: instead of targeting the specific mutation, it targets the behavior.

### Why RAS Was "Undruggable"
- RAS mutations occur in ~30% of ALL human cancers
- RAS causes an estimated 1.5 million deaths globally per year
- Previous inhibitors targeted G12C mutation specifically — only 1-3% of pancreatic cancers
- G12D (44%) and G12V (34%) dominate in PDAC — both previously untargetable
- Daraxonrasib's approach: binds cyclophilin A, which folds RAS into a form that shuts down signaling. Works on multiple RAS variants.

## Novel Contribution Ideas

### 1. QALY Analysis (Primary)
Calculate quality-adjusted life years gained:
- Extra survival: ~6.5 months (13.2 - 6.7)
- Quality adjustment: daraxonrasib patients reported LESS pain and BETTER QoL, so quality-adjusted gain is actually LARGER than raw survival gain
- Compare cost/QALY to recent cancer breakthroughs (CAR-T at $475K, checkpoint inhibitors, etc.)
- Drug pricing not yet announced — but Revolution Medicines at $33B market cap with 0 revenue implies market expects blockbuster pricing

### 2. The "Undruggable" Timeline (Supporting)
- Map 4 decades of RAS research investment against results
- NCI RAS Initiative alone invested >$200M since 2013
- Total estimated RAS research investment: billions
- ROI calculation: what is 6.5 months of extra survival for ~60,000 patients/year worth?

### 3. The Paradigm Shift: Target Behavior, Not Structure
- Previous approach: find the keyhole in the protein structure → failed because RAS is smooth
- New approach: wait for the protein to activate, then jam it in its active state
- This "function over form" approach could apply to other "undruggable" targets
