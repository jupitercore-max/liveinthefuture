## Kill Test
- **Is this new?** YES — XREAL filed its HK IPO prospectus in early April 2026. First-ever audited financials from a pure-play smart glasses/AR glasses company. Nobody has seen verified unit economics for this category before.
- **Does anyone care?** YES — Smart glasses are projected to be the next computing platform (IDC: 22.67M shipments in 2026, 56.3% YoY growth). Meta, Apple, Google, Samsung, Huawei all entering. Investors and industry need to understand whether independents can survive.
- **Can we add original analysis?** YES — unit economics calculation nobody has done: per-unit revenue, gross profit, R&D cost, and cash runway. Plus market share trajectory projection showing XREAL shrinks as the market grows.

## Novel Contribution
1. **Per-unit economics decomposition:** Revenue $72M ÷ ~130K units = ~$554 ASP. Gross margin 35.2% = ~$195 gross profit per unit. R&D $25.4M ÷ 130K = ~$195 per unit. R&D per unit exactly equals gross profit — zero operational cash flow from hardware operations.
2. **Market share trajectory projection:** XREAL shipped ~130K units in 2023, 2024, AND 2025 (flat). Market grew from ~4.5M to ~14.5M smart glasses (including Meta's 7M in 2025). XREAL's smart glasses market share: ~2.6%. If units stay flat at 130K while IDC projects 22.67M in 2026, XREAL falls to ~0.6%. In AR-only subsegment (TrendForce: 950K in 2026), XREAL could hold ~14%. But AR is only 4.2% of total smart glasses.
3. **Cash runway calculation:** $8.8M cash at end-2025, down from $28.5M at end-2024. Net cash burn: ~$19.7M/year. At that rate, cash runs out by ~mid-2026 without IPO proceeds. This is a survival filing.
4. **54:1 unit gap analysis:** Meta/EssilorLuxottica sold 7M smart glasses in 2025 vs XREAL's 130K. Meta controls 56.8% of smart glasses market; XREAL has 2.6%. Meta plans to double/triple production to 20-30M units by 2027. The gap is widening, not narrowing.
5. **Valuation sanity check:** Series D valued XREAL at $833M on $72M revenue = 11.6× revenue. But revenue growth is flat, market share is declining, and cash is nearly exhausted. Compare to EssilorLuxottica (parent) at ~2× revenue. The premium implies explosive growth that the unit data contradicts.

## Strongest Counterargument
The strongest case for XREAL despite these numbers: the Google/Android XR partnership (Project Aura, launching late 2026) could be a transformative multiplier. XREAL is the "lead hardware partner" for Android XR, and if that platform succeeds, XREAL's hardware could ship through Google's distribution at scale. Think Samsung's role in early Android — being first to partner with the winning platform could offset all the unit gap math. The IPO raises capital to bridge to that potential inflection. Additionally, XREAL's 35.2% gross margin is genuinely strong for hardware and improving rapidly (from 18.8% two years ago), suggesting manufacturing discipline even at low volumes.

## Limitations
- XREAL's exact unit breakdown by model is not publicly available from the prospectus reporting we have — 130K is an aggregate estimate across product lines
- Meta does not separately disclose smart glasses financials; the 7M figure comes from EssilorLuxottica's earnings call and may include non-Meta smart glasses (though Meta dominates)
- The $554 ASP is a blended average; display AR glasses command higher ASPs than camera-only models
- Revenue split between Meta and EssilorLuxottica on smart glasses sales is not disclosed — direct per-unit profit comparison is not possible
- XREAL makes AR glasses (with displays); Meta's high-volume products are camera-only smart glasses (different product category). The comparison is somewhat apples-to-oranges but the market convergence makes it relevant
- Cash burn rate is estimated from year-end balance changes; actual quarterly burn rate may vary
