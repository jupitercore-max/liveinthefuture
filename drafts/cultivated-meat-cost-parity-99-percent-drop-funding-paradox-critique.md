## Critic 6: 🔬 Research Rigor — Novel Contribution, Limitations, Counterargument, Verifiability

**Score: 9.5/10**

**Strengths:**
- **Novel contribution (STRONG):** Two original analyses that go well beyond synthesis:
  1. Wright's Law learning rate calculation for cultivated meat (74% annual decline vs. genome sequencing's 45%) — nobody has published this comparison.
  2. TEA model gap analysis showing Parima's achieved cost ($8.19/kg at 22,000L) is 3x cheaper than Negulescu et al. predicted for a 2x-larger reactor, with the explanation that models forecast the wrong process (tissue engineering vs. suspension fermentation).
- **Limitations section (STRONG):** Dedicated section that honestly accounts for:
  - Relative vs. absolute cost reduction ambiguity
  - Pre-merger vs. post-merger cost figure uncertainty
  - Four-point learning rate calculation weakness ("directionally interesting but nowhere near statistically robust")
  - Regional duck price variation
  - Singapore as a tiny addressable market
- **Counterargument (EXCELLENT):** Humbird analysis presented at full strength across two paragraphs:
  - First paragraph: biological constraints (doubling time, oxygen transfer, aseptic infrastructure, amino acid costs)
  - Second paragraph: the deeper "cell paste" critique that reframes the cost comparison from duck to soy protein
  - Not dismissed — the article concedes "At $8.19, cultivated biomass loses that comparison badly"
- **Methodology transparency:** Shows the math (cost table with years, specific $/kg, named sources), explains the compound annual decline calculation, and acknowledges the weakness of four data points.

**Weaknesses:**
- The 74% annual decline calculation isn't shown step-by-step (e.g., ($2.3M/$8.19)^(1/12) = ...). Showing the formula would strengthen the methodology transparency requirement. Minor issue since the inputs are clearly stated.
- Could note that "independently verified" for the €7/kg claim — verified by whom? This is stated in the source material (Gourmey's claim) but the verifier isn't named in the article.

**Verdict:** Excellent rigor. Novel analysis is genuine (not synthesis), limitations are honest, counterargument is at full strength and not strawmanned. This is one of the article's strongest dimensions.
