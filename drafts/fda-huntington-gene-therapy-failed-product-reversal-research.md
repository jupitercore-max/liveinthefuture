# Research Notes: FDA Huntington's Gene Therapy Reversal

## Topic
uniQure's AMT-130 Huntington's disease gene therapy — FDA triple reversal, same data, different leadership

## Key Data Points

### AMT-130 Clinical Data
- Phase I/II: 29 patients (17 high-dose, 12 low-dose), 12 per dose at 36 months
- 75% slowing of disease progression (cUHDRS, P = .003)
- High-dose: -0.38 from baseline vs -1.52 for external control
- 60% slowing by TFC (P = .033)
- CSF NfL decreased 8.2% from baseline
- External control: propensity-matched from Enroll-HD (940 for high dose, 626 for low)
- Delivery: 14-18 hour neurosurgery, AAV vector with miR-451 into striatum

### FDA Timeline
- 2024: FDA green-lit BLA without Phase 3 (pre-Makary)
- Sept 2025: Phase I/II data published showing 75% slowing
- Nov 2025: FDA reversed, data insufficient (Makary/Prasad era). Senior regulator called it "failed product"
- March 2, 2026: FDA recommended sham surgery-controlled trial
- May 2026: Makary + Prasad departed FDA. Kyle Diamantas acting commissioner.
- June 17, 2026: FDA reversed AGAIN, 3-year data acceptable for BLA

### Huntington's Disease
- ~30,000 symptomatic in US, ~200,000 at risk
- US prevalence ~13-15 per 100,000
- No approved disease-modifying treatments
- 12-18 years median survival from diagnosis
- 12x suicide risk vs general population
- CAG repeat in HTT gene
