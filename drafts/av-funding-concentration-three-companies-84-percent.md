# Research: AV Funding Concentration — Three Companies Got 84% of $21.4 Billion

## Story Angle
Autonomous vehicle funding tripled to $21.4B in the first four months of 2026, but the money went to just three companies: Waymo ($16B), Wayve ($1.3-1.5B), and Waabi ($1B total including Uber milestone). This isn't growth — it's a phase transition from "spray and pray" to "back the survivors." Meanwhile, China froze all new L4 robotaxi permits after a Wuhan incident, and Goldman Sachs projects a $415B global robotaxi market by 2035. The original calculation nobody ran: at current ride volumes, Waymo's investors are paying $4,846 in market cap per annual ride.

## Primary Sources
1. **Crunchbase data (via AV Market Strategist):** $21.4B across 34 deals through April 15, 2026. Up 262% from $5.9B across 99 deals in all 2025. https://avmarketstrategist.substack.com/p/av-funding-tripled-to-21b-china-freezes
2. **PitchBook data (via Business Insider/Agooka):** Nearly $19B in VC funding worldwide as of April 2026. No other year since 2014 saw that amount to fewer than a dozen players. 2021 peak: <$9B across 94 deals. https://agooka.com/news/usa/big-money-is-betting-the-self-driving-future-belongs-to-a-small-club/
3. **Goldman Sachs research:** $415B global robotaxi market by 2035. US: $19B in 2030 (up from prior $7B), $48B by 2035. Global fleet: 7K → 1M (2030) → 6M (2035). Gross margins 30-50%. Full AV sector ~$2T.
4. **Polymarket:** Waymo operations expected in 11 US cities by June 30, 2026 (43% probability).
5. **Waymo metrics:** 200M+ fully autonomous miles, 500K+ weekly rides across 10 cities. $126B valuation.

## Original Contribution: The Implied Price Per Ride
Waymo does ~500K rides/week = ~26M rides/year. At $126B valuation:
- **$4,846 in market cap per annual ride.** For context, Uber's market cap per annual ride at IPO was ~$22.
- Even at 10x current volume (260M rides/year = Uber's ~10% of 2025 rides), that's still $485/ride in market cap.
- To reach Uber-like market cap efficiency ($22/annual ride), Waymo needs ~5.7 billion rides/year — roughly matching Uber's entire 2025 global volume.
- Alternatively: $16B raise / 26M annual rides = $615 of fresh capital per ride this year. Each Waymo ride consumed more investor capital than a round-trip first-class airline ticket.

## Strongest Counterargument
The concentration IS the signal, not the bug. Woven Capital's Ro Gupta: "If we look back at earlier cycles, such as the dawn of automotive itself, we see a common pattern of experimentation, failure, success, consolidation." KPMG's Hugh Nguyen: "Investors learned from the 2019 capital cycle that spreading bets carries risk." The winners-take-most pattern is how platform markets mature, and the funding concentration means we're past the research phase and into the deployment phase where scale advantages compound. Waymo's 200M autonomous miles are a moat that no well-funded newcomer can replicate cheaply.

## Limitations
- Crunchbase vs PitchBook data differ ($21.4B vs ~$19B) depending on what counts as "AV" vs "AV-adjacent"
- Goldman projections are sell-side research with incentives to be optimistic
- China's permit freeze details are sparse — "Wuhan outage" referenced but not fully documented
- Waymo ride counts don't distinguish paid vs free/promotional rides
- Valuations are last-round markings, not market prices — actual liquidation value unknown
