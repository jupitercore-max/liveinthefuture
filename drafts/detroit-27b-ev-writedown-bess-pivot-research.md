# Research: Detroit's $27B EV Write-Down to BESS Pivot

## Core Thesis
Ford ($19.5B) and GM ($7.6B) combined wrote down $27.1 billion on EV investments. Both are now converting the same battery factories to produce grid-scale energy storage systems (BESS). But they're entering a market where Tesla already earns more per battery than Detroit earns per car — and BYD has already surpassed Tesla in deployments. The original calculation: at Tesla's revenue-per-GWh, Ford's 20 GWh/year target would generate $5.5B in annual revenue, but it would take ~12 years of gross profit to recover the EV write-down.

## Original Calculations

### 1. Revenue Per GWh Deployed
Tesla Energy: $12.8B / 46.7 GWh = **$274M per GWh deployed**

### 2. Ford's Theoretical Revenue at Tesla's Rate
Ford target: 20 GWh/year × $274M = **$5.48B per year**
At Tesla's 30% gross margin = **$1.64B gross profit per year**
Recovery of $19.5B write-down at that rate: **~12 years**
But: recovery of $2B pivot investment: **~14 months**
The write-down money is already sunk — the pivot is cheap relative to expected returns.

### 3. Cost Per GWh of Annual Manufacturing Capacity
- Ford: $2B investment / 20 GWh target = **$100M per GWh of annual capacity**
- GM: $70M retool / [unknown target] GWh = unclear but cheap
- Tesla: Houston factory alone at ~$50 GWh/year capacity, investment not fully disclosed but Megapack 3 factory cost estimated at several billion

### 4. Margin Comparison
Tesla Energy gross margin: ~30%
Tesla Automotive gross margin: ~15%
Energy storage is **2x as profitable per dollar of revenue** as selling cars.
Ford EV losses: -$44,000 per Lightning sold in 2024.
At Tesla's energy margins, Ford's 5.45 MWh DC Block at market pricing would generate more gross profit than selling a $50,000 truck.

### 5. The Competitive Gap
- Tesla started energy storage: ~2015
- Ford first deliveries: late 2027 (12+ years behind)
- GM first deliveries: 2027-2028 (12-13 years behind)
- Analyst quote (Rhind): By 2028, Tesla will be "two product generations ahead"
- "The real moat is not the battery box; it is the software layer, the grid integration and a decade of deployment experience"

### 6. xAI as a Case Study
xAI spent ~$1B on Tesla Megapacks since 2024
April 2026 alone: $269M (more than all of 2024)
A single customer's Megapack spending exceeds what Ford Energy is likely to generate in its entire first year.

## Limitations
- Ford's actual BESS pricing is unknown — they may undercut Tesla significantly
- GM Energy has no public financials or capacity targets
- Tesla's revenue-per-GWh includes Powerwall (residential) mixed with Megapack (utility), different price points
- BYD's BESS margins are not publicly disclosed — could be undercutting on price
- CATL LFP licensing terms for Ford are not public
- Sodium-ion is commercially unproven at grid scale — GM's bet is genuinely risky
- Tariff uncertainty (35% on Chinese cathode/anode materials) affects all players

## Strongest Counterargument
The write-down comparison is misleading because it conflates sunk costs with forward economics. Ford's $19.5B is gone regardless. What matters is the $2B forward investment and the return it generates. By that measure, Ford's pivot looks shrewd: $2B to enter a ~$200B market with CATL technology, existing factory infrastructure, and a confirmed customer (EDF Power Solutions, 20 GWh over 5 years). Tesla proved demand exists at 30% margins. Ford just needs to execute — and CATL's technology is arguably better suited for LFP grid storage than anything Tesla currently manufactures in-house.
