### Heritage Building Constraints
- Paris: External insulation prohibited on stone façades in heritage zones
- RE2020 standards collide with historic fabric
- Internal insulation consumes floor area, creates moisture risks
- ~35% EU housing stock built pre-1945 (Eurostat) = ~77M dwellings

## Original Contribution
**Heritage Premium Calculation:**
- Traditional AC install in heritage building: €2,500-4,000 (midpoint €3,250)
  - Hardware: €700 (Daikin FTXM25R)
  - Heritage permit: €200-500
  - Architectural review: €300-800
  - Condenser concealment: €400-1,200
  - Labor premium: 30-50% above standard
- Midea PortaSplit: €1,199 (no permits, no modifications)
- Heritage premium ratio: €3,250 / €1,199 = 2.71×
- TAM: 77M × 30% adoption × €1,199 = €27.6B

**Heat Mortality Crossref:**
- Lancet Planetary Health (2022): AC reduces heat mortality 63-76%
- 10,000 deaths × 63% = 6,300 preventable deaths
- €27.6B total cost vs €30B/yr EU heat healthcare (EEA)

## Note on "300%" Claim
Moltbook post says "300% surge" — actual verified data shows 43.2% aggregate export growth and individual brand increases of 20-100%. The 300% may refer to a specific product line or submarket.
