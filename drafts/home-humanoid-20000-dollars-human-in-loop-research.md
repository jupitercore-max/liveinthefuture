# Research: 1X NEO Home Robot — $20K, 10K Pre-Orders, Human Teleoperator

## Story Angle
The first consumer humanoid robot factory just opened in Hayward, CA. 10,000 pre-orders sold in 5 days at $20,000 each. But when NEO can't handle a task autonomously, a 1X employee in a VR headset takes over — with a live camera view of your home. Early buyers are effectively paying $20K to join a data collection program that trains the AI for future buyers.

**Novel Contribution:** Cost-per-autonomous-hour analysis comparing NEO's teleoperation model to alternatives; comparison of "customer-as-data-source" business models across Tesla FSD, 1X NEO, and Chinese competitors; manufacturing economics at different production scales.

## Core Facts

### 1X Technologies / NEO Robot
- **Factory:** 58,000 sq ft, Hayward, CA (opened April 30, 2026)
- **Robot specs:** 168 cm tall, 30 kg, noise below a refrigerator
- **Price:** $20,000 (early access)
- **Pre-orders:** 10,000 sold in 5 days (October 2025 launch)
- **Production targets:** 10,000 units year 1; 100,000 by end of 2027
- **Vertical integration:** Makes own motors (automated copper coil winding), actuators, batteries, sensors, transmissions
- **AI brain:** NEO Cortex on NVIDIA Jetson Thor platform
- **Tendon-driven actuators, 3D-lattice cushioned limbs**
- **Machine-washable nylon "knit suits" (tan, gray, dark brown)**
- **200+ jobs created in Hayward**
- **NEOs already working on factory floor** (logistics, stocking parts, collecting real-world training data)
- **Additional factory in Moss, Norway**
- **Quality: Automotive-grade DFMEA, 20+ million stress test cycles per hardware component**
- **Hayward also home to Zoox (Amazon) 220K sq ft robotaxi factory**

### The "Data Flywheel" Business Model Comparison
- **Tesla FSD:** Customers pay $12K/month for supervised self-driving; their driving data trains the neural network for all future Tesla owners. Billions of miles collected.
- **1X NEO:** Customers pay $20K for a home robot; when it fails, a human operator takes over and the interaction trains the AI. Privacy is far more intimate (inside your home vs. public roads).
- **Chinese approach (Unitree, AGIBOT):** High volume, lower price, enterprise-first data collection. Factory/warehouse data, not home data.

### Novel Calculation: Cost Per Autonomous Hour
If NEO operates 4 hours/day and requires human intervention ~30% of the time:
- Autonomous hours: 4 × 0.7 = 2.8 hours/day × 365 = 1,022 hours/year
- Cost of robot amortized over 5 years: $4,000/year
- Cost per autonomous hour: $3.92/hour
- But the teleoperator hours (~1.2 hrs/day) must be paid by 1X. If teleoperators cost $25/hour burdened, that's $10,950/year PER ROBOT in operator costs.
- At 10,000 robots × $10,950 = $109.5M/year in operator costs alone
- This is why 1X MUST reduce teleoperation or the model breaks at scale
- For comparison: a Roomba costs ~$0.14/hour for floor cleaning (fully autonomous)
- A human housekeeper costs $25-35/hour
