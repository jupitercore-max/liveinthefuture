# Quantinuum IPO Research Notes

## Topic
Quantinuum (QNT) IPO reality check — first traditional quantum computing IPO, $1.68B raised at $60/share, 410× P/S ratio, stock fell below IPO price by day 2.

## Key Data Points

### IPO Facts
- Ticker: QNT on Nasdaq
- IPO price: $60/share, 28 million shares (upsized from original 26.5M plan)
- Raised: $1.68B gross proceeds
- IPO priced: Wednesday June 3, 2026
- First trading day: Thursday June 4, 2026
- Day 1: Opened $68 (+13%), peaked $71.35, closed $60.38 (+0.6%)
- Day 2 (Friday June 5): Closed $56.26 (−6.2% below IPO price)
- Market cap at IPO close: ~$15.7B; at Friday close: ~$14.6B
- Underwriters: J.P. Morgan, Morgan Stanley (co-lead), Jefferies, Evercore ISI, BofA, UBS, etc.
- Greenshoe: 4.2 million additional shares option

### Financials (from S-1)
- 2024 revenue: $23M
- 2025 revenue: $30.9M (+34% YoY)
- Q1 2025 revenue: $19.1M
- Q1 2026 revenue: $5.2M (−73% YoY)
- 2024 net loss: $144.1M
- 2025 net loss: $192.6M
- Q1 2026 net loss: $136.6M (vs $30.5M Q1 2025)
- R&D: exceeded 5× revenue in 2025
- Cash: $677M as of March 31 (down from $762.6M year-end)
- Post-IPO liquidity: ~$2.3B
- Runway at Q1 burn rate: ~16 quarters (4 years)
- P/S ratio: ~410× (on 2025 revenue)

### Customer Concentration
- RIKEN (Japan government-affiliated research institute) = ~60% of 2025 revenue
- Estimated RIKEN contribution: ~$18.5M of $30.9M
- Non-RIKEN revenue: ~$12.4M from all other customers
- P/S on non-concentrated revenue: ~1,266×

### Technology
- Trapped-ion architecture (same as IonQ)
- Helios: 98 qubits
- Two-qubit gate fidelity: 99.921%
- Single-qubit fidelity: 99.9975%
- Physical-to-logical qubit ratio: 2:1 (best in industry)
- Next-gen system: Apollo (2D array of trapping locations)
- Formed 2021: Honeywell Quantum Solutions + Cambridge Quantum Computing
- >$2B invested in R&D over decade

### Ownership & Investors
- Honeywell: ~48.1% voting power post-IPO
- Previous round (2024): $600M at $10B pre-money
- Other investors: Nvidia (venture arm), Amgen
- CEO: Rajeeb Hazra

### Government
- Commerce Department: up to $100M LOI for fault-tolerant trapped-ion (announced May 21)
- Trump admin: $2B equity stakes in 9 quantum companies
- Polymarket gov investment odds: Rigetti 90%, D-Wave 83%, IonQ 54%
