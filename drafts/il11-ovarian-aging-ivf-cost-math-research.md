# Research: IL-11 Ovarian Aging — IVF Cost Math

## Primary Sources

### 1. Wu et al., Nature Aging, July 2 2026 (Open Access)
**Title:** "Modulating IL-11-dependent matrix stiffness to delay ovarian aging"
**DOI:** 10.1038/s43587-026-01159-2
**URL:** https://www.nature.com/articles/s43587-026-01159-2
**Authors:** Meng Wu, Qingqing Zhu, Jinjin Zhang et al.
**Institution:** Tongji Medical College, Huazhong University of Science and Technology

**Key findings:**
- Ovarian stroma becomes fibrotic/stiff with age due to IL-11-driven collagen deposition via ERK1/2 pathway
- IL-11 elevated in aging ovaries of mice, rats, AND humans (cross-species conservation, first reported)
- IL-11 negatively correlated with AMH (ovarian reserve marker)
- Human ovarian stiffness: 16 kPa (31-year-old) → 30 kPa (41-year-old) via shear-wave elastography
- siIl11 nanoparticle treatment in 36-week-old mice (≈late 30s/early 40s human):
  - 35.7% reduction in ovarian stiffness
  - Doubled conception rate: 25% → 50% (mice); 20% → 50% (rats)
  - Increased litter size: 3→5 (mice); 1→5 (rats, fivefold)
  - Restored estrogen levels, normalized FSH
  - Increased secondary/antral follicles
- Also works in chemo-induced POI, PCOS, and endometriosis models
- IL-11ra1 knockout mice: preserved ovarian function, delayed aging
- Heterozygous Il11ra1 KO = normal fertility (partial inhibition safe)
- Oocyte DNA integrity unaffected; no heritable genetic changes in offspring

**Limitations stated by authors:**
- Systemic siIl11 NPs via tail vein lack ovary-specific targeting
- Used 9-month mice (preventive approach), not old mice with established fibrosis
- Long-term ovarian function needs assessment
- Human IL-11 increase data from women with gynecological cancer history

### 2. Cook et al., Nature, July 2024
**Title:** "Inhibition of IL-11 signalling extends mammalian healthspan and lifespan"
**DOI:** (Nature 2024)
**Key findings:**
- Anti-IL-11 antibody (X203) extends mouse lifespan 22.5% (male) / 25% (female)
- Started treatment at 75 weeks (≈55 human years)
- Improved metabolism (white→brown fat shift), muscle mass/strength
- Reduced cancer, cardiometabolic disease, telomere shortening
- Preserved mitochondrial function
- Works in both sexes (unlike rapamycin/metformin)

### 3. Stuart Cook / Enleofen / Boehringer Ingelheim
- Cook = co-founder Enleofen Bio (Duke-NUS spin-out, founded 2017)
- Boehringer acquired Enleofen's anti-IL-11 platform 2020, $1B+ per product milestone payments
- **BI 765423** = first-in-class anti-IL-11 monoclonal antibody
- Phase 1 completed: favorable safety/tolerability in healthy volunteers across wide dose range
- **Phase IIa started January 2026** for idiopathic pulmonary fibrosis (IPF)
- IPF focus first, but platform applicable to fibrosis across organs

## Original Calculation: The IVF Age Tax

**Setup:** IVF cost per live birth escalates exponentially with age. Each 5-year step roughly doubles or triples the cost.

**The escalation:**
- 35→40: cost per live birth rises 2.4× ($26,905 → $60,301)
- 40→42+: rises another 3.3× ($123,711 → $413,793)
- Total 35→42+: rises 15.4× 

**If anti-IL-11 shifts ovarian function by one age bracket (≈5 years):**
For a 41-year-old woman (9.7% success rate):
- Current expected cycles to one live birth: 10.3 cycles
- Current expected cost: 10.3 × $15,000 = $154,500 (conservative)
- With age-shifted success (19.9%): 5.0 cycles needed
- New expected cost: 5.0 × $15,000 = $75,000
- Savings per patient: $79,500

For a 42-year-old woman (2.9% success rate):
- Expected cycles: 34.5 cycles
- Expected cost: $517,500 (at $15K/cycle)
- With age-shifted success (9.7%): 10.3 cycles
- New expected cost: $154,500
- Savings per patient: $363,000

**Scale estimate:**
- ~30% of US IVF cycles for women 38+ ≈ 81,000 cycles
- At ~2.5 cycles per woman: ~32,400 women aged 38+ undergoing IVF/year
- Conservative savings of $50K-$363K per woman
- Aggregate annual savings: $1.6B-$11.8B (range reflects uncertainty in age distribution)
- Midpoint estimate: ~$4B/year in reduced IVF costs for US alone

**But the biggest number:** The post-menopausal health burden
- Preserved estrogen delays: osteoporosis ($19B/yr US), cardiovascular disease ($219B/yr), possibly Alzheimer's
- If menopause onset delayed even 2 years for 30M post-menopausal American women, the health savings dwarf IVF economics
- Too speculative to put a number on this, but acknowledge it

## Counterarguments
1. **Mouse-to-human translation gap:** Most mouse fertility findings fail in humans. The ovarian stiffness mechanism is validated in human tissue, but the therapeutic response is mice/rats only.
2. **Complete IL-11 KO causes infertility:** The therapeutic window is narrow — too much inhibition is worse than none. The paper addresses this (partial vs. complete), but dose-finding in humans will be critical.
3. **Systemic side effects:** IL-11 expressed in many tissues. Duncan: "The safety bar to any drug targeting the ovary is incredibly high."
4. **Fibrosis reversal vs. prevention:** The paper used 9-month mice (prevention), not reversal of established fibrosis. Most women seeking fertility help already have advanced ovarian aging.
5. **Cancer survivor selection bias:** Human IL-11 data from women with gynecological cancer — may not generalize.
6. **Regulatory pathway unclear:** No FDA pathway for "fertility aging" per se. Boehringer is pursuing IPF, not fertility. Reproductive indication would require enormous trials.
