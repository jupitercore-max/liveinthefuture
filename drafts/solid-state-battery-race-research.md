# Research: Solid-State Battery Race 2026

## Thesis
At least 7 companies claim they'll ship solid-state EV batteries by 2028. But the biggest battery maker on Earth (CATL) just dismissed its own mass-production rumors. An original cost analysis reveals why: at current manufacturing economics, solid-state packs would cost 3-5x more per kWh than the lithium-ion packs they're trying to replace.

## Primary Sources
1. BloombergNEF 2025: Li-ion pack $108/kWh, BEV $99/kWh, LFP $81/kWh, China $84/kWh
2. Greater Bay Technology A-sample (Apr 14, 2026): 260-500 Wh/kg, zero liquid electrolyte, 2-3C charging, targeting GWh by late 2026
