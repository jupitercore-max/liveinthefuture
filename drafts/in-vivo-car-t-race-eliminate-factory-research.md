# Research: In Vivo CAR-T — The Race to Eliminate the Factory

## Thesis
Traditional CAR-T therapy costs $500K+ per patient and takes 4-6 weeks because it requires extracting, engineering, growing, and reinfusing a patient's T cells. At least five companies are now racing to skip all of that by reprogramming T cells inside the body with a single injection. Over $3.6 billion in M&A has already validated the thesis. If any of these approaches work, CAR-T could go from a last-resort treatment at 200 specialized centers to a routine injection at any oncologist's office.

## Sources (3+ Primary)

### Source 1: ASGCT 2026 — Therorna TI-0032 (BusinessWire, May 12 2026)
- Therorna Inc. presented at ASGCT 2026 (May 11-15, Boston)
- TI-0032: circular RNA (circRNA) based in vivo CAR-T
- CD19-targeted, for autoimmune diseases and oncology
- First-in-human trial underway
- Durable CAR expression and B-cell depletion in humanized mice
- US/China dual IND filings on track
- Also TI-0093 in pipeline
- circRNA advantage: more stable than mRNA (resists exonuclease degradation), longer expression window

### Source 2: Kelonia Therapeutics — KLN-1010 (Precision Medicine Online, 2026)
- FDA cleared KLN-1010 for US Phase 1 trials (expanded from Australia)
- BCMA-targeted in vivo CAR-T for relapsed/refractory multiple myeloma
- Uses lentiviral vector (permanent integration)
- Key: no lymphodepleting chemotherapy required
- First in vivo CAR-T to reach US clinical stage
- Early Australian data: promising safety, rapid B-cell depletion
- ASH 2025 data presented

### Source 3: AbbVie/Capstan Therapeutics (AbbVie press release, June 30 2025)
- AbbVie acquired Capstan for up to $2.1 billion
- CPTX2309: first-in-class targeted LNP (tLNP) anti-CD19 CAR-T
- Lipid nanoparticles deliver mRNA directly to CD8+ T cells in vivo
- For B cell-mediated autoimmune diseases
- Phase 1 trial
- Capstan originally published in Science (2022): LNP reprogram T cells in vivo in mice, clearing cardiac fibrosis

### Source 4: Roche/Poseida Therapeutics (MHE, 2025)
- Roche acquired Poseida for $1.5 billion
- P-CD19CD20-ALLO1: allogeneic (off-the-shelf) dual-targeted CAR-T
- For multiple sclerosis and lupus
- Targets both CD19 and CD20 B cells

### Source 5: CAR-T Cost/Access Data
- PMC systematic review: mean CAR-T drug cost $391,060 (75% of total treatment cost)
- Total treatment often exceeds $700K-$1M including hospitalization
- Real-world: $702K total for CAR-T vs $372K for BiTE therapy (MHE 2025)
- 60% of hematologic cancer patients live >30 min from CAR-T centers
- Treatment timeline: 4-6 weeks from leukapheresis to infusion
- India decentralized manufacturing: $25K-$50K (vs $400K+ in US)
- Time toxicity: 4+ weeks of care required
- Only ~200 centers in US can administer

### Source 6: AJMC/CancerNetwork — In Vivo CAR-T Promise
- Multiple reviews establishing the in vivo CAR-T landscape
- Nature study: CRISPR-based in vivo CAR integration (19.7% TRAC-CAR editing in mice, complete responses in B-ALL models)
- Dual-vector system: CD3-targeted delivery + AAV for precise integration

## Original Contribution
1. **First comprehensive in vivo CAR-T pipeline tracker** — map all 5+ companies, their technology platforms, clinical stage, and target indications
2. **Cost modeling:** Traditional manufacturing cost ~$200K-400K of the $500K+ total → in vivo eliminates manufacturing entirely. Model: drug cost drops to potentially $10K-50K (LNP/RNA manufacturing at scale), total treatment from $700K to <$100K
3. **Three competing technology platforms compared:**
   - Viral (lentiviral): Kelonia, Umoja — permanent integration, one-and-done, but genomic integration risk
   - Non-viral LNP (mRNA/circRNA): AbbVie/Capstan, Therorna — transient expression, redosable, no integration risk, but may need repeat dosing
   - CRISPR-based: Academic (Nature) — permanent + precise, but delivery challenge
4. **Access expansion math:** ~200 US centers → any of ~5,000+ oncology practices = 25x access expansion. Time: 4-6 weeks → potentially same-day.
5. **M&A validation:** $3.6B+ (AbbVie $2.1B + Roche $1.5B) in acquisitions in <12 months signals pharma conviction
