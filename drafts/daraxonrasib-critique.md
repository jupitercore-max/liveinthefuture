**Structure:** Excellent. The article follows the classic LITF arc — hook with a number (44 years), dramatic pivot ("It yielded."), data section, mechanistic explainer, novel analysis (QALY), limitations, counterargument, actionables, bottom line. All required sections present and well-ordered.

**Minor nits:**
