# Research: Paradromics First-In-Human Connexus BCI

## Core Facts
- **Date:** June 17, 2026
- **Company:** Paradromics Inc. (Austin, TX, founded 2015 by Matt Angle, Stanford)
- **Device:** Connexus BCI — 421 microelectrodes, fully implantable wireless
- **Architecture:** Brain electrode array → chest transceiver → wireless external receiver
- **Patient:** Michigan woman with motor neuron disease
- **Surgery:** University of Michigan Health, led by Matthew Willsey, M.D., Ph.D. + Aditya S. Pandey, M.D.
- **Trial:** Connect-One Early Feasibility Study (EFS), FDA IDE approved Nov 2025
- **Sites:** UC Davis, U Michigan, Mass General Hospital
- **Follow-up:** 6 years
- **Preclinical claim:** 200+ bits/second information transfer rate (industry-leading per company)
- **Previous human milestone:** June 2025, temporary implant during epilepsy surgery at U Michigan (placed, recorded, removed in <20 min)

## Competitive Landscape (as of June 2026)

### Neuralink
- N1 implant: 1,024 electrodes across 64 ultra-thin threads
- ~21 patients (as of Jan 2026), targeting 1,000 by year-end
- $650M raised (June 2025), multi-billion valuation
- R1 surgical robot
- Thread retraction issue in first patient (85% retracted)
- 40 WPM typing demonstrated
- Samsung making 4th-gen chip (4nm, bidirectional for vision)
- PRIME (US), GB-PRIME (UK), CAN-PRIME (Canada) + UAE
- Wired initially, now wireless through skull

### Synchron (Stentrode)
- Endovascular approach (through jugular vein to brain blood vessel)
- 16 electrodes, no open brain surgery
- Lower bandwidth but safer procedure
- 10+ patients
- $145M+ raised

### BrainGate/UC Davis (Harrell case)
- Utah array: 96 electrodes
- Mark Harrell: 3,800+ hours home use, 99% lab accuracy, 92% daily use
- Working full-time with ALS
- Research-grade, not commercial product

### Precision Neuroscience
- Layer 7 cortical array: 1,024+ electrodes
- Thin-film, placed on brain surface (not penetrating)
- Temporary implants in epilepsy patients
- Raised ~$100M+

## Key Technical Differentiators of Paradromics
1. **Fully implantable wireless** — no percutaneous connectors (unlike early BrainGate)
2. **Two-body design** — brain electrode + chest transceiver (reduces heat/RF at skull)
3. **Penetrating microelectrodes** (not surface) — records individual neurons
4. **200+ bits/sec preclinical** — highest claimed information transfer rate
5. **FDA Breakthrough Device Designation + IDE** — regulatory fast-track
