# Research: Wheels Beat Legs — Genesis Eno + Rainbow Robotics RB-Y1

## Thesis
Two companies on different continents (France, Korea) independently chose wheeled bases over bipedal designs for their first commercial general-purpose robot deployments. Gartner predicts polyfunctional (non-humanoid) robots will outperform humanoids for most companies through 2028. The counterargument is Hyundai/Boston Dynamics Atlas with $86B investment and 25,000-unit 2028 deployment plan.

## Key Sources

- Founded early 2025, $105M raised (matching Mistral AI's record French seed)
- Backers: Khosla Ventures, Eclipse, Eric Schmidt, Xavier Niel, Bpifrance

## Original Contribution
**Deployment timeline analysis:** Wheeled robots are reaching commercial deployment 2+ years ahead of legged humanoids.
- Genesis AI: 18 months from founding to commercial deployment (early 2025 → end 2026)
- Rainbow Robotics RB-Y1: In commercial warehouse trial NOW (June 2026)
- Boston Dynamics Atlas: 30+ years development → product Jan 2026 → factory deployment 2028 → assembly tasks 2030

This isn't coincidence. Wheels eliminate the hardest unsolved problem in robotics: dynamic bipedal balance under load. Every DOF devoted to walking is a DOF not devoted to working.

Also: Korea already has the world's highest robot density (1,220/10K), but those are traditional fixed industrial arms. The transition to MOBILE general-purpose robots is a different market, and even the most robot-saturated economy on earth chose wheels for its first mobile warehouse deployment.
