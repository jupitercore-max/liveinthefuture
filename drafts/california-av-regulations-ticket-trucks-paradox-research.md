# Research: California's Dual AV Regulatory Gambit

## Thesis
California just published 100 pages of the most comprehensive autonomous vehicle regulations in the country. The paradox at the center: the same regulatory package that creates a system to ticket driverless cars for traffic violations also opens California highways to 80,000-pound autonomous semi trucks for the first time. One hand tightens control; the other loosens it.
