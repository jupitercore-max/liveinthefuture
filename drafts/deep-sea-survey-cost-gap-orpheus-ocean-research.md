# Research Notes: Deep-Sea Survey Cost Gap — Orpheus Ocean

## Core Thesis
The deep seafloor holds an estimated 21.1 billion dry tons of polymetallic nodules in the CCZ alone, worth an estimated $25+ trillion. But we've barely surveyed it because traditional deep-sea vehicles cost $5-10 million each and require $70-80K/day research vessels. Orpheus Ocean (WHOI spinoff) builds AUVs for ~$200K that reach the same depths. This 25-50x cost reduction transforms the economics of baseline environmental surveys — which are required before mining can begin but have been financially impossible at scale. The timing is critical: Trump just created the Marine Minerals Administration, BOEM opened Virginia OCS for mineral leasing TODAY (June 23, 2026), and The Metals Company wants to start commercial extraction by Q4 2027.

## Original Calculations

### 1. Traditional Deep-Sea Baseline Survey Cost per km²
- Ship time: $75,000/day (midpoint for global-class research vessel)
- ROV bottom time: ~6-8 hours/day (rest = descent/ascent/maintenance at 6000m)
- Survey rate: ~1 km²/day for detailed biological/geological baseline
- ROV amortization: $7.5M vehicle ÷ 1,500 operational days (5 years) = $5,000/day
- Total per km²: ~$80,000

### 2. The CCZ Baseline Survey Bottleneck
- CCZ area: 6,000,000 km²
- At 1 km²/day: 6,000,000 ship-days needed
- With ONE vehicle: 16,438 years
- Global fleet of ~25 capable vehicles (if ALL devoted to CCZ): 658 years
- Available ship-days per year per vehicle: ~200 (weather, transit, maintenance)
- Realistic: ~5,000 ship-days/year globally available for CCZ work
- Time to baseline at current capacity: 1,200 years

### 3. Orpheus Fleet Model
- 50 vehicles = $10M (same as 1-2 traditional ROVs)
- Deployable from smaller vessels (~$15,000/day)
- Multiple vehicles per ship (demonstrated: 2 from R/V Rainier)
- Coverage per vehicle per day: ~2 km² (10 km sorties, wider area via hopping)
- Fleet of 50, deployed 10 per ship, 5 ships: 100 km²/day
- Total vessel cost: 5 × $15,000 = $75,000/day for 100 km² = $750/km²
- CCZ baseline time: 6,000,000 ÷ 100 = 60,000 days ÷ 200 operational days/year = 300 years
- Still centuries — but manageable ISA license areas (~75,000 km²) in 2-3 years per area

### 4. Per-License-Area Economics
- Average ISA license area: ~75,000 km²
- Traditional: 75,000 × $80,000 = $6 billion per license area
- Orpheus fleet: 75,000 × $750 = $56.25 million per license area
- Ratio: 107x cost reduction
- TMC's NORI-D NPV: $5.5 billion — traditional baseline survey costs exceed the mine's value
- With Orpheus: $56M baseline vs $5.5B mine value — now economically rational
