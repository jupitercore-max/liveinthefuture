# Research: Humanoid Robot Production vs Global Workforce — The Math

## Thesis
The humanoid robot industry shipped ~16,000 units globally in 2025, with announced production capacity targeting 100,000+ units/year by 2027. An original calculation reveals that at even the most optimistic production rates, it would take over a century to staff all US warehouse jobs with humanoids — but the unit economics already beat human labor costs by 3-4x in structured logistics.

### Labor Economics
- US warehouse worker (BLS): ~$18-20/hr average, ~$25/hr fully burdened
- A $50,000 robot at 6,000 hrs/yr, 150 units/hr, 90% uptime → capital cost $0.012/unit + $0.03/unit maintenance = $0.042/unit
- Human at $25/hr, 200 units/hr = $0.125/unit
- **Robot is ~3x cheaper per unit moved** (CleanTechnica analysis)

### Original Calculations To Run

1. **Years to staff all US warehouses:** 1.8M workers ÷ ~100K robots/year (optimistic 2027 capacity) = 18 years. But robots work 6,000 hrs/yr vs human's ~2,000 = 1 robot replaces ~3 workers = only 600K robots needed = 6 years of peak production.

2. **Hourly cost comparison:** $20,000 robot / (6,000 hrs × 3-year life) = $1.11/hr capital. Add $1.50/hr for electricity (~500W = $0.50 + maintenance $1.00). Total: $2.61/hr. vs $18-25/hr human. **Robot is 7-10x cheaper per hour.** But only for tasks it can actually do.

3. **Global workforce replacement timeline:** 3.5B workers, even at 1M robots/year (no company projects this before 2030s), robot works 3x hours = replaces 3M worker-equivalents/year. 3.5B ÷ 3M = 1,167 years. The number is meaningless — it just shows scale.

4. **Cumulative spending to reach 100K installations:** At average $50K/unit = $5B. At $20K/unit = $2B. At $5,900 (Unitree R1) = $590M. The industry could reach 100K cumulative for less than one AI data center costs.

5. **China vs US production gap:** China shipped 80%+ in 2025. US capacity announced for 2027 (1X 100K + Figure 12K + Agility + Tesla) could theoretically exceed China's. But China is SHIPPING NOW while US is ANNOUNCING. The gap is 12 months of execution.
