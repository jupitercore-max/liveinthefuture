# Research: CRISPR One-Shot Cholesterol vs. 14,600 Statin Pills

## Beat: 🧪 Genomics
## Journalist: Dr. Kenji Watanabe
## Date: August 6, 2026

## Core Story
CTX310, a CRISPR-Cas9 gene therapy by CRISPR Therapeutics, delivered a one-time IV infusion that reduced LDL cholesterol by 50% and triglycerides by 55% in the first-in-human Phase 1 trial (15 patients, NEJM). It targets the ANGPTL3 gene in the liver to permanently switch off a protein that raises both LDL and triglycerides. This is the first CRISPR therapy to enter cardiology — the world's largest potential patient population for gene editing.

## Primary Sources

### 1. Phase 1 Trial Data (NEJM, Nov 2025)
- DOI: 10.1056/NEJMoa2511778
- 15 participants, ascending dose (0.1-0.8 mg/kg)
- Primary endpoint: safety (no dose-limiting toxicities)
- At highest dose (0.7-0.8 mg/kg): LDL reduced ~50%, triglycerides ~55%
- Reductions appeared within 2 weeks, sustained 60+ days
- ANGPTL3 protein reduced 73-80% at highest doses
- One death (179 days post-treatment, lowest dose, deemed unrelated)
- Infusion-related reactions in 3/15 (20%), mild
- One transient liver enzyme elevation, resolved by day 14
- Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/41211945/

### 2. Statin Adherence Data
- 25-50% discontinue within 6-12 months (PMC5012887)
- 40-75% discontinue within first year in community samples (Medscape)
- 42% adherence at 120 months / 10 years (AJMC)
- Statin discontinuation = 2.78x higher 12-month all-cause mortality after ischemic stroke (Medscape 570109)
- Non-adherence: 2.04x OR for stroke death (PMC4863178)
- 79.3% of patients who died within 1 year of stroke had discontinued statins vs. 29.9% of survivors

### 3. Market Data
- Global statin market: $16.3B (2025), ~$17.5B (2026)
- ~235 million prescriptions annually worldwide
- Cardiovascular disease: #1 cause of death globally, 17.9M deaths/year (WHO)
- Statins reduce cardiovascular events by 22-30% in treated populations

### 4. Comparable Drug Pricing
- Generic statins: $30-100/year
- Branded statins: $300-1,000/year
- PCSK9 inhibitors (Repatha/Praluent): ~$5,800/year (after discounts)
- Inclisiran (Leqvio): ~$6,500/year (2 injections/year)
- Evinacumab/Evkeeza (for HoFH): ~$450,000/year
- Casgevy (CRISPR sickle cell): ~$2.2M one-time

### 5. Natural ANGPTL3 Loss-of-Function
- People born with inactive ANGPTL3 have lifelong low cholesterol
- No apparent adverse effects
- Lower lifetime ASCVD risk
- This is why the target is considered safe — nature already tested it

## Original Analysis (Kill Test)

### Calculation 1: The 14,600-Pill Replacement
- Typical statin patient starts at age 40-50, takes daily for ~40 years
- 40 years × 365 days = 14,600 pills
- CTX310 replaces this with a single 2-hour infusion
- Nobody has quantified this comparison in published literature

### Calculation 2: Adherence-Adjusted Efficacy
- Statin theoretical LDL reduction: 30-50% (average ~40%)
- Long-term adherence rate: 42% at 10 years (Benner et al.)
- Population-level adherence-adjusted LDL reduction: 0.42 × 40% = 16.8%
- CTX310 LDL reduction: 50% with 100% adherence (one-time treatment)
- CTX310 adherence-adjusted advantage: 50% / 16.8% = 2.98x
- A one-shot therapy that's nominally equal in efficacy delivers 3x the real-world benefit because adherence is 100%

### Calculation 3: The Non-Adherence Mortality Cost
- ~93 million Americans take statins (CDC estimate)
- ~50% discontinue within 2 years
- That's ~46.5 million non-adherent patients
- Non-adherence increases cardiovascular mortality risk by ~2x (OR 2.04 for stroke death)
- US cardiovascular deaths: ~928,741/year (AHA 2023)
- If statin non-adherence contributes to even 5-10% excess mortality risk in affected population, the death toll is staggering
- Conservative estimate: tens of thousands of excess cardiovascular deaths/year from statin non-adherence

### Calculation 4: Cost-Effectiveness Break-Even
- If CTX310 is priced like other gene therapies: $100K-$500K per treatment
- Generic statin lifetime cost: $30/yr × 40 years = $1,200
- Looks terrible on raw cost. But...
- Average cost of a single MI hospitalization: $22,000-$53,000 (AHA)
- Average cost of stroke hospitalization: $20,000-$80,000
- One prevented MI/stroke per treatment = break-even at $20K-$53K saved
- For non-adherent patients who would otherwise have an event: CTX310 saves money
- The pharma pricing calculation will be: what's the cost of a 50% lifetime reduction in cardiovascular events?

## Strongest Counterargument
- Phase 1, only 15 patients, 60-day follow-up
- Permanence is unproven — could the edit fade? (Unlikely given mechanism but not established)
- One patient died 179 days post-treatment (deemed unrelated, but only 15 patients)
- Generic statins cost $30/year — this could be orders of magnitude more expensive
- ANGPTL3 isn't the same pathway as statins — additional LDL lowering vs. replacement for some patients
- Off-target editing risks in genome that we won't know about for years

## Limitations
- Follow-up is only 60 days — long-term durability unknown
- No cardiovascular outcomes data (too early, too small)
- Trial population was mixed (FH, hypertriglyceridemia, mixed) — hard to generalize
- No head-to-head comparison with statins
- Pricing not yet determined — cost-effectiveness calculation uses estimates
- Our adherence-adjusted calculation assumes independent effects; real-world interactions are more complex
