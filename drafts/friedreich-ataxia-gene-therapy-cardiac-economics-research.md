# Research: Friedreich Ataxia Cardiac Gene Therapy

## Topic
First-in-human gene therapy trial for the cardiomyopathy of Friedreich ataxia — economics mismatch between existing treatment (Skyclarys) and the one-shot gene therapy approach (AAVrh.10hFXN).

## Primary Sources
1. **Crystal RG et al.** "AAVrh.10hFXN Gene Therapy for the Cardiomyopathy of Friedreich Ataxia: A Nonrandomized Clinical Trial." *JAMA Cardiology*, published online June 17, 2026. DOI: 10.1001/jamacardio.2026.1699
2. **ClinicalTrials.gov NCT05302271** — Phase IA study at Weill Cornell (9 patients, 3 dose cohorts)
3. **ClinicalTrials.gov NCT05445323** — Phase 1B study by Lexeo Therapeutics (8 patients)
4. **Komodo Research Data study** (PMID: 40614535) — 652 FRDA patients vs. 3,260 matched controls; cardiomyopathy OR = 59.2x
5. **PROFA study (Lancet)** — Annual costs €13,620 (payer), €32,679 (societal)
6. **FDA** — Skyclarys approved Feb 2023
7. **Managed Healthcare Executive** — Skyclarys wholesale acquisition cost: $370,000/year

## Key Data
- 17 patients, single IV infusion of AAVrh.10hFXN
- 3 dose cohorts: 1.8×10¹¹, 5.6×10¹¹, 1.2×10¹² vg/kg
- Cardiac frataxin increased in all 8 biopsied patients at 3 months
- LVMI decreased ≥10% in 9/17 patients (53%), stable in 8
- Mean LVMI reduction 11.4% at 12 months in those with elevated LVMI at baseline (n=4)
- Troponin I decreased ≥10% in 15/17 patients (88%)
- 4 serious adverse events (3 prednisone-related, 1 myocarditis at 12 months), all resolved
- Skyclarys: $370,000/year, treats neurological symptoms only, NOT cardiac
- Lexeo (LXEO): market cap $373M, IPO at $11, now $4.75 (down ~57%)
- Dr. Crystal founded Lexeo and is lead author → conflict of interest worth noting

## Original Calculations
1. **Lifetime Skyclarys cost vs. one-shot gene therapy break-even:**
   - Skyclarys WAC: $370,000/year
   - Median onset ~15 years; modern life expectancy ~40-50 years → ~25-35 years of treatment
   - Conservative 25 years: $370,000 × 25 = $9.25M lifetime per patient
   - Gene therapy comparables: Zolgensma $2.1M, Luxturna $850K, Hemgenix $3.5M
   - At $2M-3.5M, gene therapy breaks even in 5.4-9.5 years vs. Skyclarys
   - But Skyclarys doesn't treat the cardiac disease that kills 65% of patients

2. **NNT from trial data:**
   - LVMI ≥10% reduction: 9/17 = 52.9%, NNT ≈ 1.9 (vs. natural history of progressive worsening)
   - Troponin I ≥10% reduction: 15/17 = 88.2%, NNT ≈ 1.1
   - These are exceptional NNTs for a Phase 1 safety trial

3. **Investor math — market cap per addressable patient:**
   - ~5,000 diagnosed US patients; ~65% with cardiomyopathy = ~3,250 addressable
   - Lexeo market cap $373M / 3,250 = $114,769 per potential patient
   - If therapy priced at $2.5M: TAM = $8.1B → market cap implies ~4.6% probability-weighted capture
