## Core Thesis
GLP-1 receptor agonists are the most commercially successful drug class in history. But 25-39% of the weight patients lose is lean mass (muscle, bone, organ tissue), not fat. For older adults, this accelerates sarcopenia — the age-related muscle wasting that kills independence. Four companies with four different molecular strategies just reported Phase 2b data on how to fix it. The comparison tells you more about the future of obesity medicine than any single trial.

## The Problem (sourced)
- **25-39% of total weight lost on GLP-1 drugs is lean mass** — Lancet Diabetes & Endocrinology 2024 meta-analysis across 36-72 weeks of treatment
- **63% of physicians report muscle loss** in GLP-1 patients: 22% frequently, 41% occasionally (Sermo physician survey, n=undisclosed, Nov 2025)
- Semaglutide alone → 23 lbs lost in 26 weeks, **34.5% of which was lean mass** (COURAGE trial semaglutide-only arm, Regeneron data, 999 patients)
- ~15-20M+ Americans estimated on GLP-1s by 2026 (extrapolated from 9M semaglutide prescriptions in 2022 per AJMC, + tirzepatide surge + compounding)
- Nature Reviews Endocrinology 2026 review: "Causes of sarcopenia and frailty in people taking GLP1RAs"

## The Four Approaches

### 1. Regeneron — COURAGE Trial (Phase 2b)
- **Mechanism:** Trevogrumab (anti-GDF8/myostatin antibody) ± garetosmab (anti-activin A)
- **Trial:** 999 patients, randomized, double-blind, 26-week weight loss + 26-week maintenance
- **Results (interim, ~50% of patients at 26 weeks):**
  - Semaglutide alone: 23 lbs lost, 34.5% lean mass loss
  - Trevogrumab + semaglutide (low dose): 50.8% of lost weight was fat (vs lean)
  - Trevogrumab + semaglutide (high dose): 51.3% fat
  - Triplet (trevogrumab + garetosmab + semaglutide): 80.9% lean mass PRESERVED, 27.3% greater fat reduction vs semaglutide alone
- **Safety concerns:** Triplet arm had 28.3% discontinuation rate (vs 4-10% in other arms), severe TEAEs 10.1% (vs 2% semaglutide alone), 2 deaths in triplet group (cardiac arrest in patient with CV history, unknown cause in patient with multiple CV risk factors — Regeneron says no causal link established)
- **Source:** PharmExec, June 3 2025; NCT06299098

### 2. Versanis/Eli Lilly — BELIEVE Trial (Phase 2b)
- **Mechanism:** Bimagrumab (anti-activin type II receptor antibody, blocks ActRII signaling)
- **Trial:** 507 patients, 72 weeks
- **Results:**
  - 22.1% total weight loss
  - **92.8% of weight loss was from fat mass** (vs ~65% for semaglutide alone)
  - Lean mass substantially preserved
  - Safety profile consistent with known drug profiles, no deaths reported
- **Source:** HCPLive, Pharmacy Times (ADA 2025); Nature Medicine publication

### 3. Veru — QUALITY Trial (Phase 2b)
- **Mechanism:** Enobosarm (selective androgen receptor modulator / SARM)
- **Trial:** 168 older adults (focus on sarcopenia risk population)
- **Results:**
  - **71% less lean mass loss** vs semaglutide + placebo
  - **27% more fat loss** vs semaglutide + placebo
  - 3mg dose: 99.1% of weight lost was fat mass (nearly perfect selectivity)
  - Improved physical function (stair climb power preserved)
  - Fewer GI side effects than semaglutide alone
  - Maintenance extension: 46% less weight regain after semaglutide discontinuation
- **Advancing to Phase 3** — FDA End of Phase 2 meeting requested
- **Source:** BioSpace, Veru press releases, EINPresswire

### 4. Exercise + Protein (the counterfactual)
- **Mechanism:** Progressive resistance training + high protein intake (1.2-1.6g/kg/day)
- **Evidence:** Lancet Regional Health study — GLP-1 + strength training preserves lean mass
- **Problem:** Physicians report patients rely on medication without lifestyle changes
- **PubMed review (2026):** "GLP-1RAs primarily cause fat mass loss with minimal lean body mass decline, preserving muscle function and strength" — BUT this is with exercise intervention

## Original Calculation
- If 15M Americans on GLP-1s lose an average of 30 lbs over ~1 year
- And 30% of that is lean mass = 9 lbs of muscle per person
- That's 135 million pounds of collective muscle loss across the US population
- Each pound of muscle burns ~6-10 calories/day at rest → 135M × 7 cal ≈ 1 billion fewer calories burned per day across the GLP-1 population
- For adults over 60 (highest sarcopenia risk), this muscle loss may cross clinical thresholds for grip strength, gait speed, and fall risk

## Limitations
- COURAGE data is interim (50% of patients at 26 weeks) — full data may shift
- Trials use different endpoints, patient populations, and timeframes — not a true head-to-head
- GLP-1 prescription estimates extrapolated; exact 2026 US numbers not publicly available
- Long-term safety of all three pharmacological approaches unknown
- Calorie-per-pound-of-muscle calculation is approximate (range 6-10 cal/lb/day)

## Strongest Counterargument
Recent PubMed review and SEMALEAN study argue that GLP-1 drugs preserve muscle FUNCTION even when lean mass drops — sarcopenic obesity actually decreased from 49% to 33% at 12 months on semaglutide. The body may be shedding excess lean mass that was structurally supporting excess fat, not "useful" muscle. If functional capacity is preserved, the lean mass number may be a misleading biomarker.
