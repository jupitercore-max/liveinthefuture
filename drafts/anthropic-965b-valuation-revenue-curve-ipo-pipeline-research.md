# Research: Anthropic's $965B Valuation and the Three IPOs That Could Break Public Markets

## Journalist: Tomás Reyes (💻 AI Infrastructure / Finance)

## Thesis
Anthropic just surpassed OpenAI at $965B — but the valuation rests on a revenue curve only 5 months old. The run-rate accounting, the gross-vs-net revenue comparison with OpenAI, and the implied growth trajectory all deserve scrutiny. Meanwhile, SpaceX ($1.5T), OpenAI ($852B), and Anthropic ($965B) are collectively preparing to list in 2026, forcing index providers to rewrite inclusion rules and mutual funds to hoard cash. Combined, these three listings could absorb as much capital as all US VC-backed IPOs in the past decade. The article maps the revenue math, the accounting differences, and the systemic market risk.

## Revenue Curve Analysis (ORIGINAL CALCULATION)

Monthly revenue data points:
- Dec 2025: ~$750M/month (annualized $9B)
- Feb 2026: ~$1.17B/month (annualized $14B)
- March 2026: ~$1.6B/month ($4.8B quarterly)
- April 2026: ~$2.5B/month (annualized $30B)
- May 2026: ~$3.9B/month (annualized $47B)

Month-over-month growth rate:
- Dec→Feb (2 months): 56% total = ~24%/month
- Feb→April (2 months): 114% total = ~46%/month
- April→May (1 month): 56%/month

At 40% MoM growth (conservative midpoint):
- June 2026: $5.5B/month ($66B annualized)
- Dec 2026: $33B/month ($396B annualized)
- June 2027: $201B/month ($2.4T annualized)

That last number exceeds Apple's total 2025 revenue. The curve MUST inflect.

The question is: what is the TERMINAL run rate? If growth decelerates to 10% MoM:
- 6 months: $6.9B/month ($83B annualized)
- 12 months: $12.3B/month ($148B annualized)

HSBC's $241B by 2030 implies ~5x current run rate over 4 years, or ~4.5% MoM growth sustained. That's aggressive but at least mathematically possible.

## Gross vs Net Revenue Issue (KEY FINDING)

Reuters Breakingviews explicitly flagged: Anthropic's revenue is GROSS (before distribution partner cuts). OpenAI reports NET (after Microsoft's share).

If Anthropic's distribution partner takes 30% (standard cloud reseller margin), then:
- Anthropic NET run-rate: ~$33B (not $47B)
- Effective revenue multiple: $965B / $33B = ~29x

Still lower than SpaceX's 80x, but materially higher than the 20x headline figure suggests.

This means the "$47B vs $5.7B" comparison between Anthropic and OpenAI overstates the gap. Adjusted:
- Anthropic Q1 net: ~$3.4B
- OpenAI Q1 net: $5.7B
- OpenAI may still be LARGER on a net revenue basis

## Counterargument
The strongest case FOR Anthropic's valuation: coding automation is a $500B+ TAM (developer tools + software services). Claude Code is creating genuine value — companies report 3-10x developer productivity gains. If the productivity is real, the revenue is sustainable because the ROI for enterprises is enormous. This isn't crypto speculation; it's genuine enterprise productivity. The comparison isn't dot-com vaporware — it's early Salesforce or early AWS, where the growth looked unsustainable until it turned out the market was bigger than anyone imagined.

## Limitations
- "Annualized revenue" and "run rate" are self-reported by a private company with no auditor sign-off
- Distribution partner revenue sharing terms not publicly disclosed
- We don't know what % of revenue comes from Claude Code specifically vs API vs consumer
- SpaceX financials visible only through IPO filing; xAI component muddies pre-merger comparisons
- Index rebalancing impact is modeled by Goldman and others but unprecedented at this scale
