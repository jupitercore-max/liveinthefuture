# Research Notes: SK Hynix's $26.5B US Listing

## Core Data Points

### The Offering
- **Amount:** $26.5 billion raised via ADRs at $149/share
- **Ticker:** SKHY on Nasdaq, trading starts Friday July 10
- **Oversubscription:** 7x (Baillie Gifford, Coatue, Situational Awareness Partners indicated up to $7B)
- **Structure:** Each ADR = 1/10 of common share. Seoul closing price: 2,186,000 won ($1,445)
- **Premium:** ~3.1% over Seoul closing price
- **Ranking:** World's second-biggest share sale ever (after SpaceX's $85.7B IPO)
- **Largest US listing by foreign company ever**

### Financial Performance (Q1 2026)
- Revenue: $34.5B (52.58 trillion won) — tripled YoY (+198%)
- Net income: $26.5B (40.34 trillion won) — quintupled (+398%)
- Operating margin: 72% (all-time high)
- Stock: Up 235% in 2026; 680% over 12 months

### Market Position
- HBM market share: 56.4% (per SEC filing) / 61% (per Macquarie) / 64% (per Counterpoint)
- DRAM: #2 (behind Samsung)
- NAND: #2
- SK Hynix P/E: 5.5x forward; Micron P/E: 6.66x forward (the "Korea discount")

### Competitors Performance (for comparison)
- Samsung: stock up 130% in 2026, operating profit up 19x last quarter
- Micron: stock up 250% in 2026, profit margins at ~85% (up from 38% last year)

### Supply Chain / Geopolitics
- Indiana fab: $3.87B investment, West Lafayette, near Purdue. $458M CHIPS Act funding + $500M loans + $700M Indiana incentive. Mass production expected H2 2028. 1,000 jobs. First major HBM packaging facility in US.
- CXMT (ChangXin Memory Technologies): $4.3B Shanghai IPO, book building July 15. Pentagon military blacklist. Q1 revenue 50.8B yuan, up 700% YoY. ~7.7% global DRAM market. Apple reportedly testing CXMT DRAM (FT).
- DDR5 16GB contract prices: quadrupled YoY, expected to rise 55-60% more this quarter (TrendForce)
- BofA: memory = 35-40% of $1.5T cloud/AI infrastructure spending in 2027
- Supply crunch: little additional capacity until mid-2027 at earliest

### Key Quotes
- Daniel Newman (Futurum): "SK Hynix leads on share and Nvidia proximity, Micron competes on power efficiency, U.S. positioning"
- Shay Boloor (Futurum Equities): "the cleanest public-market bet on the HBM bottleneck"
- Jim Cramer: "The bankers are letting everyone know how oversubscribed the SK Hynix deal is. That's a dangerous game."
- SK Hynix: demand to outstrip supply "for at least three more years"

## Original Calculations

### 1. The "Memory Tax" on Every AI Dollar
- BofA: memory = 35-40% of $1.5T cloud/AI spend in 2027 = $525-600B
- SK Hynix HBM share: 56.4% → $296-338B flowing to SK Hynix
- At 72% operating margin → $213-244B operating profit from AI memory alone
- Meta's entire annual electricity bill: $10.6B (from our article #563)
- SK Hynix makes in Q1 NET INCOME ($26.5B) what exceeds Meta's annual power bill

### 2. The Korea Discount
- SK Hynix P/E: 5.5x vs Micron P/E: 6.66x
- SK Hynix has 56.4% HBM share vs Micron's ~15-21%
- If SK Hynix traded at Micron's multiple: market cap ~21% higher
- The listing premium: 3.1% — investors paying for US-listed wrapper

### 3. The Memory Cold War in IPO Form
- SK Hynix: $26.5B in New York (Nasdaq) — July 10
- CXMT: $4.3B in Shanghai — July 15-16
- Combined: $30.8B in memory chip IPOs in one week
- One is America's biggest foreign listing ever; the other is Pentagon-blacklisted
- Apple testing CXMT DRAM while US subsidizes SK Hynix's Indiana fab

### 4. Cyclicality Death Watch
- Memory is historically the most cyclical sector in semis
- Previous supercycles: 1993-95, 1999-2000, 2017-18, 2020-22 — all ended in crashes
- SK Hynix says "at least three more years" — that's what they always say
- Counter: AI demand IS structurally different from PC/smartphone cycles
- But: Samsung already warning HBM3E supply may briefly outpace demand
