# Research: Mivelsiran RNAi Alzheimer's — Zero ARIA, Upstream Math

## Topic
Alnylam's mivelsiran (formerly ALN-APP) is an RNAi therapeutic that silences the APP gene to prevent amyloid production — a fundamentally different approach from approved anti-amyloid antibodies (lecanemab, donanemab) that clear plaques after they form. Phase 1 data presented at AAIC 2026 (July 15) show 0% ARIA across 30 months of dosing. Approved antibodies cause ARIA in 12.6-40% of patients.

## Primary Sources

### 1. Alnylam AAIC 2026 Press Release (July 15, 2026)
- Source: BusinessWire / investors.alnylam.com
- Phase 1 updated data in early-onset Alzheimer's disease (EOAD)
- Single + multiple dose data, up to 30 months of treatment exposure
- Mean max reductions (highest dose): sAPPβ -89.9%, Aβ42 -70.2%
- **ZERO ARIA events** across all dose levels — "no evidence of increased risk of ARIA events"
- Most common AEs: procedural pain and procedural headache (from lumbar puncture)
- No serious or severe AEs related to study drug
- CSF safety labs: no significant elevations of total protein or white blood cells
- Dosing: intrathecal injection, 50mg every 6 months (Q6M) in MAD portion
- Phase 2 APPlauDS Study initiated: Down syndrome-associated AD, ~30 global sites
- Phase 2 cAPPricorn-1: enrollment complete for cerebral amyloid angiopathy (CAA), results expected 2028
- ALN-5288: tau-targeting RNAi, Phase 1 ongoing (initiated Q4 2025)

### 2. Earlier Phase 1 Data (AAIC prior, investors.alnylam.com)
- 20 patients enrolled in three single-dose cohorts
- 75mg single dose: peak reduction sAPPα -84%, sAPPβ -90% at 2 months
- At 6 months: mean reductions sAPPα -55%, sAPPβ -65%
- At 10 months: sAPPα -33%, sAPPβ -39%
- CSF Aβ42 and Aβ40 reductions at 2 months post 75mg dose: Aβ42 -49%, Aβ40 -71%

### 3. Lecanemab (Leqembi) Approval Data
- Source: FDA, CEN.ACS.org, PMC studies
- $26,500/year wholesale acquisition cost
- Biweekly IV infusions (26/year)
- 27% slowing of cognitive decline (CDR-SB -0.45)
- ARIA: 12.6% ARIA-E (edema), ~21% overall ARIA (Phase 3 Clarity-AD)
- Several trial-related deaths
- ICER health benefit price benchmark: $8,900-$21,500 (current price exceeds)

### 4. Donanemab (Kisunla)
- Source: New Scientist, FDA
- $32,000/year
- ~20-40% ARIA rates (New Scientist: "around 20 and 40 per cent")
- Monthly IV infusions

### 5. Down Syndrome + Alzheimer's Disease
- Sources: PMC meta-analyses, JAMA Neurology
- ~6 million people worldwide with Down syndrome
- ~200,000-400,000 in the US
- Trisomy 21 → extra copy of APP gene on chromosome 21 → lifelong APP overproduction
- 100% develop amyloid neuropathology by age 40
- >90% lifetime risk of Alzheimer's dementia (>95% by 7th decade)
- Average age of dementia onset: 54 years
- AD is leading cause of death in adults with DS over 35 (proximate cause in 70% of deaths)
- Life expectancy: ~60 years (20-year gap vs general population)
- **Lecanemab appropriate use recommendations SPECIFICALLY advise against treating DS patients** until safety studies complete — because of ARIA concerns with heavy vascular amyloid
- DS represents the largest population with genetically determined Alzheimer's in the world

### 6. Alnylam RNAi Drug Pricing Reference
- Amvuttra (vutrisiran, ATTR): $119,351/injection, $477,404/year (quarterly SC injection)
- Onpattro (patisiran, ATTR): $451,430-$677,145/year (IV q3w)

## Original Calculation

### The ARIA-Free Economics: Infusion Burden Reduction

**Lecanemab treatment for 100 patients/year:**
- 26 biweekly IV infusions × 100 patients = 2,600 infusion center visits/year
- Each infusion: ~1 hour + observation time
- Quarterly MRI monitoring for ARIA (mandatory): 400 MRIs
- ARIA events in ~21 patients: additional MRIs, potential hospitalization, treatment pause
- True all-in cost per patient: ~$35,000-40,000/year (drug + monitoring + ARIA management)

**Mivelsiran treatment for 100 patients/year (projected):**
- 2 intrathecal injections × 100 patients = 200 procedures/year  
- Each procedure: ~30 min lumbar puncture
- If 0% ARIA: NO mandatory ARIA-monitoring MRIs
- 2,600 visits → 200 visits = **13× reduction in healthcare system burden**

### The Down Syndrome Exclusion Math

- 6 million people with Down syndrome worldwide
- >90% lifetime risk of Alzheimer's dementia
- Lecanemab guidelines explicitly exclude DS patients due to ARIA risk
- Mivelsiran's zero ARIA profile + APPlauDS trial = first viable therapeutic path for DS-AD
- If mivelsiran works: potential addressable population of 5.4 million (90% of 6M)
- At 200,000 US DS patients, even 50% treatment: 100,000 patients otherwise excluded from all approved drugs

## Strongest Counterarguments

1. **Phase 1 sample size is tiny.** Only 20 patients in SAD portion. ARIA rates could emerge at scale. Phase 3 with thousands of patients could reveal different safety profile.
2. **Does stopping production help when plaques already exist?** Mivelsiran prevents new amyloid but doesn't clear existing deposits. In patients with established disease, the damage may be done. The antibodies at least remove what's there.
3. **Intrathecal delivery is a barrier.** Lumbar puncture is more invasive than IV infusion. Patient compliance may suffer. It requires trained specialists, limiting access.
4. **No cognitive benefit data yet.** The Phase 1 measured biomarkers (APP, Aβ42), not cognition. Lecanemab has proven (modest) cognitive benefit. The gap between biomarker improvement and clinical improvement has been the graveyard of Alzheimer's drugs.
5. **Pricing unknown but likely high.** Alnylam's ATTR drugs cost $477K/year. Even at 2 doses/year, mivelsiran could be priced 10-18× lecanemab.
