## 1. 🔍 General Editor — Overall Quality, Structure, Engagement
**Score: 9/10**

**Strengths:**

- Structure follows the research doc's plan perfectly: installed base → pricing cliff → district math → national math → Title I impact → two-tier risk → counterargument → limitations → action → bottom line
