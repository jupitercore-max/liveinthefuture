# Research: SpaceX Starlink Pricing Dispute with Pentagon

## Primary Source
- Reuters, May 26, 2026: "Pentagon spars with SpaceX over Starlink price hike during Iran war"
  - URL: https://www.reuters.com/business/aerospace-defense/pentagon-spars-with-spacex-over-starlink-price-hike-during-iran-war-2026-05-26/
  - Key details: SpaceX raised Starlink pricing on LUCAS suicide drones from $5,000/terminal/month to $25,000/terminal/month during the Iran war

## LUCAS Drone Specs
- Full name: Low-cost Uncrewed Combat Attack System (FLM-136 LUCAS)
- Manufacturer: SpektreWorks
- Unit cost: ~$35,000 (some sources say $30,000)
- Range: 500 miles / 403 miles (various sources)
- Endurance: 6 hours loitering
- Payload: 40 lb warhead
- First combat use: February 2026, Operation Epic Fury
- Reverse-engineered from Iran's Shahed-136
- Part of the $1 billion Drone Dominance Program

## Original Analysis Potential
1. **Cost-per-drone markup calculation**: 
   - LUCAS unit cost: $35,000
   - Original Starlink fee: $5,000/month
   - New Starlink fee: $25,000/month
   - If drone lives for one mission (1-6 hours), the Starlink subscription fee of $25K per month is 71% of unit cost
   - Old total: $35,000 + $5,000 = $40,000 (Starlink = 12.5% of total)
   - New total: $35,000 + $25,000 = $60,000 (Starlink = 41.7% of total)
   - Pentagon said this "almost doubled the cost of each LUCAS drone" (from $30K + $5K = $35K to $30K + $25K = $55K per Reuters)

2. **Monopoly revenue extraction**:
   - 3,500 new Starshield subs at even base pricing ($5K/month) = $17.5M/month = $210M/year
   - 100 aviation tier at $25K/month = $2.5M/month = $30M/year
   - Direct-to-cell: $500M launch + $100M/month = $1.7B first year
   - Total potential annual revenue boost from military: $200M-$1.7B+

3. **Single-vendor dependency risk**:
   - SpaceX has 60%+ of all orbital satellites
   - No comparable alternative exists (Pentagon's own admission)
   - 20.9% of SpaceX revenue from US government
   - Classic monopsony/monopoly dynamic
