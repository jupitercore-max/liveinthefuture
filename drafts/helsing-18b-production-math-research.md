# Helsing $18B Valuation: Production Math Research

## Core Thesis
Helsing's $18B valuation at current production rates (~450 drones/month) is pricing in a factory that doesn't exist yet. The math only works at 2,500/month — a 5.6x ramp from current output.

## Original Calculation: The Production Ramp $18B Is Pricing In

### Step 1: What revenue does $18B require?
At peer multiples:
- Anduril's 17x: $18B / 17 = $1.06B needed
- Shield AI's 25x: $18B / 25 = $720M needed
- Palantir's 50x: $18B / 50 = $360M needed
- Median defense-tech growth multiple (~20x): $900M needed

### Step 2: What does current production generate?
- 450 drones/month × 12 = 5,400/year
- Unit economics uncertain, but contract math:
  - €268M Bundestag contract / quantity unknown
  - Defense Express mentions Ukraine order separate from Bundestag
  - Reasonable HX-2 unit price estimate: €30,000-50,000 (positioned below conventional munitions, above FPV drones)
  - At €40,000/unit: 5,400 × €40,000 = €216M/year (~$234M)
  - At €50,000/unit: 5,400 × €50,000 = €270M/year (~$293M)
  - Plus Altra software licensing and services

### Step 3: What does the target rate generate?
- 2,500/month × 12 = 30,000/year
- At €40,000/unit: 30,000 × €40,000 = €1.2B/year (~$1.3B)
- At €50,000/unit: 30,000 × €50,000 = €1.5B/year (~$1.63B)
- NOW the valuation works at Anduril-like multiples

### Key insight: $18B prices in a 5.6x production ramp
- Current: 450/month = $234-293M revenue capacity
- Target: 2,500/month = $1.3-1.63B revenue capacity
- The valuation skips over the current production state entirely
- Investors are betting on the factory of tomorrow, not today
