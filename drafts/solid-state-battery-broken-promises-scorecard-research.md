- **Does anyone care?** YES — solid-state batteries are the most-hyped EV technology. $10B+ invested across industry. Every major automaker has staked strategic bets on them.

## Original Calculation
Every company in the solid-state space has either (a) already missed its production deadline, (b) downscoped from "mass production" to "pilot production" or "validation vehicles," or (c) has a deadline that hasn't arrived yet. The weighted average slippage across all companies with expired deadlines is approximately 4.3 years.

If we apply this average to current 2027 promises, the implied actual delivery date is ~2031 — which aligns perfectly with CATL chairman Zeng's "mass production around 2030" estimate. The most credible company (CATL) is the one making the most conservative promise.

## Strongest Counterargument
Unlike previous hype cycles, there IS tangible infrastructure being built: QuantumScape has an actual pilot line, Idemitsu is constructing a factory, ProLogium has shipped 600K cells, Greater Bay has A-samples. These are physical artifacts, not PowerPoint slides. The industry may genuinely be at an inflection point — just 4-5 years later than originally promised. The question is whether "pilot production" translates to "mass production" or becomes another plateau.

## Limitations
- Most energy density and cycle life claims are company-reported, not independently verified (Donut Lab case shows why this matters)
- "A-sample" ≠ mass production; pilot lines produce hundreds of cells, not millions
- Chinese companies' timelines may be more aggressive due to government subsidy pressure
- QuantumScape has been pre-revenue for 14 years; market cap of $4.2B is entirely based on future promises
- Different SSB chemistries (sulfide, oxide, polymer, composite) face different scaling challenges; lumping them together oversimplifies
- My slippage calculation uses publicly stated dates which may not represent internal targets

## What You Can Do (Actionable Insights)
- **EV buyers:** Don't wait for solid-state. Current lithium-ion (LFP, NMC) delivers 300-500 miles today. SSBs won't materially change buying decisions before ~2030.
- **Investors:** Apply "CATL rule" — the company with the most conservative timeline is usually the most accurate. Discount any SSB startup's production date by 3-5 years.
- **Follow the factories, not the press releases.** Idemitsu actually building a plant is more meaningful than 10 announcements about energy density records.
