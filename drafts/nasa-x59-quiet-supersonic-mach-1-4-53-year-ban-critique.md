# Critique: NASA X-59 Quiet Supersonic (Article #416)

## Dimensions

### Original Contribution: 8/10
Novel calculation: the 90% market lockout from the overland ban, assembled by comparing transoceanic vs. overland viable city pairs (20 vs. 200+). The $247M-to-$37.5B ratio (0.66% of Concorde's development cost) and the ICCT demand multiplier applied to Boom's pricing ($48B market) are original assemblages. Individual data points are available; the novelty is in combining them into a coherent economic picture nobody has published.
