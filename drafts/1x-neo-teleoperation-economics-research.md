# Research: 1X NEO Teleoperation Economics

## Story Angle
1X just opened America's first vertically integrated humanoid robot factory in Hayward, CA (April 30, 2026). They sold 10,000 units at $20K each in 5 days. But the robots still require human teleoperators for complex tasks. The novel contribution: calculating the hidden labor economics of teleoperation at scale, and identifying the autonomy threshold where the subscription model ($499/month) breaks even.

## Novel Contribution
Calculate: At $20/hr operator cost, how many operator-hours per robot per day can 1X sustain before the subscription model ($499/month ≈ $16.63/day) goes negative? Answer: 0.83 hours/day maximum. That's 50 minutes. If a consumer robot needs more than 50 minutes of human help per day, 1X loses money on every subscription unit.

At 10,000 robots:
- Subscription revenue: 10,000 × $499/month × 12 = $59.9M/year
- If each robot needs 1 hr/day of operator time at $20/hr: 10,000 × 365 × $20 = $73M/year in labor alone
- Break-even operator time: ~49 min/day per robot
- At 100,000 robots (2027 target): $599M/year revenue vs potentially $730M in operator costs

The key variable: What % of tasks can NEO handle autonomously? If 90% autonomous, operator cost drops to $7.3M. If only 70% autonomous, it's $21.9M. The autonomy improvement rate IS the business model.

## Strongest Counterargument
Tesla's Autopilot followed the same playbook: ship hardware, collect data through consumer deployment, improve AI over-the-air. Autopilot went from requiring constant human attention to handling most highway driving in ~5 years. If 1X's teleoperation data flywheel works similarly, today's privacy cost and operator cost are temporary investments, not permanent liabilities. The $20K buyer isn't being exploited — they're funding the R&D that makes the $5K version work.

## Limitations
- 1X doesn't disclose what % of tasks require teleoperation
- Operator wages are estimated ($20/hr) — actual wages unknown (could be lower if offshore)
- We don't know how many operators 1X currently employs
- Autonomy improvement rate is a projection, not a measured datapoint
- $499/month subscription economics may include margins for other costs (cloud, support, updates)
