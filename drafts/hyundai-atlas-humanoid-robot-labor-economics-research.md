## Original Calculations

### Calculation 1: Robot Fleet Capital Investment & Payback
- 25,000 × $130K = $3.25 billion capital investment
- 25,000 × $9,500/year maintenance = $237.5M annual maintenance
- If each robot replaces labor equivalent of 3 workers:
  - 75,000 worker-equivalents
  - At ~₩90M/year (~$65K) per Korean auto worker (fully loaded)
  - Annual labor equivalent: 3 × $65K = $195K saved per robot
  - Year 1 cost: $130K capital + $9,500 maintenance = $139,500
  - Payback: $139,500 / ($195,000 - $9,500) = 0.75 years (9 months)
  - More conservative (2 worker replacement): $130,000 / ($130,000 - $9,500) = 1.08 years

### Calculation 2: Strike Cost vs. Robot Fleet Cost
- Partial strike: ~$134M for Mon-Wed (3 days of 4-hour stoppages)
- That's ~$44.7M per partial-strike-day
- $3.25B fleet cost / $44.7M/day = 72.7 partial-strike-days
- Or: 73 days of 4-hour strikes would fund the entire 25,000-robot fleet
- Historical context: Hyundai workers have struck in 26 of the past 38 years

### Calculation 3: The Robot Density Ceiling
- S. Korea stock: ~392K robots (8.4% of 4.664M global)
- Korean manufacturing employment: ~4.1M
- Current: 1,220/10K
- Adding 25K humanoids: (392K + 25K) / 4.1M × 10K = 1,281/10K (+5% increase)
- But humanoids are categorically different from fixed industrial arms

### Calculation 4: Global Humanoid Factory Fleet Projection
- Hyundai: 25,000 by 2028
- XPeng: 12,000/year target (1,000/month)
- Morgan Stanley China: 50K units 2026, 100K in 2027
- Tesla Optimus: production starting late 2026
- Total committed for auto factories by 2028: potentially 200K+ units worldwide

## Novel Analysis: The Union's Rational Fear
Union's calculation: one $145K robot operating 24/7 costs less than one Korean auto worker over 2 years, but does the work of three. This isn't luddism — it's arithmetic. The break-even math is so favorable that the only question is execution, not economics. The union's strategy (demand structural protections now, before robots arrive) is the rational response when the math guarantees displacement unless the contract prevents it.
