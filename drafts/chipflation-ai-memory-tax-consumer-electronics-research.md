# Chipflation: AI Memory Tax on Consumer Electronics — Research Notes

## Topic
Morgan Stanley coined "chipflation" to describe how AI data center memory demand is spiking consumer electronics prices across the board. Apple just raised prices on MacBook Neo (+$100), iPad (+$100), iPad Mini (+$100), Apple TV (+$70), Vision Pro, and HomePod. Sony, Lenovo, Microsoft, and Meta all raised prices too. Original calculation: Apple's $100 MacBook Neo increase maps almost exactly to the DRAM+NAND cost delta.

## Original Calculation: The $100 Math

MacBook Neo components (estimated):
- 8GB LPDDR5 memory: was ~$15 (mid-2025 contract), now ~$90 (6x)
- 256GB NAND flash: was ~$18, now ~$45 (2.5x)
- Total memory/storage: was ~$33, now ~$135
- **Delta: $102** — matches Apple's $100 price increase almost exactly

For a standard $900 Windows notebook with 16GB DDR5 + 512GB NAND:
- DRAM: was ~$30, now ~$120-180
- NAND: was ~$35, now ~$70-100
- Total: was ~$65, now ~$190-280
- Increase: $125-215 (13-24% of $900)
- TrendForce says prices need to rise up to 40% (includes CPU increases)
