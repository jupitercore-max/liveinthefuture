# Research: OpenAI Georgia 3.2 GW Facility

## Primary Sources
- Reuters (Jul 22, 2026): Southern Co subsidiary signs 25-year power deal with OpenAI for Effingham County, Georgia. 3,200 MW demand. OpenAI to provide up to 1,000 MW back during peak demand.
- WSJ (Jul 22, 2026): OpenAI's planned cloud spending hits $750B. 3.2 GW site in Savannah Gateway Industrial Hub, Effingham County. OpenAI hiring CTO of Computing Capacity (Uday Ruddarraju). Land already acquired. Partner selection in process.
- Georgia Power's $16.3B expansion approved by PSC (Dec 2025). 80% of projected demand from AI data centers. $50-60B lifetime cost.
- Zacks (Dec 22, 2025): Georgia Power approves 50% capacity expansion. Critics warn of stranded costs.
- NNSA (Jul 20, 2026): Amentum selected for 1 GW AI data center at Savannah River Site, ~2 GW on-site power generation (gas bridging to nuclear).
- EIA data: Georgia net summer capacity 39,746 MW, total retail sales 150,003,965 MWh (2024).
- Plant Vogtle: 4,536 MW nameplate, largest US nuclear plant. Units 3-4 cost $36.8B, 11 years to build.
- Average Georgia household: 1,074 kWh/month (solarreviews).

## Original Calculations
1. One facility = 2.5M homes: 3,200 MW × 0.95 capacity factor × 8,760 hours = 26.6 TWh/year. Average US household: 10,500 kWh/year. 26.6 TWh / 10.5 MWh = ~2,533,714 homes.
2. Georgia share: 26.6 TWh / 150 TWh total Georgia retail sales = 17.8% of state consumption from ONE facility.
3. Vogtle comparison: OpenAI's demand (3,200 MW) = 70.6% of Plant Vogtle's total capacity (4,536 MW). Building equivalent nuclear: Units 3-4 (2,234 MW) cost $36.8B; at that rate 3,200 MW ≈ $52.7B.
4. Reverse flow: 1,000 MW during peak demand. Georgia Power summer peak ~20,000 MW. OpenAI = 5% of peak capacity. At wholesale $40-80/MWh, 1,000 MW × 500 peak hours = $20-40M/year.
5. 25-year electricity: at $0.05/kWh industrial rate, 26.6 TWh × $0.05 × 25 = $33.3B. At $0.06: $39.9B.
6. Effingham County pop ~68,000 (2024 est). OpenAI facility could power 37× the county's population equivalent in homes.
