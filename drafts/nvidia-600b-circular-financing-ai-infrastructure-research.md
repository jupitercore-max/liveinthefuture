# Research: Nvidia's $600B Circular Financing Bet

## Core Story
Nvidia is in talks to guarantee ~$250B in financing for OpenAI's lease of a 10-gigawatt data center project in Ohio, built by SoftBank's SB Energy subsidiary. Separately, Nvidia is discussing financing OpenAI's chip purchases worth up to $350B. Total: ~$600B where the chip maker finances its own customer to buy its own chips.

## Original Analysis: The Circular Financing Problem

### Calculation 1: Nvidia as Its Own Customer's Bank
- Nvidia's TTM revenue: ~$130B (FY2026)
- $250B guarantee = ~1.9x Nvidia's annual revenue
- $350B chip financing = ~2.7x Nvidia's annual revenue
- Total exposure: ~$600B = ~4.6x annual revenue
- For comparison: JPMorgan's total assets are ~$4T with $700B in loans. Nvidia would be taking on bank-level exposure without being a bank.

### Calculation 2: The 10 GW Campus in Context
- US total installed generating capacity: ~1,290 GW (EIA 2024)
- 10 GW = 0.78% of ALL US power capacity
- Largest US power plant (Grand Coulee Dam): 6.8 GW
- This single data center would need 47% more power than America's largest dam
- At ~3 kW per GPU: 10 GW supports ~3.3 million GPUs running simultaneously
- At ~$30K per GPU (B200 pricing): $99B in chips for full buildout

### Calculation 3: Industry-Wide AI Power Demand
- Microsoft projects: 50 GW by 2028
- Current US data center power: ~35 GW (2025 estimate)
- Nuclear contracts by Big 4 tech: 10+ GW
- This Ohio project alone: 10 GW
- Total announced AI power projects in 2026: well over 100 GW
- US added ~25 GW of new generation capacity in 2025
- Gap: AI companies want to add more capacity in 3 years than the US built in 4 years

### Calculation 4: When the Guarantor IS the Vendor
- Historical parallel: GE Capital's role in financing GE equipment purchases
- GE Capital grew to $500B+ in assets, became GE's largest profit center
- When the financial crisis hit, GE Capital nearly destroyed the parent company
- If Nvidia is guaranteeing $250B in leases for facilities that exist to buy Nvidia chips, and the AI revenue model doesn't materialize, Nvidia is exposed on both the financing AND the chip demand side
- This is double-correlated risk: the same event (AI revenue shortfall) triggers both the lease default AND the chip demand collapse

## Strongest Counterargument
Nvidia isn't actually lending money — it's providing a guarantee. If OpenAI generates revenue from the compute, the guarantee is never called. And Nvidia has $44B+ in cash. The guarantee structure may include collateral (the data center itself, the chips, the land). Also, Nvidia's margins are 60%+ on these chips, so even substantial write-downs wouldn't eliminate profitability. The chips have value as physical assets.

## Limitations
- Deal terms are reported but not finalized. Structure may change significantly.
- We don't know the specific guarantee structure (partial vs full, collateral, duration).
- Nvidia's actual balance sheet exposure may be much lower than the headline guarantee number.
- OpenAI's revenue trajectory is uncertain; $13.4B ARR as of early 2026 but unprofitable.
- Japan's $33B power funding terms are unclear.
