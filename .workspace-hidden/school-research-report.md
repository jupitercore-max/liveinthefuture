## 1. CASTILLEJA SCHOOL

**Girls-only | Grades 6–12 | Palo Alto, CA**
Website: [castilleja.org](https://www.castilleja.org)

### Tuition (2025–26)
- **$62,400** (all grades)
- Sources: [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Castilleja_School), [Castilleja Tuition Page](https://www.castilleja.org/admission/tuition-and-affordability)

### Enrollment & Structure
- **Total enrollment: ~426** (Wikipedia)
- **Upper School (9–12): 235** students; **Class of 2026: 51** students
- Source: [Castilleja Upper School Profile 2025–26 PDF](https://resources.finalsite.net/images/v1756157073/castilleja/zqwz4gakfwje0bvern5d/CastillejaUpperSchoolProfile25-26website.pdf)

### Student-Teacher Ratio & Class Size
- **Student-teacher ratio: 6:1**
- **Average class size: 14** (upper school), **15** (Wikipedia overall)
- Source: Wikipedia, School Profile PDF

### Math Curriculum
- **Courses offered (upper school):** Algebra II & Trig (Honors), Euclidean & Analytic Geometry (Honors), Introductory Calculus (Honors), AT Calculus, AT Calculus with Infinite Series, AT Linear Algebra, AT Multivariable Calculus, AT Statistics I & II
- **Math goes up through Multivariable Calculus and Linear Algebra** — very strong for a school this size
- **Math acceleration:** The course sequence implies differentiation — honors tracks available; AT (Advanced Topics) courses are the most rigorous, generally for juniors/seniors
- **Competition teams:** Castilleja has an award-winning FIRST Robotics team (Team 1700 Gatorbotics). No specific mention of MATHCOUNTS or AMC teams found, but the school has a strong STEM culture
- Source: School Profile PDF

### College Matriculation (Class of 2025)
- **97% enroll in 4-year colleges; ~5% choose a gap year**
- Class of 2025 acceptances include (bold = at least one student matriculated): **Harvard, Yale, Princeton, Stanford, Brown, Cornell, Dartmouth, Duke, Georgetown, Northwestern, UPenn, Rice, Vanderbilt, WashU, University of Chicago, UC Berkeley, UCLA, MIT (athlete recruited)**, and many more
- Very strong placement across Ivy League, elite LACs, and top UCs
- Source: [School Profile PDF](https://resources.finalsite.net/images/v1756157073/castilleja/zqwz4gakfwje0bvern5d/CastillejaUpperSchoolProfile25-26website.pdf)

### AP/IB Offerings
- **Castilleja does NOT teach AP curriculum.** Instead, they offer proprietary "Advanced Topics" (AT) courses designed by their faculty, "grounded in research into how adolescent girls learn best."
- Students may **sit for AP exams** that align with AT courses: AP Calculus AB/BC, AP Statistics, AP English Language & Literature, AP Computer Science A, AP Chinese, AP French, AP Spanish, AP Music Theory, AP 2-D Art & Design/Drawing
- **AP Exam results (May 2025):** 104 students took 181 AP exams — **79% scored 4 or 5; 93% scored 3+**
- **No IB program**
- Source: School Profile PDF

### National Merit Scholars
- Not published in the profile. Data not found.

### Endowment
- **~$75 million** (per Bloomberg/bond documents reporting)
- The school is undergoing a $198 million campus expansion, funded partly by $106M in bonds
- Source: [Bloomberg via LiveMint](http://livemint.com/news/exclusive-palo-alto-girls-school-borrows-106-million-for-campus/amp-11727204382507.html)

### Financial Aid
- **1 in 5 students (20%) receives tuition assistance**
- **Annual budget: over $4 million**
- Awards range from $7,500 to $65,250
- Need-blind in admissions
- Source: [Castilleja Tuition Page](https://www.castilleja.org/admission/tuition-and-affordability)

### Acceptance Rate / Selectivity
- **Not publicly disclosed.** Castilleja is known to be highly selective; estimates range ~20–25% based on admissions consultant reports
- Source: Various admissions consultant sites

### Niche.com Grade & Ranking
- **Overall: A+**
- Ranked **#18 Best Private High School in the US** and **#2 Best All-Girls High School in the US** (Niche 2023)
- In 2026 Bay Area rankings: **#5 (or #6) among private high schools** per Niche methodology
- Source: [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Castilleja_School), [IvyMax rankings summary](https://ivymax.com/blog/cpp/san-francisco-bay-area-private-high-schools/)

### GreatSchools Score
- GreatSchools does not typically rate private schools with a numbered score. **N/A for Castilleja.**

### Diversity Stats
- **75% of students identify as students of color**
- **22% receive tuition assistance**
- Source: School Profile PDF 2025–26

### Entry Points & Admissions
- **Main entry points:** Grade 6, Grade 9 (largest intake)
- Some spaces occasionally open in other grades
- **Testing:** ISEE required
- **Application deadline:** January (via Ravenna); decisions in March
- Source: [Castilleja Admissions](https://www.castilleja.org/admission/applying-to-castilleja)

### Unique Programs / Notable Features
- **Bourn Idea Lab** — First FabLab@School in a US school, in collaboration with Stanford University
- **Award-winning FIRST Robotics team** (Team 1700 Gatorbotics)
- **Global Investigator Program** — endowed travel-based learning
- **ACE Center** — Awareness, Compassion, Engagement leadership program
- Custom AT curriculum replacing AP courses
- **Only non-sectarian all-girls 6–12 school in the Bay Area**
- Massive $198M campus expansion approved in 2022
- Founded in 1907 — 119 years of history
- Source: School Profile PDF, Wikipedia

### Notable Alumni
- **Amy Chow** — Olympic gold medalist (gymnastics), pediatrician
- **Grace Slick** — singer/songwriter (Jefferson Airplane)
- **Penny Pritzker** — U.S. Secretary of Commerce, Pritzker family philanthropist
- **Pansy Ho** — Macau casino operator/heiress
- **Josie Maran** — actress/model
- **Nancy Ditz** — Olympic marathon runner
- **Tori Anthony** — pole vaulter
- Source: [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Castilleja_School)

### Strongest Case AGAINST
- **All-girls environment** limits co-ed social development and may not suit every learner
- **Extremely small graduating class (~51)** = fewer course offerings, limited athletic teams, narrow social circle
- **No traditional AP curriculum** — while AT courses are rigorous, some families worry about transcript legibility for college admissions (though results suggest this isn't an issue)
- **Ongoing massive construction** ($198M expansion) — disruption to campus life
- **Historic neighborhood controversy** over expansion (traffic, zoning disputes with Palo Alto neighbors)
- **Smallest school on this list** — fewer extracurricular options, less competitive athletics

- **$7.9 million** financial aid budget (2025–26) — **up from $7.3M** the prior year
- **Meets 100% of demonstrated need** for all enrolled students

- Need-blind admissions
- Source: [Menlo Tuition Page](https://www.menloschool.org/admissions/affording-menlo/), School Profile

- **Total enrollment: ~1,216** across all divisions (PK–12)
- **Prep (9–12): ~625–629** students
- **Middle School (6–8): ~215**, Lower School (1–5): ~323, PK/K: ~107
- Source: [Private School Review](https://www.privateschoolreview.com/sacred-heart-schools-atherton-profile), [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sacred_Heart_Schools,_Atherton)

### Student-Teacher Ratio & Class Size
- **Student-teacher ratio: 8:1**
- **Average class size: 15**
- **146 classroom teachers; 73% hold advanced degrees (Prep: 89%)**
- Source: Private School Review, Wikipedia

### Math Curriculum
- Standard college prep math sequence through AP level
- **AP courses offered in math likely include AP Calculus AB/BC, AP Statistics** (school offers 28 AP courses total — see below)
- Religious studies requirement (4 years) is unique and takes schedule space
- No specific information found on math competition teams
- Source: [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sacred_Heart_Schools,_Atherton)

### College Matriculation
- **Class of 2025: 160 graduates** — with **$12.2 million in scholarship earnings**
- Specific college list not found in public sources; however, SHP is known for strong placements at:
  - Stanford, UC Berkeley, USC, UCLA, Boston College, Georgetown, Notre Dame, and other Catholic institutions

- SHP has traditionally sent students across a broad range of colleges; as a Catholic school, strong representation at Jesuit/Catholic universities

- **No IB program**
- Source: [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sacred_Heart_Schools,_Atherton)

### National Merit Scholars
- Not published in available sources

### Endowment
- Not publicly disclosed. The school's 2008 capital campaign raised **$101 million**. The campus has received massive investment ($79,000 sq ft Campbell building in 2019, Homer Science Center 2009)
- Source: [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sacred_Heart_Schools,_Atherton)

### Financial Aid
- **30% of students receive financial assistance**
- Non-Tuition Related Financial Aid (NTRFA) program covers textbooks, supplies, retreats, meals, etc.
- Source: [Private School Review](https://www.privateschoolreview.com/sacred-heart-schools-atherton-profile), [SHP Tuition page](https://www.shschools.org/admission/tuition-and-affordability)

### Acceptance Rate / Selectivity

- One of the top Catholic PK–12 schools in the country

### GreatSchools Score
- **N/A** for private schools

### Diversity Stats
- **15% students of color** (Private School Review) — this is significantly lower than all other schools on this list
- **58% Catholic** (as of 2017–18)
- Source: [Private School Review](https://www.privateschoolreview.com/sacred-heart-schools-atherton-profile), Wikipedia

### Entry Points & Admissions
- **Natural entry points:** Preschool/Kindergarten, Grade 6 (middle school expands by ~30), Grade 9 (prep expands by ~90 students)
- **Testing:** ISEE for middle school; HSPT (High School Placement Test) for Prep
- **Application deadline:** January 6
- Source: [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sacred_Heart_Schools,_Atherton), SHP admissions

### Unique Programs / Notable Features
- **Only PK–12 school on one campus on the Peninsula** — 63-acre campus (the largest on this list by far)
- **28 AP courses** — the most traditional AP program of any school on this list
- **Religious studies required** all four years (but described as "ecumenical and interfaith")
- **Strong athletics program** — CCS champions in multiple sports; dominant in water polo, basketball, soccer
- **Valpo Bowl** — annual rivalry football game vs. Menlo School for charity
- **Catholic mission** with Society of the Sacred Heart governance; traditions include Congé (surprise day off), Goûter
- **Campbell Academic & Arts Building** (2019) — 79,000 sq ft with innovation lab, TV/radio station, performing arts
- **Homer Science Center** (2009) — LEED Platinum certified, 700-seat auditorium
- **Olympic-size swimming pool**
- **Sustainable agriculture course** — students cultivate vegetables and butcher a goat
- Part of global **Network of Sacred Heart schools** (200+ schools worldwide)
- Source: [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sacred_Heart_Schools,_Atherton)

### Notable Alumni
- **Tierna Davidson** (SHP '16) — US Women's National Soccer Team, Stanford soccer
- **Abby Dahlkemper** (SHP '11) — US Women's National Soccer Team
- Strong athletics pipeline
- Source: [Private School Review](https://www.privateschoolreview.com/sacred-heart-schools-atherton-profile), Wikipedia

### Strongest Case AGAINST
- **Lowest diversity of any school on this list** — only 15% students of color vs. 75% at Castilleja and 55% at Menlo
- **Religious requirement** — 4 years of religious studies is mandatory; Catholic traditions permeate school life (may not suit secular families)
- **Lower tuition ($47,826) suggests different resource model** but still a significant investment
- **Largest school on this list (1,216 students)** — less intimate than competitors
- **Less prestigious college placement perception** compared to Castilleja/Menlo/Nueva — fewer published top-20 placements
- **The 25% acceptance rate is for the broadest possible pool** including PK applicants; high school selectivity data is harder to parse
- **Athletics-heavy culture** may not suit every student

## 5. THE NUEVA SCHOOL

**Coed | Grades PreK–12 | Hillsborough & San Mateo, CA**
Website: [nuevaschool.org](https://www.nuevaschool.org)

### Tuition (2025–26)
| Grade | Tuition + Fees + Trips |
|---|---|
| Pre-K | $42,060 |
| Kindergarten | $46,950 |
| 1st | $47,010 |
| 2nd–4th | $47,410 |
| **5th–8th (Middle School)** | **$65,760** |
| **9th–12th (Upper School)** | **$66,960** |

- **The most expensive school on this list** for middle and upper school
- Source: [Nueva Affording page](https://www.nuevaschool.org/admissions/affording-nueva)

### Enrollment & Structure
- **Total enrollment: ~930** (PreK–12)
- **Two campuses:** Lower/Middle School in Hillsborough (Crocker Mansion campus, 36 acres total combined), Upper School in San Mateo (Bay Meadows)
- Upper School opened in 2014 — relatively new high school program (first graduating class ~2018)
- Source: [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Nueva_School)

### Student-Teacher Ratio & Class Size
- **Student-teacher ratio: 6.5:1**
- **Class sizes: 15–18**
- **140 full-time faculty; ~80% hold advanced degrees**
- Source: Wikipedia

### Math Curriculum
- **Integrated core math curriculum** with advanced elective classes in upper school including:
  - **Multivariable Calculus, Complex Analysis** (this is college-level — exceptional)
- **Out-of-grade math classes available** for students who exceed grade-level requirements — explicit acceleration
- **No honors or AP designation** but school claims "most core classes meet or exceed AP rigor"
- **Competition culture is strong:** Nueva students have **won the U.S. Mathematical Olympiad** and **competed at the International Mathematics Olympiad (IMO)**
- **Girls x Math** chapter connects high school mentors with elementary students
- **MathWorks Math Modeling Challenge** finalists
- Source: [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Nueva_School), [nuevaschool.org](https://www.nuevaschool.org/academics/upper-school)

### College Matriculation
- The school's high school is young (first class graduated ~2018), so data is more limited than 100+ year-old institutions
- Known for strong placements at top universities — students admitted to MIT, Stanford, Ivy League schools
- **No publicly available comprehensive matriculation list found**
- More than 85% of curriculum is elective-based, producing distinctive applicants
- Average test scores: **ACT: 34/36, SAT Reading/Writing: 743/800, SAT Math: 760/800** — these are exceptional
- Source: [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Nueva_School)

### AP/IB Offerings
- **Nueva does NOT offer AP or honors courses**
- The school claims most core classes "meet or exceed the rigor of Advanced Placement"
- No IB program
- Students are not prevented from taking AP exams independently
- Source: [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Nueva_School)

### National Merit Scholars
- **Nearly 10% of the senior class won National Merit Scholarships in 2019**
- Source: [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Nueva_School)

### Endowment
- Not publicly disclosed. The school was founded with significant backing from **W. Clement Stone** (billionaire philanthropist). The Hillsborough campus is the former **W.H. Crocker Skyfarm mansion** donated to the school.
- Estimated to be substantial given the two-campus expansion and $8.6M annual financial aid budget

### Financial Aid
- **20% of students receive financial assistance**
- **$8.6 million** annual financial aid budget
- **100% of demonstrated need met with grants (not loans)**
- **Free tuition** for families earning under $150,000/year (as of 2024)

### Strongest Case AGAINST
- **Requires IQ testing / gifted identification** — not all bright kids test "gifted" on IQ assessments; this creates a gatekeeping barrier
- **Most expensive school on this list** ($66,960/year for upper school)
- **Athletics rated C+** by Niche — weakest sports program of any school on this list by far
- **Young high school program** (first class graduated ~2018) — limited alumni network, shorter track record for college placement
- **No AP courses, no letter grades until 10th grade** — extremely non-traditional; some colleges/universities may have less familiarity with Nueva transcripts
- **Two-campus model** means upper school students are in San Mateo, not on the beautiful Hillsborough campus
- **"Gifted" label can create identity pressure** — defining children primarily by their cognitive testing
- **Farthest from Menlo Park** — Hillsborough/San Mateo is a meaningful commute
- **Niche sports rating of C+** means student-athletes have limited competitive options
