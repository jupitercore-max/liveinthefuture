## Collecting Philosophy

### Core Principles
1. **"One in, one out"** (currently more like "two out, one in")
   - Applies to everything: watches, cars, surfboards, T-shirts, shoes
   - Originated from his wife Kristopher Dukes
   - "I don't want to be controlled by stuff"

2. **12-watch limit**
   - Stores watches in two Pelican cases with foam inserts (6 slots each)
   - Anything beyond 12 means something has to go

3. **Story over acquisition**
   - "Just being inquisitive for the sake of acquisition is not interesting to me"
   - Wants to understand the "why" of things, not just "how" and "what"
   - Not a "flipper" — doesn't trade frequently

4. **Unrestored originals only**
   - Whether watches, cars, or architecture
   - "If something's restored I'm generally not interested in it"
   - Pieces must have a story and original character

5. **Use everything you own**
   - "I don't want watches I don't wear and I don't want cars I don't drive"
   - "Nothing makes a car degrade like not using it"
   - Steward, not hoarder

6. **"Be the best part of someone else's day"**
   - His personal mantra
   - Advice he gave his daughter when starting grad school
   - "Be present, give someone your full attention, not looking at your phone"
