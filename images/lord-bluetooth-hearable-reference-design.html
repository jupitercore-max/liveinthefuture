00Executive Summary

Product Concept

Lord Bluetooth is an all-day OWS (Open Wearable Stereo) AI hearable that combines the comfort and situational awareness of open-ear design with on-device LLM inference capability. Born from a corporate satire / dystopia synth-rock band merch line that's becoming a real hardware product, Lord Bluetooth represents the intersection of entertainment, technology, and commentary on the corporate surveillance state — now packaged as a pair of earbuds you can actually wear.

Vesper's piezoelectric MEMS generates its own signal without bias voltage, enabling true zero-power standby monitoring. For OWS, the speaker challenge is delivering clear audio without blocking ambient sound. Two approaches:

Recommendation: Goertek LBS for Designs B/C/E (cost-optimized). Knowles BA + Goertek dynamic for Design A (premium flagship).
