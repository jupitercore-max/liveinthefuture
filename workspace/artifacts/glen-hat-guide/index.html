📏 How to Measure Your Head

  1. Grab a soft measuring tape (or a string you'll measure against a ruler).
  2. Wrap it around your head just above your ears and about one inch above your eyebrows.
  3. Keep it snug but not tight. You want the fit, not a headache.
  4. Read the measurement in inches. That's your head circumference.
  5. If you're between sizes, always size up. A slightly loose hat beats a too-tight one every time.

Pro tip: Crown depth matters as much as circumference. If hats always sit high on your head, look for 4"+ crown depth.
