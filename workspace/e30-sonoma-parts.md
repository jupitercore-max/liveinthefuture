## 📋 RACE WEEKEND STRATEGY

### Night Before the Race:
1. Check Row52 for BMW 325i/325is/318is and E36 325i/328i at **American Canyon** and **Windsor** Pick-n-Pulls
2. Check Craigslist + FB Marketplace for local sellers
3. Have Specialized German's number saved: **(877) 643-7626** — if you blow up Friday, they can pull an engine and you pick up Saturday morning early

### When the Engine Dies Mid-Race:
1. **Call American Canyon Pick-n-Pull** first — 25 min from the track
2. Check Row52 on your phone while someone starts pulling the dead engine
3. If no BMW at American Canyon, try Windsor (50 min)
4. If going M50/M52 route, you need: engine + wiring harness + ECU + intake manifold. Try to grab from same donor car.
5. **Nuclear option:** Call Specialized German, have them overnight an engine. More expensive but guaranteed to work.

### What to Bring to Pick-n-Pull:
- Socket set (metric — 10, 13, 15, 17, 19mm are the BMW essentials)
- Wrenches (same sizes)
- Engine hoist / cherry picker rental (some yards have them, ASK)
- Drain pan for oil/coolant
- Zip-lock bags for bolts
- Phone with Row52 app
- $200-500 cash
- A friend who owes you a favor
