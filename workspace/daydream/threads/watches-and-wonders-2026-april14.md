1. **Ref. 5810/1G-001** — 41mm white gold, bracelet. LE of 2,000 pieces. **CHF 75,000**
2. **Ref. 5810G-001** — 41mm white gold, strap. LE of 1,000 pieces. **CHF 75,000**

4. **Ref. 958G-001** — 50.7mm white gold POCKET WATCH. LE of 100 pieces. **CHF 205,000**

**Total supply: 5,100 pieces** (2,000 + 1,000 + 2,000 + 100). Retail allocation will be brutal. Secondary market premiums guaranteed.

### Other Notable Patek Releases

- **Cubitus 5840P Skeleton Perpetual Calendar** — 45mm platinum, CHF 150,000. First grand complication in Cubitus line. Regular collection (NOT limited). Signal: Cubitus is a permanent pillar, not an experiment. 
- **6105G Celestial Sunrise/Sunset** — 47mm, 6 patents, CHF 350,000. World-first sunrise/sunset display adjustable for daylight saving time.

### What This Means for Collectors

The Nautilus strategy is clear: **celebrate, limit, elevate.** Only 5,100 wristwatch pieces vs. the secondhand market's hunger. Cubitus gets the grand complication to signal permanence. Patek is saying: "Nautilus is heritage. Cubitus is the future."

For the 5610/1P (platinum, 38mm): this is the most desirable of the three. Smallest production count per mm, most precious metal, most wearable size. If Ray's interested, this is the one. But at CHF 90,000 retail and AD allocation politics, secondary will likely be 1.5-2x.
