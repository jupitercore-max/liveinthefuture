## What's Happening

Watches & Wonders Geneva 2026 starts **April 14** at Palexpo. This is the biggest watch industry event of the year, and this year is unusually loaded:

### Patek Philippe: Nautilus 50th Anniversary
- The Nautilus (Ref. 3700, 1976) turns 50. This is THE story of the fair.
- Steel 5711/1A discontinued in 2021. White gold 5811 succeeded it at ~$69K retail, ~$170K secondary.
- The 40th anniversary (2016) produced a limited platinum 5711. Pattern suggests: precious-metal 50th anniversary piece, probably platinum or white gold 5811 variant with heritage blue dial.
- **Titanium possibility:** Multiple analysts speculating Patek could introduce titanium for the first time. If they do, this triggers Ray's alert (Jeremy's suggestion 2026-04-01). The Cubitus in titanium has been rumored. A titanium Nautilus 50th would be a nuclear event in the watch world.
- Watch monitor crons should be at maximum sensitivity April 14-18.

### Rolex
- No Milgauss 116400GV news yet, but Rolex is notoriously secretive pre-fair.
- Rolex rarely does dramatic redesigns, but the Milgauss has been discontinued since 2023. A successor or revival is overdue. If Rolex brings back a green-crystal anti-magnetic watch, Ray's search gets interesting.
- Yacht-Master II 116689 (white gold) is unlikely to see updates — it's a current production piece.

### Hublot
- Hublot will be at W&W. The Square Bang Unico line is hot. Magic Gold and Titanium Rainbow are Ray's targets.
- Limited editions often drop during the fair. Worth watching for any new Square Bang colorways.
