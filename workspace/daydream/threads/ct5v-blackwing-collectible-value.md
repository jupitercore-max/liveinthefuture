# CT5-V Blackwing: The Last V8 Sedan Is a Future Collectible

**Created:** 2026-04-05 (Iteration 1)
**Status:** Background thread (no action needed, just awareness)
**Urgency:** Low

## The Thesis

CarBuzz and others are now calling the CT5-V Blackwing "The Last True V8 Monster Sedan." GM is going all-electric for Cadillac. The LT4 supercharged 6.2L V8 (668 hp) will not have a successor. This car is the end of a lineage.

The collector car market consistently rewards "last of" vehicles:
- Last air-cooled Porsche 911 (993): $100K new → $250K+ today
- Last Viper ACR: appreciated 30-50% in 5 years
- BMW E46 M3 (last naturally aspirated M3 inline-6): doubled in value

The Blackwing specifically:
- Available with a 6-speed manual (most won't be — the 10-speed auto outsells it heavily)
- 668 hp supercharged V8 in a sports sedan
- Cadillac is now an all-EV brand going forward
- Production volumes are finite and known

## What Ray Should Know
- If his Blackwing is a manual, it's significantly more collectible. Manuals are already commanding premiums.
- Keep meticulous service records. Cadillac dealer-serviced with paperwork = maximum value.
- Don't modify it (or if modifying, keep all OEM parts). Modified collector cars are worth less than stock.
- Low miles matter, but not as much as condition and documentation.
- The Escalade IQL is the opposite — an EV that depreciates like all EVs.
