## THE PIVOT: Fresh Content Wins in AI Overviews

New April 2026 data from Averi.ai changes the entire strategic picture:

### The Bad News (confirmed, getting worse)
- **AI Overviews now appear on 48% of ALL queries** (up from 31% in Feb 2025 — 58% YoY surge)
- Organic CTR drops 34.5-61% when AI Overviews appear
- Reach: **2 billion monthly users**
- **AI Mode** (fully conversational, NO organic results) reached 75M daily users, 1B monthly queries
- AI Mode and AI Overviews cite different sources — only 13.7% citation overlap

### The GOOD News (this is the pivot)
1. **Content under 3 months old is 3x more likely to be cited** (AirOps research)
   - Ray publishes 3-5 articles/day across 8 sites. This is a MASSIVE advantage.
2. **AI Overview citations convert at 14.2% vs 2.8% for traditional organic** (5x quality premium)
   - Fewer clicks but MUCH higher quality visitors
3. **76% of citations come from Top 10 pages** — but 46.5% come from pages outside top 50
   - Even non-ranking pages can get cited if their content is structured right
4. **Only 274,455 domains have appeared in AI Overviews** out of 18.4M indexed
   - Being cited puts you in the top 1.5% of all domains

### What This Means for Ray's 600+ Articles
The previous framing was "you're losing 60% of traffic." The NEW framing is:
- Ray's daily publishing pipeline creates fresh content (3x citation advantage)
- His sites have original data: FARS death rates, IIHS ratings, model evaluations, school analysis
- Original data is harder for AI to synthesize from elsewhere — it MUST cite the source
- If even 10% of Ray's articles get cited in AI Overviews, the conversion quality (14.2% vs 2.8%) may offset the traffic decline

**The strategy isn't "survive AI Overviews" — it's "become the source AI Overviews cite."**

4. **Schema.org markup across all 8 sites.** Help Google's AI understand and cite the content
