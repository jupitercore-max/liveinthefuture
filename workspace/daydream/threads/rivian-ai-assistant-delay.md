## What Happened

Rivian's OTA update 2026.07 is rolling out, but the **Rivian Assistant** — their in-house AI voice interface announced at Autonomy & AI Day (December 11, 2025) — is NOT included. It was promised for "early 2026."

### What 2026.07 Does Include
- Dolby Audio for Apple Music
- Streaming quality auto-adjustment
- Improved WiFi/cellular/app connectivity
- Rear defrost + windshield wiper heater linked
- **Gen 2 R1 fixes:** Lane Change on Command bug, steering wheel vibration reduction, battery range fluctuation fix, charge port door sticking fix

### What's Missing
- Rivian Assistant (AI voice agent that connects to Google Calendar, third-party apps)
- The assistant was built on an "agentic framework" — same pattern as Meta's AI agents in glasses

## Why This Connects to Ray's World

1. **As an owner:** Ray has 2x R1S. The range fluctuation bug fix alone is relevant. The lane change bug fix matters for highway driving.

2. **As a wearables engineer:** Rivian's AI Assistant is the VEHICLE version of what Meta is doing with AI on glasses. Same agentic pattern: device AI → third-party API access → calendar, apps, smart home. Rivian's delay shows how hard this is to ship even with a two-year development effort.

3. **The agentic pattern everywhere:** 
   - Meta glasses → AI agent with multimodal input (camera, mic, bone conduction)
   - Rivian → AI agent with vehicle context (navigation, climate, diagnostics)
   - Hatch/OpenClaw → AI agent with life context (memory, tools, communications)
   - The convergence: these agents will eventually talk to each other. Your glasses agent tells your car agent where you're going.

## No Action Needed
This is a monitoring thread. If the Rivian Assistant finally ships, it'll be worth testing and comparing to Meta's approach. But for now, just context.
