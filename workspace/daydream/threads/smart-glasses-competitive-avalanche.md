## The Landscape Just Changed

Meta has been essentially alone in the smart glasses market. That's over. Seven serious entrants are converging in 2026:

### 1. Meta (incumbent)
- **Ray-Ban Meta Display + Neural Band** — now on Best Buy. In-lens display + EMG wristband (CTRL-Labs, 6 years in development). This is the moat.
- **Blayzer & Scriber** — two new prescription-ready frames at $499, shipping April 14. Prescription = addressable market goes from "tech enthusiasts" to "everyone who wears glasses."
- EssilorLuxottica partnership sold **7 million smart glasses in 2025**, Meta considering doubling production.
- **BUT:** EFF investigation, Swedish newspaper exposé on workers reviewing intimate footage, class action filed March 4 2026 (N.D. Cal.). Privacy is a real vulnerability.

### 2. Apple (nuclear threat)
- **Scrapped Vision Air headset** to focus on lightweight AI smart glasses. Announcement expected 2026, launch 2027.
- Custom silicon based on Apple Watch S-series architecture. This is a specific technical threat — Apple Watch gets all-day battery while powering health sensors, GPS, cellular. If they can do that for glasses + cameras + AI, Meta's battery-life weakness gets exposed.
- "Think more Ray-Ban than Vision Pro" — Apple explicitly going after Meta's form factor.
- 3-5 million units projected for 2027 (Ming-Chi Kuo).

### 3. Google + Warby Parker
- "Intelligent Eyewear" — AI-powered, fashion-forward. Google's software-first approach means the killer apps (Maps, Translate, Assistant) might be better on Google's platform.
- Warby Parker brings fashion credibility and retail distribution (180+ stores).

### 4. Samsung
- First consumer smart glasses confirmed for 2026. Carrier-backed retail (think phone launch model).
- Android XR platform. If Samsung nails distribution via T-Mobile/Verizon/AT&T, it's a very different go-to-market than Meta's direct/EssilorLuxottica approach.

### 5. Nothing (Carl Pei)
- Rumored AI glasses following earbud playbook. Design-forward, tight phone integration.
- Nothing's brand attracts the "I won't wear Meta/Apple" crowd.

### 6. Snap
- AR-first, social features, camera focus. Different positioning but overlapping market.

### 7. Lenovo
- CES concept glasses — monochrome LED, lower cost. Budget entry point.

## What This Means for Ray's Team

The Boz persona project predicts this: Boz's bet architecture is about owning the platform while competitors fight over form factors. The Neural Band + Display combo is Meta's strategic differentiation — nobody else has EMG input. But:

1. **Battery life is the #1 complaint.** Apple's S-series silicon is purpose-built to solve this. If Apple ships with genuinely all-day battery, Meta's current 4-6 hour window looks bad.
2. **Privacy backlash is real and getting louder.** EFF, class action, Swedish investigation. Google and Apple will both position as "more private" alternatives.
3. **Prescription support is the unlock.** Meta's Blayzer/Scriber at $499 is smart — 4 billion people worldwide wear corrective lenses. But Apple and Warby Parker also target prescription wearers.
4. **The content/app ecosystem matters.** Ray's MRBD games run on Meta's platform. If Google's software layer makes better apps possible, developers follow the ecosystem.
