### The Competitive Landscape (as of April 2026)

### Key Thesis
The competitive axis is **bifurcating**:
1. Display glasses (Meta, Xreal, Viture) — expensive, optical expertise needed
2. AI-only glasses (Nothing, Amazon) — cheaper, looks normal, voice-first
3. Neural input (Meta Neural Band) — unique moat, no competitor match

Meta is the only player in camps #1 AND #3.
