### Competitive Landscape Reassessment

The original thread identified 7 competitors. Iteration 2 insight: **the real competitive axis isn't hardware form factor — it's the neural input layer.**

Everyone can make glasses. The question is: who has the best way to CONTROL them hands-free?
- **Meta:** Neural Band (EMG wristband) — 6 years of CTRL-Labs development, TRIBE research
- **Apple:** AirPods already sense head gestures. Could extend to Watch-based neural sensing.
- **Google:** Voice-first (Assistant). Software advantage but no novel input.
- **Samsung:** Android XR defaults to voice + touch. No neural play.
- **China:** Government-backed BCI programs with $100M+ in new capital. Long-term threat.

**Ray's team's moat is the Neural Band.** Everything else (cameras, speakers, displays) is commoditizable. The input problem is the hard part.
