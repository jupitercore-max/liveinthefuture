# Daydream Article Seeds

Seeds must pass the same kill test as regular articles: Is there a novel contribution? Would a knowledgeable reader learn something new?

## Seed 1: AI Overviews Are Killing Independent Publishing — Or Are They? (LITF)
**Pitch:** 600+ articles across 8 sites, all built assuming organic search traffic. Four independent studies now converge on ~60% CTR collapse. BUT: new Averi.ai/BrightEdge data shows content under 3 months old is 3x more likely to be cited, and AI citations convert at 14.2% vs 2.8% (5x premium). Frame this as the pivot from defense to offense: Ray's daily publishing pipeline may be a competitive advantage, not a liability.
**Novel contribution:** First-person data from an AI-assisted publishing operation measuring its own transformation. Four converging studies + the fresh-content citation advantage + actual Cloudflare analytics.
**Kill test risk:** Needs real traffic data to transcend opinion. Cloudflare analytics API is available.
**Status:** STRONGEST — data keeps getting richer. Strategic pivot from "we're doomed" to "fresh content wins" is the novel angle.

## Seed 2: The $499 Glasses That Just Got More Expensive (LITF)
**Pitch:** Trace the full supply chain of a single pair of Ray-Ban Meta glasses from Italian frame to Chinese sensor to retail shelf. Show where tariffs hit. Now with three form factors: Ray-Ban (lifestyle), Blayzer/Scriber (prescription), Oakley Meta (sports). Each has different supply chain exposure. NEW: chip tariff delay to June 2027 means semiconductor components are safe for now, but rare earth restrictions + Section 232 investigation are wildcards.
**Novel contribution:** Supply-chain tariff mapping for a specific consumer product across three form factors, with the chip delay as a nuancing factor.
**Kill test risk:** Supply chain specifics may be hard to source publicly.
**Status:** Strong. Chip tariff delay adds nuance (not all tariffs hit equally).
