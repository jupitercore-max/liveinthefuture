# Daydream Article Seeds

Seeds must pass the same kill test as regular articles: Is there a novel contribution? Would a knowledgeable reader learn something new?

---

## Seed 1: AI Overviews Are Killing Independent Publishing (LITF)
**Pitch:** 500+ articles across 8 sites, all built assuming organic search traffic. Google AI Overviews now appear on 25%+ of searches with 42% click drops documented. Use Ray's own sites as the case study. The self-aware angle (we built an AI content machine, a bigger AI content machine is eating us) is exactly LITF's brand.
**Novel contribution:** First-person data from an AI-assisted publishing operation measuring its own obsolescence. Plus concrete defense strategy (Schema.org, RSS, newsletter, ACO).
**Kill test risk:** Could feel navel-gazing. Needs hard traffic data to transcend opinion.
**Status:** STRONGER — Cloudflare Zone Analytics can provide the data. Kit can pull it proactively.

## Seed 2: The $499 Glasses That Just Got More Expensive (LITF)
**Pitch:** Trace the full supply chain of a single pair of Ray-Ban Meta glasses from Italian frame to Chinese sensor to retail shelf. Show exactly where the new tariff regime (15.8% effective, 250% China) hits. EssilorLuxottica, Qualcomm, camera module suppliers — who absorbs the cost? The optical industry is in alarm. What does this mean for the 6 competitors launching smart glasses in 2026?
**Novel contribution:** Supply-chain tariff mapping for a specific consumer product. Nobody's done this for smart glasses.
**Kill test risk:** Supply chain specifics may be hard to source publicly. Need to verify component origins.
**Status:** Still strong. J.P. Morgan data (15.8% effective rate) adds credibility. April timing (tariffs + April 14 launch) is perfect.

## Seed 3: The Economics of "The Last" (Efficient Design)
**Pitch:** Why end-of-line performance cars always appreciate, with data from Bring a Trailer auction results. 993 Porsche, last Viper ACR, E46 M3, CT5-V Blackwing, and now the ZR1X Zora as the final C8. Plot the appreciation curves.
**Novel contribution:** Data-driven analysis from actual auction results, not vibes.
**Kill test risk:** Need to pull actual BaT data.
**Status:** Background — not urgent, good evergreen piece for ED.

## Seed 4: Building a $75 Emergency Mesh Network (LITF) — NEW
**Pitch:** Meshtastic on ESP32 ($25/node) + GMRS repeater + Home Assistant = a resilient communication and sensor network that works without internet. Perfect for earthquake country, PG&E PSPS events, or just monitoring your property. Show the exact build: 3 nodes, MQTT broker, HA integration, sensor entities for temperature/humidity/motion.
**Novel contribution:** The specific convergence of Meshtastic + GMRS + Home Assistant as a unified system. Most GMRS operators don't know about Meshtastic, most Meshtastic users don't have GMRS licenses.
**Kill test risk:** Low — this is hardware DIY with a clear parts list, exact config, and real use cases.
**Status:** NEW — strong candidate. Could double as a personal project for Ray.
