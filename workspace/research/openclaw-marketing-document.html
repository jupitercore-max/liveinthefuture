What it cannot do (yet)

Anyone who only shows you the highlights is selling you something.

Facebook automation took 6 hours. Most platforms treat agents as second-class citizens. No official APIs for group reading. Sessions expire. IP blocks. React forms that reject programmatic input. The workarounds work, but they are workarounds.
Memory resets every session. Your agent wakes up fresh each time and reconstructs context from files. It is good at this, but it is not the same as continuous consciousness. Complex multi-day projects require good note-taking discipline.
LLMs make mistakes. Your agent will occasionally hallucinate a price, misread a listing, or deploy an article with a bug. The 7-critic pipeline catches most errors but not all. Human review still matters for anything consequential.
Residential IP matters. Some services (Facebook, Google) block datacenter IPs. The workaround is proxying through your home network, but that requires a device on your LAN (Mac Mini, Raspberry Pi, etc.).
Setup takes time. The first week is infrastructure: connecting APIs, teaching your agent your preferences, building the cron jobs that run while you sleep. After that it compounds. Week 1 is work. Week 4 is magic.
