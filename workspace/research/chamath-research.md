### 💰 California Billionaire Tax Exodus (Feb–Apr 2026)
**Claim:** Estimated that people worth over $500 billion "scrambled and left" California after the One-Time Billionaire Tax Act was announced.

**Background:** SEIU-backed ballot initiative for Nov 2026 — one-time 5% tax on net worth over $1B for healthcare/education. Jan 1, 2026 was the eligibility cutoff date.

**Key data points:**
- At least 6 of California's 214 billionaires left before the deadline (Larry Page, Sergey Brin, Travis Kalanick confirmed)
- Those 6 alone = $26.8B in projected tax revenue (>25% of the $100B SEIU estimate)
- Hoover Institution: net effect likely NEGATIVE by ~$25B (permanent income tax loss > one-time revenue)
- CA Legislative Analyst's Office: "temporary increase in revenues" but "ongoing decrease in state income tax revenues of hundreds of millions per year"
- Lake Tahoe (Nevada side) saw a real estate boom from CA billionaires

**Chamath's position:** The tax is counterproductive — it will deepen the budget deficit, not solve it. Classic Laffer Curve argument applied to wealth taxation.

**Significance:** MEDIUM. Shows his positioning on state tax policy. He himself is reportedly still in California but was vocal about the exodus. Aligns with his general pro-business, anti-regulation stance.

### ⚛️ Quantum Computing Threat to Bitcoin (Apr 5, 2026)
**Source:** All-In Podcast discussion

- Quantum computing timeline to break Bitcoin encryption accelerated from 25 years to **7 years at most**
- Bitcoin will be the **first target** ("honeypot") for non-state actors with quantum capability
- Unlike banks/institutions, crypto is an "obvious honeypot" — drain it, announce it's broken, prices go to zero, attacker has all the money
- Bitcoin network has a **5-7 year deadline** to migrate and redesign nodes/wallets for quantum resistance

**Quote:** *"A non-state actor's incentive will first be to drain the obvious honeypots and then tell everybody that it's broken, so that then everything goes to sh*t, all the prices go to zero, and then they have all the money, and then they can buy stuff."*

**Significance:** HIGH for crypto markets. Chamath is one of the most prominent crypto-adjacent VCs and this is a stark warning. He's not saying sell Bitcoin — he's saying the ecosystem must organize to quantum-proof itself.
